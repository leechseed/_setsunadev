How the run goes

1 · RECONzero tokens: the script file (private/BOLO-169/OXO-full-script.txt), 185 pages, one page per form-feed block
2 · ORDERyou say go, and name the slice: the whole script or a page range
3 · EXECUTEall eight read the same pages at once. They never read each other's reports and never launch agents
4 · CONSOLIDATEI merge the eight reports into one list for you. Findings stay in reports and never enter canon until you promote them (BOLO 156)

The seats

Every editor is advisory: it points to a page with a short quote, ranks each problem A (breaks the story), B (weakens it) or C (polish), names what works, and asks you the questions only you can answer. None rewrites the script. Report size: under 900 words for 40 pages or fewer, under 1,500 for the whole script. The last line of each report is DONE, DONE_WITH_CONCERNS or BLOCKED.

Who sits where

Structure, character and theme-tone judge the story. Dialogue and line judge the words. Continuity and production judge the world and its cost. The cold reader is the control: it has no canon, so it tells you what the page alone says. Shared rules live in .claude/agents/references/editor-method.md. Books come from the distilled library; the full list per seat is on the books page (oxo-editor-library.html).
