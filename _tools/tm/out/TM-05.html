OVERVIEW

What this is: texture promoted from a taxonomy, the Ten Questions of the Telling, to a system with a slice, an instrument, and an instance, the fourth top-layer model in the lattice beside 02 (character), 03 (setting), and 04 (plot). It is written from the TEXTURE shelf of the library, which now holds eight distills: the wave's five, Hühn, Schmid & Schönert (Focalization) (Hühn, Schmid & Schönert), Bal (Narratology in Practice) (Bal), Hall (Writing Deep Point of View) (Hall), Kempton (Dialogue) (Kempton), Iglesias (Writing for Emotional Impact) (Iglesias), plus three older sources carrying a TEXTURE key, Card (Characters and Viewpoint) (Card), McKee (Story) (McKee), Kennedy (Against Worldbuilding) (Kennedy). The texture layer doc (1.0.1, a sibling module of this system) remains the taxonomy source this document is written from; it is not superseded, and PART B below restates its Ten Questions rather than replacing them.

What the texture system owns: the discourse side of the telling, who speaks, who sees, in what order, at what speed, how often, in what words, with what feeling, through what thresholds, breaking which frames, and to whom; the TELLING PROFILE as the work's fixed configuration of those answers; the SCENE CARD's telling: line as the per-unit override; and the medium's re-instrumenting of all of it, since a storyform survives adaptation but a Telling Profile does not.

What it does not own: everything story-side. Structure belongs to Dramatica's storyform, events belong to plot_systems' twelve-layer PLOT SLICE, place belongs to the setting system's twelve-layer SETTING SLICE, and who a character is belongs to the LEECHSEED Character System, never to how that character is told. Per Card, viewpoint mechanics, person, tense, levels of penetration, map onto none of the twelve character layers and are declared out of scope for the stack (Card (Characters and Viewpoint)); per Hühn, Schmid & Schönert, the volume's own working assumption is the positive theoretical case for that same exclusion, mediation is a property of the telling and never of the person told about (Hühn, Schmid & Schönert (Focalization)). The exclusion runs both ways: the texture system claims no story-spine content, no event structure, and no character psychology, exactly as those systems claim no telling.

Root claim: every telling is one configuration of a fixed set of answers, and eight independent sources, a reference-shelf volume, a single-author theory book, three craft manuals, and three older texture-keyed distills, converge on the same load-bearing law without having read one another: who speaks and who sees are two decisions, never one. Hühn, Schmid & Schönert spend fourteen essays proving the collapse breaks on a real example every time it is attempted, in prose, drama, film, or games; Bal calls the collapse "nonsensical," as absurd as claiming a third-person-narrated character narrates her own sentence; Card names the same split, cinematic, light, deep, as a dial inside limited third person a working novelist turns scene by scene. The trade's craft vocabulary, deep POV, third limited, show don't tell, register, is a set of compressed settings on that same fixed answer set, and the distance between reader and focal character is the dial the craft books are really turning, whatever name each one gives it: Hall's barrier-word removal, Kempton's viewpoint silence, Iglesias's reader-emotion taxonomy all aim at the same knob from three different trades.

The law: who speaks is not who sees. Hühn, Schmid & Schönert's DNS model splits mediation into three simultaneous dimensions, perception, reflection, and mediation proper, and argues that Genette's single word "focalization" silently covers two different operations, an epistemic position and an information-regulation decision (Hühn, Schmid & Schönert (Focalization)). Bal names the same split as EF versus CF, external focalizer or character-focalizer, each independently markable perceptible or non-perceptible, and argues the narrator never touches the focalized object directly, only through the focalizor (Bal (Narratology in Practice)). Both sources argue the current single focalization_mode value on T2 is really two bundled decisions, a perceptual one and an informational one, which PART A below carries as separate fields.

The dial: distance, cinematic, light, or deep. Card names the three settings inside limited third person in a single page, cinematic (camera only, no thoughts), light (the narrator dips in and out), and deep (no thought tags needed, the character's judgment colors every clause) (Card (Characters and Viewpoint)). Hall spends an entire book operationalizing the deep end of that same dial into a checkable technique inventory, barrier words removed, body language gated by awareness, emotion rendered as physical sensation instead of named (Hall (Writing Deep Point of View)). Two craft sources, twenty-six years apart, converge on the identical three-point scale without citing each other; T6's distance policy field carries that scale as house vocabulary from this version forward.

PART A · THE TEXTURE SLICE, twelve layers, mirror of the character, setting and plot stacks

Same architecture as the LEECHSEED Character System, the SETTING SLICE, and the PLOT SLICE: surface to depth to structural function, T12 fed independently from the storyform exactly as L12, S12, and P12 are. Layer names are ruled (OPEN 2, 9/16), same status as the P-layer and S-layer names.

Sub-values and checks on the slice (ruled 9/16, built 2026-10-04):

  • T2 split. perceptual_scope records whose view the reader gets. informational_denial records what the reader is denied (Hühn, Schmid & Schönert (Focalization), Bal (Narratology in Practice)).
  • T2 sub-values. focalization_mode allows silence and memory as values. Silence is a focalized character's deliberate non-report of her own thought (Kempton (Dialogue)). Memory is retrospective, unreliable, sense-based, and itself narrativized (Bal (Narratology in Practice)).
  • T1/T2 gap flag. t1_t2_gap: none | flagged (note) records the gap between a T1 reading and a T2 reading of one passage. It is Bal's manipulation check as one portable field (Bal (Narratology in Practice)).
  • T4 pause test. Where a scene marks the Pause speed, check three things: delimitation, motivation, and rhetorical binding (Bal (Narratology in Practice)).
  • T6 distance instrument. Count barrier words per 100 words of a passage (Hall (Writing Deep Point of View)). Bands are cinematic, light, and deep. The first trial passage calibrates the cut points.
  • T6 terms applied by 07.1 (IF-01-07-09; IF catalog call 7, ruled 2026-10-05). The writer applies T6's register, distance_policy, fid, barrier words per 100 words and Kempton's seven genre registers through 07.1 prose style. Those values are the terms 07.1 uses.
  • T7 reflection axis. T7 absorbs the narrator's or character's cognitive, emotive, and normative mark-up of what is perceived (Hühn, Schmid & Schönert (Focalization)).
  • T7 mood-curve entry. Each entry is one three-field line: emotion · technique · payoff owed (Iglesias (Writing for Emotional Impact)).
  • T7 payoff ledger. The ledger is a running list of owed payoffs. Each entry closes when the story pays it. It sits outside the twelve layers, as a reader model. The reader's trust in a character is tracked in §9A The Reader Model, which keys entries by character_id and shifted_at (the time code). The ledger uses the same time code to mark where a payoff is owed and where it is paid.
  • Dialogue function count. Kempton's seven-function test audits a line by removal (Kempton (Dialogue)). It is a named revision check, not a schema field (OPEN 7).

Binding rule (mirror of the character, setting, and plot bindings): T12 is fed independently from the storyform, exactly as L12 FUNCTION, S12 FUNCTION, and P12 FUNCTION are, keyed by storyform_id. A telling with T12 empty is a style, an author's habitual voice running with no argument to serve; a telling with T12 filled is an argument's instrument, the way this scene's voice and distance choices are doing work for a specific throughline's signpost. The count is twelve, ruled 9/16 (OPEN 1): T11 and T12 are house additions beyond the layer doc's ten questions.

THE INSTRUMENT · the TELLING PROFILE and the telling line

No new card. The TELLING PROFILE, the layer doc's own instrument, recorded per ladder address, usually R5, is the slice's instance format; nothing above requires a second notation. The SCENE CARD's telling: line remains the per-unit override, unchanged in position, extended in content.

TELLING PROFILE
scope:         OXO.primary                          (ladder address)
T1 voice:      hetero/homo · level · reliability · narration time
T2 sees:       focalization mode · focal character(s) · perceptual scope (whose view) · informational denial (what the reader is denied)
T3 order:      baseline + licensed anachronies
T4 speed:      default gait + signature moves
T5 frequency:  signature uses (iterative? repetitive?)
T6 words:      register · distance policy (cinematic/light/deep) · FID yes/no · barrier words per 100
T7 feeling:    tone · target mood curve (emotion · technique · payoff owed) · reflection mark-up
T8 thresholds: paratext apparatus (titles, epigraphs, in-world docs)
T9 frames:     metalepsis policy (permitted? never?)
T10 aimed at:  narratee construction
T11 medium:    channel + grammar overrides
T12 function:  story point discharged · storyform_id
gap:           t1_t2_gap: none | flagged (note)

The telling: line's proposed content stays lean by design, T1 plus T2 plus T4 plus a register slot at T6 plus the t1_t2_gap flag, per scene, rather than all twelve fields restated every time:

telling:  voice/level + focal character (T1+T2) · speed (T4) · register (T6) · t1_t2_gap

Kempton's seven genre registers are the readiest source for that register slot, a ready-made, non-academic vocabulary that could seed the TRANSLATION GRID's craft-term column and let a scene card flag a mismatch, a literary scene running a breathless register, the way duration: already flags pace (Kempton (Dialogue)). Hall's deep-PoV checklist is the readiest source for what T1 plus T2 plus T6 look like at their strictest setting: heterodiegetic or homodiegetic voice, internal fixed focalization, one mind per scene, and distance dialed to deep (Hall (Writing Deep Point of View)).
