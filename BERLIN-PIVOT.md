America runs a sex economy like a war economy — Rosie the Relay, "all our dads are dead," everybody lining up for the line. ## Route A — the German academic spine: Hochschule Merseburg

**M.A. Angewandte Sexualwissenschaft** — the only applied-sexology master's in Germany, ~40 min from Leipzig, ~2h from Berlin by train.

| Gate | Requirement | Status |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Bachelor's degree** — social work, pedagogy, psychology, medicine, health sciences; *or* a comparable social/humanities degree (180 ECTS) + documented social-pedagogical competencies | ⚠️ the Gate-0 question |
| 2 | **German at C1** — TestDaF 4×4 / DSH-2 / Telc C1 Hochschule / Goethe C2 | ~1.5–2 yrs serious study from zero |
| 3 | **Selection procedure** — locally admission-restricted (NC + Auswahlverfahren) | competitive, not lottery-hard |
| 4 | **Money** — blocked account **€11,904/yr** (€992/mo withdrawal), semester fees nominal; program-specific fees to verify (it's part-time/berufsbegleitend format, 6 semesters) | ≈ one good content quarter |

**The honest read:** the gate is not money and not admissions mystique. It's **German C1** and the **bachelor's question**. Everything else is paperwork.

**Sequencing that works:** Berlin **language-course visa** year one (legal residence while grinding German) → C1 cert → apply Merseburg → student visa. The ethnography starts the day you land, not the day you matriculate.

## Sexual fields — the reading list
