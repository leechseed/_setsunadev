## The cohort is real — verified numbers

- **1 in 12 U.S. children will lose a parent or sibling before 18** — Childhood Bereavement Estimation Model (JAG Institute / Judi's House, w/ New York Life Foundation), 2023 national report. Bereavement from opioid overdose specifically is soaring.

## Research leads — is there a field for this?

There is. Several, and the German angle is real:

- **Sexualwissenschaft is a German invention.** Magnus Hirschfeld's Institut für Sexualwissenschaft (Berlin, 1919 — burned by the Nazis in '33). The lineage continues:
  - **Hochschule Merseburg** — M.A. Angewandte Sexualwissenschaft (applied sexology). The current German academic home for this.
  - **Charité Berlin** — Institut für Sexualwissenschaft und Sexualmedizin.
  - German public universities: no tuition for internationals (nominal semester fee; Baden-Württemberg excepted). The Berlin scene as fieldwork site is not a joke — it's literally ethnography.
- **"Somatic sexuality out west"** = **sexological bodywork** — certified as a profession in California (~2003, Joseph Kramer lineage); trainings exist in Europe.
- **The academic fields to sink teeth into:**
  - **Sexual fields theory** (sociology) — Adam Isaiah Green, *Sexual Fields: Toward a Sociology of Collective Sexual Life*. Literally the study of erotic scenes as markets/fields. Closest single match to "what we're doing."
  - **Anthropology of sexuality** — Gayle Rubin, "Thinking Sex"; Margot Weiss, *Techniques of Pleasure* (ethnography of the SF BDSM scene — a model for studying a scene from inside it).
  - **Porn Studies** — an actual peer-reviewed Routledge journal since 2014.
  - **Kinsey Institute** (Indiana) — the U.S. institutional anchor.
  - Sexuality studies degree programs: San Francisco State; Widener University (Center for Human Sexuality Studies).
