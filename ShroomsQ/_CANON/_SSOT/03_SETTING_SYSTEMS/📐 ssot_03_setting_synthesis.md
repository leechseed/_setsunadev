# 📐 SSOT: THE SETTING SYSTEM — synthesis of 42 distills

**What this is:** a reading of 42 new setting-shelf distills against the existing taxonomy in [ssot_03_setting_system.md](📐%20ssot_03_setting_system.md) v1.1.0 — the four axes, the twelve-layer SETTING SLICE, the SCENE CARD, and the DCUS instance. Nothing here is ruled. Each distill carries a `feeds:` block mapping itself to S-layers at a strength (primary / supporting / contextual); this synthesis sorts that load by layer, then reads across it for what recurs.

---

## PART ONE · PER-LAYER REGISTER

Depth call: what ssot_03 v1.1.0 already carried (mostly BVX.0458/0349/1122), against what this wave adds. In aggregate, ten of the twelve layers move from adequate to deep — this wave is the single largest infusion the SETTING SLICE has had since the B→A→instance delivery. Only two layers move by kind rather than degree: S3 clears its flagged-thin status outright, and S12 climbs off "DCUS-only" for the first time. S2 is the one layer where the honest read is mixed — the method gap closes, the instance gap does not, and those are two different kinds of "thin" that the original flag didn't distinguish.

**S1 BODY — adequate → deep.** Roberts' order still stands: nations before coastline [[BVX.0458]]. New: draw the map *during* drafting to lock distances before prose can contradict them [[BVX.0245]]; a depth-keyed terrain taxonomy for a non-land medium (light zones, buoyancy) [[BVX.1169]]; cosmology scaled to S1's own logic, a four-ring plane structure [[BVX.1174]]; geography run on stated canon-fiat rather than climate, a border mechanic that lets incompatible terrain sit side by side [[BVX.1181]].

**S2 WEATHER — thin, flagged → method adequate, instance still thin.** New: disaster and season read through architecture and behavior, not plot [[BVX.0141]]; an explicit rain-shadow/axial-tilt worksheet [[BVX.1186]]; weather as villain-law, the sky itself cursed [[BVX.1173]]. One source confirms the gap isn't closed by proximity alone — a worldbuilding-for-games text that only glances at climate does nothing for it [[BVX.1172]]. **Plain answer:** the method gap is closed; DCUS's own S2 record is still unwritten.

**S3 SENSORIUM — thin, flagged → deep, no longer thin.** New: the shelf's strongest S3 source — measured physical facts (street widths, lantern spacing) usable with the game system stripped out [[BVX.0479]]; sensory detail keyed to one character's body and history, never a checklist [[BVX.0056]]; eight separate presentation-surface tables rolled after the physical shell exists [[BVX.1176]]; felt imagery matched to what a setting's own instruments report [[BVX.0831]]. **Plain answer:** fixed.

**S4 LAW — adequate → deep.** New: law encoded as a scoring rule — honor levels and decaying dishonor points run at the table [[BVX.1170]]; a graded permeability field run identically across fifty nations [[BVX.1181]]; a policing layer purpose-built for a new medium, absent from the previous edition of the same setting [[BVX.1185]]; a nine-article constitution as the direct legal output of one revolution [[BVX.1180]].

**S5 SCAR — adequate → deep.** New: a forward scar ledger — future dissolution dates fixed before the wound lands [[BVX.0362]]; a present-tense damage-status field, rolled not narrated [[BVX.1176]]; precursor falls stacked five deep, a load-bearing recursion [[BVX.1184]]; a foreknown, structurally inevitable collective doom seeded at the dawn of time [[BVX.1189]]; one rupture that fuses the wound (S5) and the promise (S9) into a single causal chain [[BVX.0575]].

**S6 ECONOMY — adequate → deep.** New: an entire mechanisms chapter as a parts-list for any resource system [[BVX.0387]]; an eight-tier lifestyle-expense ladder, a complete economic skeleton free of setting flavor [[BVX.1174]]; one tech premise (coal and steam) that reshapes religion, class, and war at once [[BVX.1180]].

**S7 FOUNDING — adequate → deep, the most-reinforced layer in the wave.** New: a creation myth read as the birth-myth pattern run at civilizational scale [[BVX.0822]]; terrain reliability determining what kind of founding myth and ruler a culture gets — predictable land breeds identity-with-the-divine, contested land breeds fear-and-service [[BVX.0835]]; the present-tense-bite rule made grammatical — a car racing now versus a car that had already raced [[BVX.1190]]. Twelve further sources independently converge on the same rule (see Cross-Book Methods, item 1).

**S8 HABIT — adequate → deep.** New: three independent sliders (tech, divine involvement, magic prevalence) replacing a fixed culture catalog [[BVX.0343]]; rites of passage tied to a real threshold, invented ceremony needs a life-stage function behind it [[BVX.0823]] [[BVX.0841]]; belonging taught by one culture's stated, unedited bias toward its neighbors [[BVX.1175]]; species and culture decoupled into independent chargen axes instead of one bundled package [[BVX.1168]].

**S9 ALLURE — adequate → deep.** New: the "Ah!" of recognized divinity — what makes a cosmology want-able, not a lore-dump [[BVX.0831]]; a private vision becomes public myth only when it lands, uncoerced, on other people [[BVX.0836]]; gating real character options behind cult membership as what makes joining a real promise [[BVX.1187]]; a deity's whole footprint compressed to alignment plus domain plus symbol [[BVX.0483]].

**S10 UNDERSIDE — adequate → deep.** New: shadowrunners as "deniable assets" — the underside as the structural mechanism that lets the visible power layer fight without touching its own hands [[BVX.0508]]; a room read as externalized, repressed institutional psychology [[BVX.1172]]; fairy-tale forensics — a sanctioned collected text scoured over older, buried content [[BVX.0258]]; a public/folk/secret three-tier model, sharper than a binary public/personal split [[BVX.1184]].

**S11 VECTOR — adequate → deep.** New: an era snapshot narrated twice, its own golden-age myth then the truth under it [[BVX.0362]]; a place that keeps moving offstage, confirmed only later — a place stops being scenery exactly when it can move without the camera [[BVX.0538]]; consistency trading directly against completeness, a retcon as a real cost against trust [[BVX.0630]].

**S12 FUNCTION — thin (DCUS-only) → adequate.** New: the standout of the wave — S12 filled, not empty, in a non-DCUS instance: a villain's grief *is* the land's physics, the wound encoded directly into setting law [[BVX.1173]]. Also: a reusable pipeline — write the background players never see, run the scene forward, then read the plot backward from the climax to catch causality holes [[BVX.1185]]; a whole setting's logic collapsing to one held-constant premise, arrived at without Dramatica vocabulary [[BVX.0394]]. **Plain answer:** off "thin" — it now has a worked instance proving the schema transfers beyond DCUS, but stays the layer with the fewest direct methods; most distills explicitly say "none" here, since S12 binds to the storyform, which no trade source supplies.

## PART TWO · CROSS-BOOK METHODS

1. **Present-tense history — found the past only where it bites now.** Stated as a rule [[BVX.0458]], demonstrated as an in-universe zine article [[BVX.0508]], gated behind discovery [[BVX.0538]], mechanized as a verb-tense test [[BVX.1190]], operationalized as a mandatory die roll [[BVX.1176]], and independently reached by a wargame, a pulp setting, and a video-game text [[BVX.1169]] [[BVX.1170]] [[BVX.1172]] [[BVX.1173]] [[BVX.1181]] [[BVX.1187]].
2. **Name the wound a faction or institution fills.** Dune's Butlerian Jihad [[BVX.0575]]; a Great Flood calendar [[BVX.1169]]; Pure Steam's Abolition War [[BVX.1180]]; a monomyth's founding sacrifice [[BVX.0822]] [[BVX.1188]]; a house-politics cascade where a wound at any level ripples upward through every level above it [[BVX.1182]].
3. **Convert tone into a rule, not a mood.** Dread survives play only when it costs a resource, not when it's described better [[BVX.1184]]; honor as a scoring rule [[BVX.1170]]; danger as a visible climbing meter [[BVX.1185]]; every game mechanism read as a worldbuilding answer to a design question [[BVX.0387]].
4. **A repeatable faction / nation / city card.** A formal NATION stat block [[BVX.0343]]; an eight-field per-faction card portable across universes [[BVX.1170]]; a graded border field run identically across fifty nations [[BVX.1181]]; a two-line government-plus-economy conceit re-rendered at shrinking scale for a city [[BVX.1171]]; a founding-sentence-plus-binding-principle-plus-one-secret stub for a place a party may never visit [[BVX.1177]].
5. **One premise, checked against every layer.** Pure Steam's "science any commoner can wield" reshapes religion, politics, class, and war from one sentence [[BVX.1180]]; the same move at design-instinct scale [[BVX.0394]]; a culture read as the direct output of how its people eats [[BVX.1188]] or what its environment permits [[BVX.0630]].
6. **Public/secret layering, generalized past a binary.** A three-tier public/folk/secret model [[BVX.1184]]; a dyadic, "unseen" city sharing one territory under two rule-sets [[BVX.0630]]; a hidden layer redrawn as literal geography, not mere secrecy [[BVX.1185]].
7. **Build order / top-down cascade.** Nations before coastline [[BVX.0458]]; star, then planet, then geography, then culture [[BVX.1186]]; a one-sentence premise compressed before any scene-list [[BVX.1183]]; a terrain hierarchy kept separate from post-placement physics [[BVX.0343]] [[BVX.0479]].
8. **A forward-dated ledger of what hasn't happened yet.** Dissolution dates fixed before the fall [[BVX.0362]]; a present-tense damage-status field instead of a narrated timeline [[BVX.1176]]; irreversible legacy-game state carried forward [[BVX.0387]]; five ages foretelling the ending on page one [[BVX.1184]].
9. **Border/seam as the mechanism that lets incompatible things coexist.** A rated border between two otherwise incompatible zones [[BVX.1181]]; build the frontier, leave the interior blank [[BVX.1177]]; keep the center dense and the edges deliberately vague so a setting travels [[BVX.1179]]; a crosshatched world running two rule-sets in one territory [[BVX.0630]].

## PART FOUR · DCUS APPLICATION

1. **Write S2 WEATHER now**, using BVX.0141's method: heat, humidity, hurricane season shown through what the campus built (covered walkways, retrofitted storm shutters) and how people move, not a climate paragraph.
2. **Rewrite S5's rename lattice as one throwaway line**, per BVX.0141's palimpsest technique — a bricked-over sign, a mascot mural painted over twice — show the erasure in the fabric, not the narration.
3. **Apply the forward-ledger move to the six-movement descent**: give the slice a Current-Status field with M2–M4 already dated and named, the same discipline BattleTech uses to fix a fall before it happens.
4. **Build a FACTION CARD for the Administration, the Bishops, and the Sync Cult**, using the Part Three item-1 template, each with a border/seam rating for how porous it is to legacy-vs-Sync students.
5. **Run existing DCUS history lines through BVX.1190's grammar test** — anything phrased so the outcome is already known should be rewritten live wherever it appears in a scene.
6. **Name S9's meritocratic promise as a specific stage-promise**, not "prestige" in the abstract — the exact thing the Feed and Star-Rating promise — so Tori's Growth: Stop has a concrete thing to stop believing in.
7. **Split S10 into public/folk/secret**, per the three-tier model: the folk layer (what legacy students quietly still do) separated from the secret layer (what the Bishop acquisition actually did, recoverable in Movement 3).
8. **Give the campus a rated border to the Middle Bands**, since Movement 3's forensic mode needs a stated seam between the old fabric and the Feed-linked retrofit.

## PART FIVE · CONTRADICTIONS

- **Top-down vs. location-first.** BVX.1186 and BVX.0458 argue strict top-down; BVX.0394 argues the opposite — build outward from one controlled location. **Leans toward BVX.0458's own resolution**: pick a direction and commit. DCUS is already location-first (an existing node fleshed out after the fact); no change needed there.
- **Completeness as virtue vs. risk.** New sources hand over engines that could run to full S1–S11 coverage [[BVX.0343]] [[BVX.1170]] [[BVX.1181]] [[BVX.1186]], but BVX.0630 shows completeness trades against consistency, echoing Kennedy's existing warning. **Leans toward keeping thin-by-default exactly as ruled.**
- **Diegetic/gated history vs. printed timelines.** BVX.0538 and BVX.1175 gate history behind discovery; BVX.0362, BVX.1170, BVX.1181, BVX.1184 print bullet timelines outright. **Not actually opposed** — every printed timeline restricts itself to entries that still bite, the same rule delivered as a reference list instead of a scene gate. The real fork is BVX.0630's portal-vs-immersive choice. **Leans immersive for DCUS**, with one portal-style beat per movement as an entry point.
- **Which genre-contract tool is primary.** Baur's five-lineage taxonomy (BVX.0458) vs. Ryan's nine-axis scored checklist (BVX.0630), which states in its own frontmatter that it supersedes the five-lineage tool. **Leans toward Ryan** — a sharpening claim, not a real disagreement.
- **How much a faction card should hold.** The nine sources behind Part Three item 1 don't agree on card depth: BVX.0343's NATION block runs eight named fields with sub-tables; BVX.1177 and BVX.1179 argue explicitly for a founding-sentence-plus-one-secret stub and nothing more, reserving depth for whatever the story actually visits. **Leans toward the stub** for any place DCUS's story doesn't center — the full eight-field block is warranted only for the Administration, the Bishops, and the Sync Cult themselves, per DCUS Application item 4, not for every named neighbor or rival institution mentioned in passing.
