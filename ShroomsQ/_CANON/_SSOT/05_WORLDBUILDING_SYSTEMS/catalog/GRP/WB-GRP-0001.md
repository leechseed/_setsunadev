# The Bishops

The Bishops bought Red Hills Academy when the son sold his father's century of prestige "for spoils", then rebranded it DCUS.
Canon: they run Ultra Schools the way a chain runs franchises, with DCUS as flagship. That chain reading is provisional tissue, not ruled.
Who keeps it: Quinn Bishop runs "Administrative Fixes" for the Administration. No fuller hierarchy is canon.
The Bishops and the Administration are two factions: the owners buy the school, the system runs it. ⧗ Scale "locality" because canon shows one school. ⧗ Era ends M4 (canon casts DCUS for Movements 2-4).
Presses on the story: the ownership war and the rename lattice (Skeeter Creek to Red Hills to DCUS) are their seam.
