# The University (mandatory, the whole Coast)

**Spoken 2026-10-17 (the lore drop):** the Ultra School exists inside a super university, "like the IB magnet program, but not without the implications of magnet, but just like an elite program within a much larger university". Everyone in the Delta Coast must attend by 15 and leaves at 21. The Ultra School takes about "100 per class every year ... or 52 because of the cards".
**Filed 2026-10-17** (Chief: "go" on all four recs). Every point below is provisional ⧗.

- **The name (rec taken):** the big school is just **the University**. Everyone goes, so nobody needs a better word for it ⧗.
- **The span (rec taken):** attendance runs from 15 to 21, seven years. The 52 are picked when they enter at 15 and spend all seven years in the Ultra School ⧗. Open option: a second draw at 17 or 18, a shorter Ultra run and a drama beat.
- **The class (rec taken):** each year takes **52 Stock and 2 Jokers**, 54 in all. Each Stock Ultra is a card: the suit is a house or discipline, the rank is standing in the class. Seven classes of 52 is 364, one for every day of the year but one ⧗.
- **The Jokers:** the Randoms of the intake (WB-CUS-0002) are the Jokers, cards outside the suits, needed for "randomosity" and never quite one of the deck ⧗. Open: how many Outliers are Jokers, and from which classes.
- **The scale:** Georgia, Louisiana, Alabama and Florida hold about 43 million people today, about 4 million of them aged 15 to 21. The University is a city, or a system of campuses across the Coast. Florida State, at 40,000, is the yardstick.
- **Ties:** the Ultra School is WB-INS-0002; its campus is 03 `dcus`; the agoge reading comes from WB-CUS-0002.
