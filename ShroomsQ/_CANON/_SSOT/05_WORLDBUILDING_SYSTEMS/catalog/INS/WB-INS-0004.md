# The Power Line (the standards body)

**Name RULED 2026-09-29** (Chief: "go recommendations"): the Power Line. The irony is the point: it sounds like a utility line, and it is the line that protects the Delta Coast's power. The Outliers call it **the Zona**; Coast church folk pray for **the Hedge**.
**What it is (spoken 9/29):** the Delta Coast built it from its own trash and the junk from space, as a shield against the outside: a Great Wall against the Kudzu, and the barrier between Providence and the Real Cartel. Toxic, raised land, a mountain range. The Outliers live in it and come from it; Kowloon Walled City blocks run along it like The Line.
**This entry is the body that keeps it:** who sets the standard for crossing. The place itself stays a 03 setting slice ⧗ (the seed's S1–S12); no 03 header exists for it yet.
**The heat (RULED 2026-10-01, BOLO 96, "all rec"):** the wreck of the Moon's Severance has been falling for a century: orbital reactors, plutonium power cells, warhead cores, Moon-built hulls. Wherever it lands, **autonomous dozers push it onto the belt**, and the North, the South and every other power have agreed that the hot heap is the line between them: a treaty border made of fallout (Chief 10/1). The Coast steers reentries onto it, so the belt is the impact range: that is why it is built of space junk, why it is hot, and why its hot spots are craters. **Grades** (sieverts per hour): G0 background · G1 the streets (deadly over years to outsiders, nothing to the belt-born) · G2 work zones (~0.1, shifts in hours) · G3 crater rims (~1, an outsider sick within the hour) · G4 **the heat**, crater cores (10+, minutes to a lethal dose; [divers](../CUS/WB-CUS-0003.md) only). **The belt-born resist** by inheritance: the first Moon colonists were engineered for lunar radiation (DNA-shielding and repair genes, the tardigrade's Dsup), and the Outliers carry it without knowing why. Divers and dozer crews call the belt **the Hotline**; the Power Line stays the official name.
**The Coast's strength is isolationism and building the best**, so the Power Line also feeds the school: see [the intake](../CUS/WB-CUS-0002.md).
