# The dive rig (the line and the crawler)

**RULED 2026-10-01** (BOLO 96, Chief: "all rec"), from the spoken seed.
**What it is:** an umbilical, Evangelion-style, seated along the diver's spine, across the exposed back where her strength is, and in both calves. It runs up to a quarter mile back to an autonomous **crawler** that carries her [Mother](../BIO/WB-BIO-0002.md).
**How it keeps her alive:** radiation kills dividing cells first, marrow and then gut lining. The diver is not shielded; she is **resupplied**. The **spine ports** seat in the vertebrae, which hold much of the adult marrow, and stream broth straight in: a marrow transplant running live. The **calf ports** are the pump: the calf muscles (the body's "second heart") squeeze the broth up the veins, so she has to keep moving or the flow stalls.
**Why the crawler hangs back:** distance is shielding (the inverse square law), and the Mother would die in the heat. It parks at the G2/G3 rim; only the line goes in.
**The calves** carry an in-world erotic genre of their own (Chief 10/1) ⧗ to be named.
Worn by the [hot-zone divers](../CUS/WB-CUS-0003.md); Tori is one.
