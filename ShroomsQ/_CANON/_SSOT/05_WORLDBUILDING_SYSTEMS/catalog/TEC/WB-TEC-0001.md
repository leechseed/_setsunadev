# The Feed (DCUS)

The Feed is the signal DCUS runs on. Canon names it in S3 (its glow), S4 (enforced legibility) and S9 (its glamour).
Who keeps it: the Administration (WB-INS-0001). ⧗ Canon does not say what hardware or protocol it is.
In the tracking file, Victoria's Sync feed shows artifacts (sync_feed.artifact_visible, M2).
Not to be confused with the [TIK-TOK] in-world feed of the Tremor Cult seed, which belongs to another story.
