# The Mother (the broth)

**RULED 2026-10-01** (BOLO 96, Chief: "all rec"). Named for a kombucha culture's floating mat, the *mother*.
**The film:** radiotrophic, like the melanized fungi on Chernobyl's reactor walls that grow faster under gamma radiation. It feeds on the dose that rides back down the line in the diver's blood, and it powers the broth.
**The broth:** the diver's own pluripotent cells, so it is **locked to her genome** and works on her alone (Chief 10/1: "it's her DNA in the thing").
**Quickening:** left unspent long enough, the broth organizes into a parthenote, a fetus grown from her cells alone. The broth *quickens*.
**The cost (Chief 10/1):** a hard dose breaks the quickened tissue down for parts. "That little fetus thing she's killing all the time is like her kid, that she's sacrificing every time she walks into the heat."
Carried by the [dive rig](../TEC/WB-TEC-0002.md)'s crawler.
