# The hot-zone divers

**RULED 2026-10-01** (BOLO 96, Chief: "all rec").
**What they do:** walk into the hottest craters of the [Power Line](../INS/WB-INS-0004.md) on the [dive rig](../TEC/WB-TEC-0002.md) and bring out Moon material: the wreck of the Severance, still falling a century on. Helium-3-rich regolith ballast (fusion fuel), vacuum-forged alloys, Moon-built parts Earth can no longer make. Earth's grid runs on it; the craters are the mine.
**The price:** each hard dive spends the quickened tissue in the diver's [Mother](../BIO/WB-BIO-0002.md).
**Slang:** divers and dozer crews call the belt **the Hotline**.
**Tori is one** (RULED 10/1): belt-born, Moon blood she doesn't know about, Red Hills legacy at school. All three hold.
