# The intake (belt-born into DCUS)

**Spoken 2026-09-29:** the Delta Coast's strength is isolationism and building the best; like the Spartans in *300* it is "almost too good", so it pulls from the people of the belt, because "there has to be randomosity".
**Filed 2026-09-29** (Chief: "go recommendations"). The model is Sparta's **mothakes**: sons of helots and poor families raised in the agoge beside Spartan boys, fresh blood the elite needed and never fully accepted. The Ultra School is the agoge; the recruits are needed for their difference and never quite one of them ⧗.
**Ties:** "Stock", the pure-bloodline idea in the 6/20/2025 dump: the Coast breeds Stock, and Stock grows uniform ⧗. The fabula event `m2_recruited` ("outside the Academy → recruited to the Academy") is Tori's own recruitment.
**Tori (RULED 2026-09-29, "Legacy"):** she stays Red Hills legacy. The belt recruits are the people she meets at DCUS.
**The word (RULED 2026-10-04, provisional; Chief: "we can go with randoms, but that's just temporary for now"):** the recruits are **Randoms**, from Chief's "randomosity". The Stock use it as a slur; the recruits wear it. A better word replaces it later ⧗.
**The gauge (RULED 2026-10-04, Chief: "yes, memory"):** **Memory**, what the recruits carry from the belt that the Stock never had.
**Who the Outliers are (RULED 2026-10-04):** most are the descendants of the thousands sent to the Moon a century before; the rest are outsiders and outcasts from all the other Earth realms, who go to the belt because it is the only place they can go.
