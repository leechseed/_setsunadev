# Palantir (the breakaway nation)

The **techno-corpo state** (RULED 9/29, "all rec"). Chief's 2026 notes: "palantir as the breakaway nation and america as bioshock infinite", and the three powers "techno corpo state · narco state · the kudzu".
It breaks away from [the America it leaves behind](WB-GRP-0007.md) ⧗. If any power in the North is "ruled by an AI", it is this one ⧗ (Chief, 9/29: "being ruled by an AI, I think").
**When:** a power of era 2 (DCUS) ⧗; the moment of the breakaway has no event yet.
**Not the same as** the AI rulers elsewhere in the notes: China's Grand Committee of the People's Spirits (the East), the W&W AI-led trade union, the MODS of ASTRO7EX, the DCUS Administration's "depersonalized OS".
