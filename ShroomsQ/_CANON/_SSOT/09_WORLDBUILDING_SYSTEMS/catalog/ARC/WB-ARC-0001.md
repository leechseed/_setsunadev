# Southern Gothic prestige brick (DCUS campus)

The campus is a century-old Southern prestige build: old brick and moss carrying a retrofit (03 S1 BODY and S3 SENSORIUM). Register ruled Southern Gothic.
Aesthetic line, kept 9/29: kudzu over century brick, a product word bolted onto prestige stone.
Interiors are Feed-conditioned and cooled; whoever does not rate a cooled building stays in the Gulf heat (03 S2, ruled 1.4.1).
Who keeps it: the school (WB-INS-0002). ⧗ Origin set to the Skeeter Creek founding because the brick is "century" old; canon does not date the buildings themselves.
One building's fabric stays in 03 S1; this entry is the style only.
