# The Power Line (the standards body)

**Name RULED 2026-09-29** (Chief: "go recommendations"): the Power Line. The irony is the point: it sounds like a utility line, and it is the line that protects the Delta Coast's power. The Outliers call it **the Zona**; Coast church folk pray for **the Hedge**.
**What it is (spoken 9/29):** the Delta Coast built it from its own trash and the junk from space, as a shield against the outside: a Great Wall against the Kudzu, and the barrier between Providence and the Real Cartel. Toxic, raised land, a mountain range. The Outliers live in it and come from it; Kowloon Walled City blocks run along it like The Line.
**This entry is the body that keeps it:** who sets the standard for crossing. The place itself stays a 03 setting slice ⧗ (the seed's S1–S12); no 03 header exists for it yet.
**The Coast's strength is isolationism and building the best**, so the Power Line also feeds the school: see [the intake](../CUS/WB-CUS-0002.md).
