# The Administration

The Administration is the operating layer of DCUS. It issues the rating that decides who stays legible.
Canon: it is a "depersonalized OS" (03 S4 LAW). Its binding principle is that legitimacy is issued, not earned (the Sacrament of Verification).
Who keeps it: the Bishops own the charter; the Administration runs the school without them. The holder is unnamed.
Origin: it has no canon founding. The rebrand is where it takes the school, not where it begins. ⧗ Era ends M4 because canon casts DCUS for Movements 2-4; M5-M6 are unruled. ⧗ Scale "nation-state" is read from "always-already the Spiral's operating layer" in the card.
Presses on the story: Victoria's Growth is to stop believing its output (S12 FUNCTION). Its S12 invariant, ruled keep 9/15: no Star-Rating rises without a memory going missing from the record.
