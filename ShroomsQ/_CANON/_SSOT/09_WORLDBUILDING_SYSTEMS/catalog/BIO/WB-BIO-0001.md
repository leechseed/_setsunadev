# The Kudzu (the creep)

Part plant, part virus, part animal: a techno-organic growth that spreads over ground and takes what it covers, like the zerg creep in StarCraft II (Chief, 9/29).
**Origin ⧗:** the 5/11/2025 setup note: a south-pole "3rd impact" sets off a buried quantum-entangled object that "awakens an ancient evil … the techno kudzu?" The 2026 notes pair "the kudzu zerg" with "ocean 3rd impact · Antarctica".
**When:** the impact is an era 2 event (RULED 9/29); the creep is spreading in the West during OXO.
**Canon texture:** DCUS reads "kudzu over century brick" (three-check pass A3). That may be the first creep reaching the South ⧗, unruled.
**Scale:** world-economy ⧗, since it began at the pole and holds a whole region.
Not the 2024 City Kudzu (Leonola lineage, kept separate).
