## Purpose

This page is the Pattern half of the foundation. A pattern is a simple cycle that repeats at every scale. Small patterns stack on each other. The stack produces emergent structure.

Chief, 10/4: "we'll be using simple patterns over and over again… then they're stacked on top each other to be emergent."

Each entry gives the name, the steps, the tier where it runs, and its scales.

## Three kinds of cycle

Chief asked: epistemology patterns or design patterns? "software development cycle and like research cycles". All three are real. Each has one home.

| Cycle | Steps | Home |
|---|---|---|
| **Research cycle** | observe, guess, test, conclude | Truth half |
| **Design patterns** | story and world shapes | Pattern half (this page) |
| **Development cycle** | plan, build, test, ship | Operations |

All three share one loop underneath: **try, check, adjust, repeat.** Engineers know it as PDCA: plan, do, check, act.

## Pattern 2: the change cycle

**Steps** (names provisional: the CDR proposed them, Chief has not ruled the words)

1. **Want.** A person or group needs something.
2. **Push.** They act to get it.
3. **Cost.** The world charges them for acting.
4. **Change.** They, or the world, end up different.

The change feeds the next want. The cycle repeats.

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| **Tier** | Fabula (the world tick) and narratology (the story beat) |
| **Scales** | Scene (a character), arc (a season), era (a faction or city over centuries) |

At the fabula tier the cycle moves the world forward. At the narratology tier it becomes the beat the bard tells.
