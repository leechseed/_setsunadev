## THE SEVEN CANONIZED PRINCIPLES (May 2025, ratified 2026-08-31)

### P1 · ENTERTAINMENT-FIRST — the primacy
**The law:** the primary function of storytelling is to entertain; all other objectives ride on its back. Engagement before message · emotional logic over realism · momentum is sacred · stylization is power · character as catalyst.
**Forbids:** realism fetishism · moral didacticism · fear-based writing · representation tokenism · subversion fatigue.
**Dictum:** *"A bored audience is a dead audience."*
**Address:** the prescriptive L0 — the root claim in command form. Ancestor of the Drop tone canon.

### P2 · THE AMERICAN CENTER — the audience contract
**The law:** house stories are for Americans — rooted, localized, specific; universality is reached by depth into the specific, never by flattening. The American mythic terrain (contradiction · excess · violence · spiritual hunger · individualism · decay) is story fuel, not backdrop.
**Forbids:** creative globalism · moral exporting · cultural neutrality.
**Dictum:** *"The American Experience isn't your backdrop. It's your spine."*
**Address:** L7 genre/audience. Carries the challenge-phrase thesis; DCUS's Southern Gothic register descends from its trope canon.

### P3 · SATURATION — sexuality as bloodstream
**The law:** sexuality is aesthetic, not genre — woven into the DNA of the narrative, never flattened to "sex scenes" nor neutered to metaphor. Mastery over indulgence: when to reveal, restrain, transgress, withhold. Earned, always.
**Forbids:** working around it (failure of nerve) · spectacle-only (failure of depth) · refusing it (refusal of truth).
**Dictum:** *"Sexuality is not a spice. It is the bloodstream."*
**Address:** L6 theme + the L9 EROS bridge — the BLACK/ORANGE seam, stated as doctrine. How OXO carries it without being it.

### P4 · ORIGINALITY AS CRAFT — the library charter
**The law:** originality is mastery recombined with intent, never rebellion from ignorance. Know the canon to bend it. This is the BVX-LEARN thesis in doctrine form — the why of the 1,106-source library and the spine's rival map.
**Forbids:** performative weirdness · imitation disguised as innovation · chasing novelty over excellence.
**Dictum:** *"Craft is the mother of originality."*
**Address:** preamble to the spine's rival map; the distill queue's mandate.

### P5 · THE SETPIECE PRINCIPLE — plot_systems seed #1
**The law:** the setpiece is the apex unit of Entertainment-First design — engineered spectacle carrying theme, stakes, and arc. Eight archetypes (Action · Emotional · Ideological · Revelation · Atmospheric · Symbolic · Meta · Nested); anatomy Setup → Rising Tension → Payoff → Aftermath; placed at act breaks, never crowded, always followed by reset.
**Forbids:** spectacle without substance · saturation of peaks · empty emotional payload · player disempowerment.
**Dictum:** *"If it doesn't thrill, it kills."*
**Address:** L4, below the spine's floor — first shelf of the plot_systems domain (lands where decision #8 puts it).

### P6 · THE SERIAL HOOK — plot_systems seed #2
**The law:** every container closes on compulsion. Five hook types (Mystery · Consequence · Thematic Echo · Character · Mechanical); interlocked and leapfrogged across installments; paid off, never just teased. The coupling mechanism between the Scale Ladder's containers — the ladder stacks them, the hook welds them.
**Forbids:** empty teases · unpaid hooks · uniform intensity.
**Dictum:** *"A great hook doesn't ask you to keep going — it dares you not to."*
**Address:** L4 + the ladder; feeds M6–M8 serialized grammars (BOLO 16).

### P7 · THE NARRATIVE DILEMMA — plot_systems seed #3
**The law:** the highest-efficiency device — mutually exclusive options, real stakes on every path, double fallout for inaction; the choice advances plot and exposes character in one stroke. The folk form of the spine's Problem quad under Optionlock pressure, and its scene-level executable.
**Forbids:** painless exits · choices without consequence · stasis-as-option.
**Dictum:** *"Not 'what happens?' — 'what will they choose, and what does that choice reveal?'"*
**Address:** L2/L4 bridge; scene-card fuel.

## THE THREE NATIVE CHECKS (coined 2026-08-31 — v0, spoken register preserved by order)

**The mode is the doctrine.** Each check is answered **off the cuff, in one breath — voice-memo register.** Papi's order, verbatim intent: the articulation must never be "multiplied upon itself." **Forbidden for all three:** taxonomies of the check, nested frameworks, meta-layers, scoring rubrics. One element, one breath, next.

### P8 · THE FANTASY DRIVE
**The law:** every element in the system — every character, place, object, scene, mechanic, every little tiny thing — carries its **intended fantasy**: what the audience gets to *be, feel, or do* through it. If no one can say the fantasy of a thing in one breath, the thing is not done.
**The capture:** a voice-memo-style line per element — off the cuff, esoteric welcome, never systematized.
**Rank:** Papi, on coining it: *"probably the most important thing."*
**Address:** systemic — every spine level, every ladder rung, every 12-layer sheet, every plot element. No exceptions by design.

### P9 · THE SEXY CHECK
**The law:** at every address, up and down the spine and the plot: **is this sexy? Yes or no** — then one line of why (or why that's acceptable). Binary first, explanation second. Rides P3: saturation is the doctrine, this is its per-element enforcement.
**Address:** systemic, same coverage as P8.

### P10 · THE AESTHETIC CHECK
**The law:** every element must be interesting in at least one of **COLOR · SHAPE · TEXTURE**. If it isn't interesting in color, it must be in shape or texture. Name which one carries it.
**Address:** systemic; natural seam with the Texture Layer (verbal texture) and the taxonomy engine's aesthetic libraries (visual ground truth).
