# 📐 SSOT: THE MEDIUM GRAMMARS — the channel contracts

**What this is:** the final limb. A medium is a **channel contract**: what carries the telling (signal), who controls time (regime), what the audience can do (agency), and what apparatus produces it. **The governing law: story-side is medium-independent; discourse-side is where the medium bites.** A storyform survives adaptation intact; a Telling Profile does not — every medium re-instruments the Ten Questions with its own machinery. That is the transmedia principle (McErlean 0126): one IP at R7, many medium instantiations.

---

|---|---|---|---|---|---|

## THE GRAMMARS — how each medium re-instruments texture

### M1 · PROSE
Full interiority access — the only medium where T2 internal focalization is native and free; free indirect discourse is prose's signature instrument. Duration (T4) fully elastic: a sentence can hold a year, a chapter a second. **Holdings:** the entire CRE craft shelf; the Telling Profile applies verbatim.

### M2 · FILM
- **T2 focalization = shot selection** — POV shot, over-shoulder, insert: the camera decides who sees (Grammar of the Shot 1042; Master Shots 0138/1053/1054).
- **T4 duration = editing** — the cut IS ellipsis; montage is summary; the long take is stretch. Pacing lives in the edit suite.
- **T1 voice** = camera + optional VO; interiority must be *performed or shown*, never stated free.
- **Holdings:** VIS shelf (100) · Aronson 0188, Frayne 0228 (screenplay = the prose-side notation of film).

### M3 · TV / SERIAL
Film grammar plus the R6/R7 machinery made native: seasons = nested storyforms, "previously on" = repetitive frequency (T5) as apparatus, title cards and cold opens = paratext (T8) as rhythm. **Mittell (0565)** is the governing holding — complex TV as the operational poetics of seriality.

### M4 · GAMES
The contract inverts: **the player co-authors.** Four native mechanisms:
- **Ludonarrative** — mechanics ARE meaning; dissonance occurs when the systems argue against the story (the invariant layer applies to *rules*: a mechanic can breach a narrative invariant).
- **Environmental storytelling** — the setting slice *narrates*: S5 SCAR and S10 UNDERSIDE read as level design; the player is the focalizer walking the strata.
- **T3 order becomes topology** — quest structure is the ladder with player-variable sequence; the four R7 series patterns appear as campaign architectures.
- **T10 collapses** — narratee = player; direct address is the tutorial voice.
**Holdings:** GAM (217) — Berger 0093/0404 · Breault 0534 · Despain 0498 · Once Upon a Pixel 0144 · Finley 0104. House consumers: **Bongobabe** (clicker loop = a duration grammar), the h-game board.

### M5 · TTRPG
The purest structural mirror the lattice has:
- **The GM is a live extradiegetic narrator**; the table is collective authorship — emergent storyform.
- **Rules are invariants made mechanical** — the constraint layer implemented as a game system; a rulebook is a DOCTRINE 0 artifact with dice.
- **Sourcebooks/gazetteers are printed setting slices** (the Module 2 echo, confirmed: the scaffolding Papi called runs both directions — TTRPG books validate the slice; the slice can *author* TTRPG books).
**Holdings:** GURPS 0447 · Kobold 0458/0541 · Against Worldbuilding 0349.

### M6 · MUSIC VIDEO — v1 scaffold (BOLO 16 deepens)
- **The track is the autocrat:** duration (T4) is dictated — the edit obeys the beat grid; narrative must compress to song length.
- **Three canonical modes** (Vernallis's field): performance · narrative · conceptual — most videos braid them.
- **Lyric–image counterpoint** = the medium's native thematic instrument (T7 by juxtaposition).
- ⚠️ Zero holdings — Vernallis (*Experiencing Music Video*) is the anchor acquisition candidate.

### M7 · SOCIAL / SHORT-FORM — v1 scaffold (BOLO 16 deepens)
- **The hook economy:** retention is won per-second; the first second is the inciting incident or death — R1/R2 scale *is* the whole container.
- **The creator-persona = homodiegetic narrator** whose persistence across posts is the R7 serial pattern (braided, parasocial).
- **The algorithm is distributor-paratext** (T8): captions, sounds, trends are threshold apparatus the platform co-writes.
- House consumer: the Social channels project (25 convs, zero artifacts — this grammar is what those conversations lacked).

### M8 · PMV / HMV — v1 scaffold, ORANGE (BOLO 16 deepens)
- Music-video grammar × erotic function: the edit escalates with the track — **structure = an arousal ladder cut to a beat grid**; HMV sources animate material, PMV live-action.
- The compilation logic is curatorial: sourced-clip selection is the authorship (T1 voice = the edit itself).
- House echoes: the **PMV call-and-response series concept** (forgotten-generation doc) · taxonomy-engine act-track vocabulary. Surfaces are Ultrasin-side per the firewall.

### M9 · PORN (live-action) — ORANGE
- **Direct address is normalized metalepsis** (T9/T10): performer-to-camera is the default contract, not a violation — the medium runs on parasocial second person.
- **The scene is the native unit** with a conventionalized escalation arc; register vocabulary = the **house register** (taxonomy engine ruling 10 — photo-type production/dress/expression axes shared with the shoot set).
- Craft grammar lives here; content systems live Ultrasin-side (§B, shoot set, taxonomy engine).

### M10 · THEATRE
Live co-presence: duration is mostly scene-speed (T4 cannot cut), setting is physically real (S1 BODY is built, not described), direct address is an honored tradition (T10), and every performance is a variant text. **Holdings:** PRF (15) — the thinnest shelf; low priority by volume.
