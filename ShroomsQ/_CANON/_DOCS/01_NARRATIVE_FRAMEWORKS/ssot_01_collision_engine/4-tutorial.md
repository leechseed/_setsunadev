# Tutorial: run Tori × the DCUS

You will classify rows the way the 2026-08-26 proof did. Have the [reference](2-reference.md) open for the table.

## Step 1: set the scope

Write the scope line: `tori × DCUS [OXO.primary.M2–M4]`. This is a C×S collision, so you use the diagonal.

## Step 2: take the first row

Pair L1 CORE (13) with S1 BODY. Tori's identity is Earth-built: Taurus Sun, shaped by labor. The campus is heritage masonry. She reads it fluently. Mark it Void→Trine. No war is here. This is a cold row.

## Step 3: take the second row

Pair L2 VITAL (14) with S2 WEATHER. Mars-exalted physicality thrives in Gulf heat. Mark Trine. Then notice the warning: S2 is canon-thin. Mark the row underpowered. You found a gap.

## Step 4: take a hot row

Pair L4 WILL (14) with S4 LAW. Her will sits one point above her CORE. The Law is total: Star-Rating and the Administration OS. Mark Opposition. Note that Sun□Saturn is the Ban mechanism and S4 is the Ban's institutional body.

## Step 5: take the hottest row

Pair L9 EROS with S9 ALLURE. The meritocratic promise offers the feeling racing gave her. Racing is her only clean channel. Mark Trine-as-trap. The promise hooks the one unguarded line of her chart.

## Step 6: take the structural row

Pair L12 FUNCTION with S12 FUNCTION. You interpret nothing here. The storyform already binds the MC into the OS arena. Mark Structural.

## Step 7: read the verdict

Count your rows. You should have seven hot rows (3, 4, 5, 8, 9, 10, 11) and two cold rows (1, 2). You should also have one gap, one re-derived beat (row 6) and one structural row (12). The hot rows are the scene menu for Movements 2–4.

## Check yourself

You did it right if some rows came out cold. The engine found what canon knew, what canon implied, and one thing canon had not stated.
