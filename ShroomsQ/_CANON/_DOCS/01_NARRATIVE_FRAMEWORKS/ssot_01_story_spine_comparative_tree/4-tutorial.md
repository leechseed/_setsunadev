# Tutorial: read The Outliers through the spine

You will read one populated story, The Outliers (OXO), through the eight spine levels. You will end by naming its ending shape. The source holds every value you use. Keep the [reference](2-reference.md) open.

## Step 1: state the root claim (L0)

The spine says a story is a Grand Argument. Say it for OXO in one sentence: one Story Mind argues that an approach to a problem succeeds or fails.

The spine's OXO instance gives the verdict in the dynamics. You will find it in step 4.

## Step 2: name the four seats (L1)

Write four labels: OS, MC, IC, RS. Next to each write the pronoun: they, I, you, we. In OXO, Tori is the main character. The source also names Anna as the influence-character derivation. Check: no seat is empty.

## Step 3: give each seat a Domain (L2)

The four Domains are Situation, Activity, Fixed Attitude and Manipulation. Each seat takes exactly one. The source gives one OXO fact here: the overall story sits in Situation, and its Concern is The Past. DCUS is the place that embodies that Domain.

> **OPEN:** the source gives no Domain for the MC, IC or RS seats. Leave those three blank.

## Step 4: set the eight switches (L3)

Fill this table from the source's OXO instance. Copy it exactly.

| Dynamic | OXO |
|---|---|
| Resolve | Change |
| Growth | Stop |
| Approach | Do-er |
| Problem-Solving Style | Linear |
| Driver | Action |
| Limit | Optionlock |
| Outcome | Failure |
| Judgment | Good |

## Step 5: read the ending

Take Outcome and Judgment. Find the pair in the ending matrix.

Failure with Good is the **personal triumph**. That is OXO's shape. Check: it is not tragedy (Failure/Bad) and not triumph (Success/Good).

## Step 6: find where plot begins (L4)

The Driver is Action. Actions turn the acts. Each throughline runs 4 signposts and 3 journeys. The spine stops here. Scenes and beats belong to plot_systems.

## Step 7: place Tori and the school (L5 and setting)

Tori's function lives in L12_FUNCTION. Her body, psychology and origin live in L1 to L11. DCUS is the setting entity. Its collapse is the Failure/Good corridor at L3.
