# Tutorial: run the three checks on DCUS

You will run each check on the Delta Coast Ultra School (DCUS). Then you will compare your lines with the ones Chief kept. Keep the [reference](2-reference.md) open, but do not look at the DCUS rows yet.

## Step 1: the Fantasy Drive

Ask: what does the audience get to be, feel or do through DCUS? Write one line. Number it F3.

Compare with the kept line. It reads: getting the letter from the school that eats people, being special enough that the trap wants you.

Check: your line states an audience experience, not a feature of the school.

## Step 2: the Sexy Check

Ask: is DCUS sexy? Write YES or NO, then one line of why. Number it S3.

Compare with the kept line. The verdict is YES. Seduction is the school's mechanism. It is an institution that wants you. Feed-linked clothing makes the gaze a uniform.

Check: your answer gave a verdict first and one line after.

## Step 3: the Aesthetic Check

Ask: is DCUS interesting in color, shape or texture? Pick one carrier. Write one line. Number it A3.

Compare with the kept line. The carrier is TEXTURE. The line is Southern Gothic rot: kudzu over century brick, a product word bolted onto prestige stone.

## Step 4: land the lines

DCUS has a sheet: `_CANON_NODES/delta-coast-ultra-school.md`. Kept lines F3, S3 and A3 land there as `fantasy:`, `sexy:` and `aesthetic:` fields.

## Step 5: try an element with a gap

Look at Anna. Her sexy line is thin. Her aesthetic carrier is GAP. Run Step 3 on Anna. The honest answer is the one the pass gave: no visual tissue is authored. So you write GAP and park the sheet. You do not invent a carrier.

## What you did

You ran three separate checks on one element, matched them with Chief's kept lines, and met a gap. Next, read the [how-to](3-how-to.md) to run a full sweep.
