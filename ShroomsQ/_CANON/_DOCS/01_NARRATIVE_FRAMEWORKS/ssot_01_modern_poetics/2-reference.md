# Modern poetics: reference

Source: [📐 ssot_01_modern_poetics.md](../../../_SSOT/01_NARRATIVE_FRAMEWORKS/📐%20ssot_01_modern_poetics.md). Version 1.1.0. Name ruled 2026-08-31. Trunk: BLACK (P3 and the three checks bridge ORANGE). Applies to all IPs. Why: [concept](1-concept.md).

## THE SEVEN CANONIZED PRINCIPLES (May 2025, ratified 2026-08-31)

Each entry gives the law, what it forbids, its dictum and its address.

### P1 · ENTERTAINMENT-FIRST — the primacy

- **Law:** the primary function of storytelling is to entertain; all other objectives ride on its back. Engagement before message; emotional logic over realism; momentum is sacred; stylization is power; character as catalyst.
- **Forbids:** realism fetishism, moral didacticism, fear-based writing, representation tokenism, subversion fatigue.
- **Dictum:** "A bored audience is a dead audience."
- **Address:** the prescriptive L0, the root claim in command form. Ancestor of the Drop tone canon.

### P2 · THE AMERICAN CENTER — the audience contract

- **Law:** house stories are for Americans: rooted, localized, specific. Universality comes by depth into the specific. The American mythic terrain (contradiction, excess, violence, spiritual hunger, individualism, decay) is story fuel.
- **Forbids:** creative globalism, moral exporting, cultural neutrality.
- **Dictum:** "The American Experience isn't your backdrop. It's your spine."
- **Address:** L7 genre/audience. Carries the challenge-phrase thesis. DCUS's Southern Gothic register descends from its trope canon.

### P3 · SATURATION — sexuality as bloodstream

- **Law:** sexuality is aesthetic, not genre. It is woven into the narrative's DNA, never flattened to sex scenes nor neutered to metaphor. Mastery over indulgence: when to reveal, restrain, transgress, withhold. Earned, always.
- **Forbids:** working around it (failure of nerve), spectacle-only (failure of depth), refusing it (refusal of truth).
- **Dictum:** "Sexuality is not a spice. It is the bloodstream."
- **Address:** L6 theme plus the L9 EROS bridge. The BLACK/ORANGE seam stated as doctrine.

### P4 · ORIGINALITY AS CRAFT — the library charter

- **Law:** originality is mastery recombined with intent, never rebellion from ignorance. Know the canon to bend it. The BVX-LEARN thesis in doctrine form: the why of the 1,106-source library and the spine's rival map.
- **Forbids:** performative weirdness, imitation disguised as innovation, chasing novelty over excellence.
- **Dictum:** "Craft is the mother of originality."
- **Address:** preamble to the spine's rival map; the distill queue's mandate.

### P5 · THE SETPIECE PRINCIPLE — plot_systems seed #1

- **Law:** the setpiece is the apex unit of Entertainment-First design: engineered spectacle carrying theme, stakes and arc. Eight archetypes: Action, Emotional, Ideological, Revelation, Atmospheric, Symbolic, Meta, Nested. Anatomy: Setup, Rising Tension, Payoff, Aftermath. Placed at act breaks, never crowded, always followed by reset.
- **Forbids:** spectacle without substance, saturation of peaks, empty emotional payload, player disempowerment.
- **Dictum:** "If it doesn't thrill, it kills."
- **Address:** L4, below the spine's floor. First shelf of the plot_systems domain.

### P6 · THE SERIAL HOOK — plot_systems seed #2

- **Law:** every container closes on compulsion. Five hook types: Mystery, Consequence, Thematic Echo, Character, Mechanical. Interlocked and leapfrogged across installments; paid off, never just teased. The coupling between the Scale Ladder's containers: the ladder stacks them, the hook welds them.
- **Forbids:** empty teases, unpaid hooks, uniform intensity.
- **Dictum:** "A great hook doesn't ask you to keep going — it dares you not to."
- **Address:** L4 plus the ladder. Feeds the M6 to M8 serialized grammars (BOLO 16).
- **Ledger (IF catalog call 6, ruled 2026-10-05):** every hook is an entry in the hook ledger in 08 §9A: hook type, container it closes, payoff address (an 08 time code).

### P7 · THE NARRATIVE DILEMMA — plot_systems seed #3

- **Law:** the highest-efficiency device: mutually exclusive options, real stakes on every path, double fallout for inaction. The choice advances plot and exposes character in one stroke. The folk form of the spine's Problem quad under Optionlock pressure, and its scene-level executable.
- **Forbids:** painless exits, choices without consequence, stasis-as-option.
- **Dictum:** "Not 'what happens?' — 'what will they choose, and what does that choice reveal?'"
- **Address:** L2/L4 bridge; scene-card fuel.

## THE THREE NATIVE CHECKS (coined 2026-08-31 — v0, spoken register preserved by order)

**The mode is the doctrine.** Each check is answered off the cuff, in one breath, in voice-memo register. Forbidden for all three: taxonomies of the check, nested frameworks, meta-layers, scoring rubrics. One element, one breath, next.

### P8 · THE FANTASY DRIVE

- **Law:** every element carries its intended fantasy: what the audience gets to be, feel or do through it. If no one can say the fantasy in one breath, the thing is not done.
- **Capture (RULED 2026-08-31):** Chief speaks it in-chat, dictation as-is. Chief's voice is the line of quality and the canon form. Claude may draft candidate lines, marked `draft`. A draft becomes canon only when Chief keeps it or speaks it over.
- **Rank:** Chief: "probably the most important thing."
- **Address:** systemic. Every spine level, ladder rung, 12-layer sheet and plot element. No exceptions.
- **Landing (IF catalog call 6, ruled 2026-10-05):** the three checks land as `fantasy:`, `sexy:` and `aesthetic:` fields on the entity sheet, the plot card and the scene card.
- **Sweep order (RULED 2026-08-31):** three full sweeps: fantasy across everything, then sexy across everything, then aesthetic across everything. Never bundled per element.

### P9 · THE SEXY CHECK

- **Law:** at every address: is this sexy? Yes or no, then one line of why (or why that is acceptable). Binary first, explanation second. It rides P3: saturation is the doctrine, this is its per-element enforcement.
- **Address:** systemic, same coverage as P8.

### P10 · THE AESTHETIC CHECK

- **Law:** every element must be interesting in at least one of COLOR, SHAPE, TEXTURE. Name which one carries it.
- **Address:** systemic. Natural seam with the Texture Layer (verbal texture) and the taxonomy engine's aesthetic libraries (visual ground truth).
