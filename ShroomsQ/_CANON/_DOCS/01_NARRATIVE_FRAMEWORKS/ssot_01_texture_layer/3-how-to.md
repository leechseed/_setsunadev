# The texture layer: how-to

Source: [📐 ssot_01_texture_layer.md](../../../_SSOT/01_NARRATIVE_FRAMEWORKS/📐%20ssot_01_texture_layer.md). Fields are in the [reference](2-reference.md).

## Record a TELLING PROFILE

1. Pick the ladder address. Use R5 unless a unit needs its own profile.
2. Write `scope:` with the address, for example `OXO.primary`.
3. Answer T1: person, level, reliability, narration time.
4. Answer T2: focalization mode and the focal character or characters.
5. Answer T3: the order baseline and any licensed anachronies.
6. Answer T4: the default gait and the signature moves.
7. Answer T5: the signature uses, iterative or repetitive.
8. Answer T6: register, distance policy, and whether free indirect discourse is allowed.
9. Answer T7: tone and the target mood curve.
10. Answer T8: the paratext apparatus.
11. Answer T9: the metalepsis policy, permitted or never.
12. Answer T10: how the narratee is built.
13. For the T11 and T12 fields, use the [texture system](../ssot_01_texture_system/3-how-to.md) profile.

## Translate a craft term into a configuration

1. Find the craft term in the translation grid.
2. Read its configuration, for example third limited is heterodiegetic plus internal fixed.
3. Write that configuration in the T1 and T2 fields.
4. If the term is not in the grid, split it into voice (T1) and vision (T2) first.

## Separate who speaks from who sees

1. Write T1: who tells the passage.
2. Write T2: whose perception the reader gets.
3. Check that the two entries are separate decisions.
4. If a narrator outside the story sees through one character, record heterodiegetic voice and internal fixed focalization.

## Override the profile for one scene

1. Open the scene card.
2. Fill the `telling:` line with T1, T2 and T4 for that scene.
3. Leave the profile unchanged. The scene override holds only for that unit.

## Set a passage's speed

1. Pick one of the five speeds: scene, summary, stretch, pause or ellipsis.
2. Record it in the scene card's `duration:` field.
3. Repeat across units. The pattern of speeds is the pacing.

## Choose a frequency

1. Ask how often the event happened and how often the text tells it.
2. Mark it singulative, repetitive or iterative.
3. Use iterative for habit. The S8 HABIT layer renders iteratively.

## Design the paratext

1. List the apparatus: titles, chapter heads, epigraphs, in-world documents.
2. Record the list in T8 of the profile.
3. Treat each item as a surface the reader crosses before the text.

## Queue a missing source

1. Name the source and the question it answers.
2. Add it to the BVX.1107+ acquisition queue.
