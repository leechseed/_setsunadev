# Dramatica-Astrology Interface (DAI): how-to

Tables and labels live in the [reference](2-reference.md).

## Derive a full chart from a Dramatica ingest

1. Open the locked Dramatica Ingest Template.
2. Place Sun, Moon, Ascendant, Mercury, Venus, Mars and Saturn (Steps 1-7), in that order.
3. Place Jupiter, Uranus, Neptune and Pluto (Steps 8-11).
4. Derive the Midheaven, Descendant and IC.
5. Derive the aspects.
6. Assign the houses.
7. Calculate the dignities.
8. Assign the 12th House.
9. Transfer every assignment to the Character Astrology Ingest Template.

## Derive a minimum viable chart

1. Place Steps 1-7: Sun, Moon, Ascendant, Mercury, Venus, Mars, Saturn.
2. Derive the aspects (Step 15).
3. Assign the houses (Step 16).
4. Calculate the dignities (Step 17).
5. Hand the chart to the Step 1 gate.

## Pick a Sun sign

1. Read the MC Domain. Map it to an element.
2. Read the MC Concern. Map it to a modality.
3. Find the intersection on the Sign Selection Grid.
4. Read the sign description against the character's self-concept.
5. If it feels wrong, re-examine the modality first.

## Pick a Moon sign

1. Read the MC Problem and MC Response.
2. Look up the Problem element in the Planetary Resonance table.
3. Select a sign for the stress-collapse pattern.
4. Prefer an element different from the Sun's.
5. Test: does the character retreat into this sign under maximum stress?

## Pick an Ascendant

1. Read the Approach. Do-er: Cardinal or Fire. Be-er: Fixed or Water.
2. Within the subset, choose the sign whose shadow quality matches the MC Critical Flaw.
3. Test: does the Critical Flaw hide inside the first impression?

## Derive an aspect from Dramatica tension

1. List the character's element pairs: Problem/Solution, Focus/Direction, Unique Ability/Critical Flaw, and reinforcing quad elements.
2. Give Problem/Solution an Opposition.
3. Give Focus/Direction a Square.
4. Give Unique Ability/Critical Flaw a Conjunction if the flaw is inside the ability, or a Square if it undermines from outside.
5. Give reinforcing quad elements a Trine.
6. Check sign geometry. If it contradicts the tension, keep the Dramatica tension.

## Assign houses

1. Read the MC Domain and take its house emphasis.
2. Put the MC Problem planet in an angular house or the 12th.
3. Put Saturn in the house of the limit's domain.
4. Put Mars in the field of action.
5. Put the Moon in the emotional center of gravity.

## Calculate dignity

1. Mark each planet in its domicile or exaltation sign as Essential Dignity.
2. Mark each planet in its detriment or fall sign as Essential Debility.
3. Mark the rest Peregrine.
4. If the story disagrees with the traditional result, override with a written reason.

## Place a planet in the 12th House

1. Check the MC Problem planet if the character is a Change character.
2. Check any planet whose function the character denies, represses or projects.
3. Cross-reference L10 SHADOW in the authoritative documentation.
4. Note any Mercury aspect to a 12th House planet. The Hauntology Effect applies.

## Derive a Tier 2 chart

1. Choose the Sun sign from the primary element-signature element.
2. Choose the Moon sign from a secondary element.
3. Assign one aspect between Sun and Moon: Square or Opposition for tension, Conjunction or Trine for reinforcement.
4. Add no houses, dignities, angles or outer planets.
