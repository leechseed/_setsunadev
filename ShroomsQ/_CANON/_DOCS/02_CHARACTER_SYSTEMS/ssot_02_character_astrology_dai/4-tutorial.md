# Tutorial: derive a Sun sign and a Moon sign

You will run Steps 1 and 2 of the derivation sequence. The source's worked example, the Victoria Midnight chart, has moved to [victoria-midnight-chart.md](../../../../_CANON_NODES/victoria-midnight-chart.md).

> **OPEN:** the source holds no worked chart in this document, and the moved chart lacks the MC Concern. This lesson uses a training input marked as invented for practice. It is not canon for any character.

Tables are in the [reference](2-reference.md).

## Step 1: Pick the training input

Practice character: MC Domain = Situation. MC Concern = a process concern, "Obtaining". MC Problem = Pursuit. MC Response = Avoid. These are invented for the lesson. The Domain and the concern types come from the source's tables.

## Step 2: Map the Domain to an element

Look up Situation in the Domain-to-Element table. Situation maps to Earth.

## Step 3: Map the Concern to a modality

Look up "Obtaining" in the Concern-to-Modality table. Obtaining is a process concern, so the modality is Cardinal.

## Step 4: Cross them on the grid

Find the Earth row and the Cardinal column on the Sign Selection Grid. The cell reads Capricorn. The Sun sign is Capricorn.

## Step 5: Validate

Read the Capricorn description against the self-concept the Domain and Concern define. If the sign feels wrong, re-examine the modality. That mapping is the most flexible.

## Step 6: Start the Moon

Look up Pursuit / Avoid in the Problem Elements to Planetary Resonance table. The affinity is the Mars axis. The signs of strength are Aries, Scorpio and Capricorn.

## Step 7: Choose a different element from the Sun

The rule prefers a Moon in a different element than the Sun. Capricorn is Earth. Capricorn is therefore out, unless the stress response mirrors identity. That case is rare.

Choose from the remaining signs: Aries (Fire) or Scorpio (Water). Pick the one that encodes how this character collapses under stress. For the practice, pick Scorpio.

## Step 8: Test the Moon

Ask whether the character retreats into Scorpio behavior under maximum stress. If the sign feels comfortable or rational, discard it. The Moon is where the character reacts, not where they think clearly.

## Step 9: Record the result

You have Sun in Capricorn and Moon in Scorpio for the training character. Each came from a named rule and a named table.

## What you learned

You mapped a Domain and a Concern to a sign through a grid. You used a resonance table as a decision space and then chose with judgment. You applied the different-element rule. Next, see the [how-to](3-how-to.md) for the other planets, aspects and houses.
