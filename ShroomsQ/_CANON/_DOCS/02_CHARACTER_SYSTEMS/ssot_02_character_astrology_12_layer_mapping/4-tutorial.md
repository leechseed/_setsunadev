# Tutorial: check Victoria's SOCIAL value against her chart

You will run one consistency check. The source supplies the case: Victoria Midnight has SOCIAL = 10 with an Aries Ascendant and a Virgo Venus. Feed definitions are in the [reference](2-reference.md).

## Step 1: Find the layer's feed

Open the Master Feed Table. The L3 SOCIAL row lists four sources: Ascendant sign, Venus sign, 7th House contents and Venus aspects. The feed type is justification plus texture.

## Step 2: Read the Ascendant

Her Ascendant is Aries. The Ascendant is the mask the world meets. Aries is a Cardinal sign, and Cardinal Ascendants project initiating energy.

## Step 3: Read Venus

Her Venus is in Virgo. Venus tells what she values in social exchange and how she attracts or repels others.

> **OPEN:** the source gives no 7th House contents or Venus aspects for Victoria. This check uses the two placements it does give.

## Step 4: Compare the pair

The source tells you to look hard at the Ascendant-Venus relationship. Harmonious signs mean a coherent interface. Tense signs mean an internal contradiction that the value should reflect.

> **OPEN:** the source does not rule whether Aries and Virgo count as harmonious or tense here. It gives its own reading in Step 5.

## Step 5: Read the source's verdict

The source says her SOCIAL = 10 describes an interface that is accurate but unmanaged. The Ascendant does not filter. Venus does not perform. So the interface transmits raw signal without protective encoding.

## Step 6: Test the number

The key nuance: low or average SOCIAL is not social incompetence. Victoria's capability is high elsewhere. The chart explains why SOCIAL stays at 10 despite that capability.

## Step 7: Record the result

Write one line of justification in the Vertical Slice for L3. It names the Aries Ascendant and the Virgo Venus. It states that the interface is accurate but unmanaged.

The check passes. The value and the chart agree, and no conflict protocol is needed.

## What you learned

You ran one consistency check from the feed table to the layer prose. You saw that an average number can be consistent with a high-capability chart. You saw that the chart justifies the number and never sets it. Next, see the [how-to](3-how-to.md) for the full checklist and for resolving conflicts.
