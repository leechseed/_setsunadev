# The plot system (syuzhet): tutorial

You will fill the plot card for M2 row 9, Tori's first Sync-rating session. Keep the [reference](2-reference.md) open.

1. **Read the scene.** Tori must choose: pull out and stay grey, or reach and take the counterfeit wholeness. She reaches and says "breathe". The Feed archives it as "Unrecognized input". Her rating climbs anyway. She is marked Verified.
2. **Find the fabula event.** The event is `m2_first_sync_verified`. You will read most fields from it.
3. **P1 ADDRESS.** Write `OXO.primary.M2.q1.s3`. Convert it to the canonical form `M2 · Q1 · S03`.
4. **P2 DRIVER.** The choice is a Decision, local to the crisis beat. It does not override the story-level Driver, which is Action.
5. **P3 VALUE-IN.** Read the before half of the transition: whole, polarity minus. She is grey, unranked and keeps control as safety.
6. **P4 TURN.** Name the mechanism: the Gap. Her minimal action, saying "breathe", draws a bigger-than-expected response. All five commandments are present.
7. **P5 VALUE-OUT.** In her read, it goes minus to plus. Underneath, it goes plus to minus. The gap between the two reads sets `irony_flag`.
8. **P6 SEAT.** MC throughline, signpost 1, The Past, Movement 2.
9. **P7 REVEAL.** The audience learns the hook is a trap. Tori learns nothing yet. The type is mixed, action plus revelation.
10. **P8 and P9.** Collision row L9×S9. This is the first unit in its sequence, so `stakes_delta: baseline`.
11. **P10.** Story level is cultcore. Scene level is Institutionalized, the right row for M2.
12. **P11.** `anachrony.type: none`. Duration is scene. Frequency is singulative, though the fabula event class is iterative. Frequency measures the telling.
13. **P12.** Write `storyform_id: oxo_primary_v1` and the story point.
14. **Check your work.** Ten of twelve layers fill from the scene card directly. The source parks `opposition_goal`, `test_phase` and the P12 Equity read for phase 2. Do the same, and leave them blank.

Next, try the [how-to](3-how-to.md) task "Classify a reordering (P11)" on the M3 crash analepsis card.
