# The trope graph: tutorial

You will walk the MC throughline, the hero's journey from inside one skin, across four acts. Keep the [reference](2-reference.md) open.

1. **Pick the rail.** MC is perspective "I". It has four signposts in a fixed order. You may not reorder them.
2. **Step to Act 1.** Take `vog.01_ordinary_world`. This is the baseline before any call.
3. **Move to the call.** Take `vog.02_call_to_adventure`. It matches `cam.01_call_to_adventure` and `prp.09_mediation`. These are three names for one unit.
4. **Attach a type.** The call needs a flavor. `tob.01_quest` is a whole-plot node with no fixed position, so it may ride here. Schmidt's own text calls `sch.17_daring_enterprise` "similar to a Quest".
5. **Pull a trope.** Open the trope record for `ConsequenceCombo`. Its `node` is `vog.02_call_to_adventure` and its `alt` is `vog.03_refusal_of_the_call`. That is a contested cell.
6. **Commit.** Choose one node at this signpost. Do not fire both. The tooltip would show `or: Refusal of the Call`.
7. **Jump ahead? No.** Try to go straight to `vog.08_the_ordeal`. The rail forbids it. Signpost 2 must resolve first.
8. **Reach the middle.** After signpost 2 resolves, take `vog.08_the_ordeal`. Pull dramatization from `prp.16_struggle`: `BigBadassBattleSequence` and `CataclysmClimax`.
9. **Reach the end.** Take `vog.12_return_with_the_elixir`. Love and Descension both link heavily into it. So a walk that carried either earlier has real support for resolving it here.
10. **Hand off.** Each node is a proposal. Send the Call to the fabula's event record. The fabula decides whether it becomes a dated event.
11. **Check your work.** You walked four real signposts in order. Any-phase content rode inside the rail and replaced nothing.

Next, try the [how-to](3-how-to.md) task "Seat a plot-card unit with the graph (P6)".
