# Pattern library: run the Command loop once

You will follow one small task through Pattern 1. The source's proof case is the ten system review pages on 10/4. You will use a plain stand-in task, a doc to review.

## Step 1. Chief speaks

Chief states the task: review one system page.

## Step 2. Capture to the BOLO

The CDR writes the task into the BOLO the same turn. The task now has a home.

## Step 3. Recon

The CDR reads the page and its source before writing anything. Recon costs no build.

## Step 4. Recs

The CDR brings recommendations. Each open question gets one rec.

## Step 5. One-word ruling

Chief rules in one word. The ruling is the order.

## Step 6. Build

The team builds the fix the ruling names. Nothing beyond it.

## Step 7. Script check

A script checks the build. The source's proof case counted pages passing, 1 of 10 at the start.

## Step 8. Publish

The CDR publishes the result.

## Step 9. Log

The CDR logs what happened. The log feeds the next loop.

## Step 10. See the pattern

The nine steps map to the SOP's four: recon, order, execute, consolidate. Steps 1 to 3 are recon. Steps 4 and 5 are the order. Steps 6 and 7 are execute. Steps 8 and 9 are consolidate.

> **OPEN:** the source does not give this step-to-SOP mapping. It only says the loop is the SOP's four steps seen as a pattern.

## What you did

You ran one cycle. On 10/4 the loop ran about eight times. The pages went from 1 of 10 to 10 of 10 passing. Next, try Pattern 2 on a scene: want, push, cost, change. The [how-to](3-how-to.md) holds the steps and the [reference](2-reference.md) holds both patterns.
