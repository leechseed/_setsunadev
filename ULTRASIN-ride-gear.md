# Ride Gear — chair taxonomy, bougiest → typical

**Field finding that started it:** flipped plastic folding chair = improvised grind rig. Works — "hardest buck running" — problems are slide and collapse risk. The product ladder above it:

| Tier | What | ~Cost | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Powered saddles** — The Cowgirl / The Unicorn, Sybian, Motorbunny | $700–2,200 | Machine grinds back. Taper form taken to its conclusion. DIY cousin = the queued **OSSM build** — research overlaps. |
| 2 | **Clear chairs** — Kartell **Victoria Ghost** (armless = the straddle pick), Louis Ghost, acrylic knockoffs | $100–450 | Polycarbonate, genuinely strong. **The camera argument: clear seat = only chair that doesn't block the shot.** |
| 3 | **Purpose-built foam** — **Liberator BonBon** | $100–200 | Toy-mount pocket, non-slip base, washable cover. *The single best direct upgrade from the folding chair.* |
| 4 | **Sex stools** — elastic/bungee, open-center variants | $60–200 | The chair itself bounces; open centers take suction mounts from below. Adds a vertical dynamic. |
| 5 | **The actual burlesque chair** — Thonet bentwood No. 14/18 silhouette | $80–400 | The cabaret prop. Straddle-friendly, backrest rail, solid wood, photographs like scenography. |
| 6 | **Improvised tier** — plastic/metal folding chair, **IKEA Marius stool** ($8, cult suction-cup base) | $8–20 | Current home. |

## Fixes for the current rig

1. **Slide**: rubber rug-grip pad or yoga mat under the legs — beats carpet, grips both directions, ~$10.
2. **Collapse risk (real)**: folding chairs fold mid-session with weight cantilevered off-design. Zip-tie/clamp the folding braces locked. Inspect plastic for **stress-whitening** (the pale lines before a crack).
3. Pad hard seat edges (thigh pressure) if sessions run long.

## Taper vs wide — resolved as "both"

- **Taper** (saddle, BonBon): thigh clearance, deeper rock.
- **Wide** (flat seat, ghost chair): pressure spread, stability, better presenting line on camera.
- Not one answer — **two setups for two shoots**. Argument for owning a BonBon *and* something clear.
