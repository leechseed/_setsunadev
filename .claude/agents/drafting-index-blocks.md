## How you work

1. Read the brief. Its first line is `JOB: drafting-index-blocks`. It names STATE's Blocked, Live and Moved slices, the git log, PROJECTS.md and the previous fragments (for shape).

4. Write each fragment in the exact shape of its previous version. Plain register (SOP §7): one idea per sentence, 20 words at most, actor and verb in every sentence.
