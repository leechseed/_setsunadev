# CTO reading list (for the cto seat)

Source: awesome-cto, https://github.com/kuchin/awesome-cto, CC0. Fifteen links from Architecture, Development process, Technologies, Due Diligence and Money; the hiring sections are left out. Fetch one link on demand; never in bulk. The list is thin on hardware (GPUs, motion capture, workstations), so for those, fetch the vendor spec page the brief names.

## Keep the design small
1. https://12factor.net - Twelve-Factor App: config, logs and state kept apart.
2. https://medium.com/@rdsubhas/10-modern-software-engineering-mistakes-bc67fbef4fc8 - ten over-engineering traps.
3. https://riak.com/posts/technical/microservices-please-dont/ - why not to split early; our box is one machine.
4. https://leaddev.com/technical-decision-making/accentuate-negative-making-non-perfect-decision - choosing when no option is clean.
5. https://github.com/danistefanovic/build-your-own-x - build-it-yourself references.

## Process and failure
6. https://nvie.com/posts/a-successful-git-branching-model/ - a branching model, a contrast to our trunk.
7. https://www.atlassian.com/continuous-delivery/continuous-integration/trunk-based-development - trunk-based work, our habit.
8. https://marker.medium.com/why-you-should-learn-to-stop-worrying-and-love-technical-debt-55bb5684f94c - when debt is fine.
9. http://www.startuplessonslearned.com/2008/11/five-whys.html - Five Whys for root cause.
10. https://github.com/danluu/post-mortems - real failures, to spot risks in a plan.

## Buying and diligence
11. https://akfpartners.com/growth-blog/technical-due-diligence-checklists - a checklist to adapt to any buy.
12. https://github.com/altunyurt/technical_due_diligence - a question list for any vendor or system.
13. https://gist.github.com/raphaelbauer/b31d49d91a0af6c1106bfc8ef4bf6d13 - a short IT checklist.
14. https://medium.com/engineering-operations/a-framework-for-balancing-and-budgeting-engineering-resourcing-d0cce0e6911c - budget framing for a spend.
15. https://www.infoentrepreneurs.org/en/guides/negotiate-the-right-deal-with-suppliers/ - supplier terms, warranty and returns.
