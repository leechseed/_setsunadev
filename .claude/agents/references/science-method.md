# Science method (hypothesis · test · contested-claim pass)

Used by sci-literature and sci-plausibility. Plain text; no scripts.

1. **Hypothesis.** State the claim as one sentence: "X holds because Y." Name at least one rival explanation.
2. **Test.** Say what evidence would break it: a number, a source, a known limit. Check the claim against that. Report what you checked and what you could not.
3. **Contested-claim pass.** Mark each claim established, emerging or contested (SOP 1.13). For a contested one, give the strongest rival view in one line and who holds it. Thin evidence (one paper, a preprint, pop-sci) gets flagged as thin.

The science advises; the story wins (ruled 2026-10-13).
