# CEO reading list (for the chief-of-staff seat)

Source: awesome-ceo, https://github.com/kuchin/awesome-ceo, CC0. Fifteen links from the Management, Product, Finance and Entrepreneurship sections. Fundraising, Sales and Hiring are left out because they do not fit a one-person operation. Fetch one link on demand; never in bulk.

## Running yourself and the work
1. https://www.paulgraham.com/makersschedule.html - Maker's Schedule, Manager's Schedule: why long blocks beat scattered meetings.
2. https://blog.doist.com/context-switching/ - what a context switch costs; supports "the one thing first".
3. https://delian.io/lessons-3 - Keith Rabois on being an effective executive: focus, delegate, own the output.
4. https://danluu.com/people-matter/ - why individual judgment still decides outcomes.
5. https://cutlefish.substack.com/p/tbm-2752-mandate-levels - Mandate Levels: how much a delegate may decide alone; fits our escalate rules.
6. https://github.com/LappleApple/awesome-leading-and-managing - a wider list; use only to find one topic.

## Product and ventures
7. https://www.mindtheproduct.com/product-is-hard-by-marty-cagan/ - the four tests: valuable, usable, feasible, viable.
8. https://a16z.com/2017/02/18/12-things-about-product-market-fit-2/ - how to tell if anyone wants the thing.
9. https://bothsidesofthetable.com/most-startups-should-be-deer-hunters-7fdecf58f4f6 - pick small targets you can finish.
10. https://leanstack.com/leancanvas - one-page business model for a venture riff.
11. https://playbook.samaltman.com - short startup playbook; read the focus and speed parts.

## Money and choices
12. https://medium.com/engineering-operations/a-framework-for-balancing-and-budgeting-engineering-resourcing-d0cce0e6911c - how to split a fixed budget across work.
13. https://www.infoentrepreneurs.org/en/guides/negotiate-the-right-deal-with-suppliers/ - supplier terms, returns and price.
14. https://blog.ycombinator.com/the-second-job-of-a-startup-ceo/ - the second job: keep the plan and the people in view.
15. https://hackernoon.com/85-things-i-learned-being-a-ceo-4c25fc1c7b99 - a grab bag; skim for traps.
