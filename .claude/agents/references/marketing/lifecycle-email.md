# Lifecycle email and lead magnets

Use this once a list exists, or when the brief asks for a free hook that earns email addresses plus the emails that follow.

## Email principles

- One email, one job, one main call to action.
- Value before the ask. Fewer, better emails win.
- Every email moves the reader somewhere clear.
- Write like a person: short paragraphs, mobile first, read it aloud.

## Sequences

| Type | Length | Goal |
|---|---|---|
| Welcome | 5 to 7 emails over 12 to 14 days | Deliver the promise, build trust, convert |
| Lead nurture | 6 to 8 over 2 to 3 weeks | Teach, prove, make the offer |
| Re-engagement | 3 to 4 over 2 weeks, after 30 to 60 days quiet | Win back or clean the list |

Welcome order: deliver the promised value, a quick win, the story, proof, an objection answered, the core benefit, the offer. Nurture ends with a direct offer.

Timing: welcome at once, early emails 1 to 2 days apart, nurture 2 to 4 days, long-term weekly.

Subject lines: clear beats clever, specific beats vague, 40 to 60 characters. Preview text adds to the subject, about 90 to 140 characters. Length: 150 to 300 words to teach, 300 to 500 to tell a story. Button text says action plus outcome.

Per email, write: send timing, subject, preview, body, call to action and its link, and the condition that skips it. Add exit conditions to the sequence and a metrics plan.

Never write: "it's not X, it's Y", "no X, no Y" lists, "the result?" reveals, "I hope this finds you well", "just checking in", bait subjects, em dashes in subject lines.

## Lead magnets

One specific problem, solved in under 30 minutes (ideally under 10). One format, works on a phone, leads naturally to the offer.

Formats by effort: checklist or cheat sheet (hours), template (hours), swipe file, email mini-course (weeks), ebook (weeks), quiz.

Gating: ask for the minimum. Every extra field loses 5 to 10 percent. Choices run from full gate to a preview to an ungated version with an optional signup.

Landing page: benefit headline, a preview image, three to five things inside, proof, a short form, FAQ. Use the thank-you page: confirm delivery, offer one next step.

Judge it by landing conversion (about 20 to 40 percent for warm traffic, 5 to 15 for cold), unsubscribes after delivery, and whether leads match the target buyer.

## How the Command uses it

The BOLO 135 creator income report is the lead magnet for BOLO 138 and for a future BoldVenture list: gated by email, followed by a short nurture ending in the offer. Kobra cart buyers get at most a post-purchase note. Keep adult-arm lists generic here and out of public files.
