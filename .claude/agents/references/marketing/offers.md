# Offers

Use this when the brief asks you to build or fix the thing being sold, not just the page that shows it. A better offer beats better copy on a weak one. Most "we need better copy" requests are really "we need a better offer."

## The value equation

Value rises with the dream outcome and the buyer's belief they will get it. It falls with the time to the result and the effort and sacrifice the buyer carries. Four levers:

| Lever | Raise value by |
|---|---|
| Dream outcome | Naming the bigger goal behind the surface ask |
| Belief it will work | Proof, a guarantee, a specific method |
| Time to result | A faster first win |
| Effort and sacrifice | Doing more of the work for them, fewer decisions |

"Lower the price" usually means "raise the top or cut the bottom." Price is the comparison, not the value.

## Six parts of a complete offer

1. Core deliverable: what they get.
2. Bonuses: extras that make the core feel fully valued.
3. Guarantee: what happens if it does not work.
4. Scarcity or urgency: why now. It must be real.
5. Name: what it is called.
6. Price and payment structure.

Weak offers usually lack bonuses, a guarantee, or real scarcity. Loud offers over-promise or fake the clock.

## Diagnostic loop

1. State the business type (service, course, info product, and so on).
2. Write the current offer in plain words: name, price, contents, guarantee, deadline.
3. Score the four levers 1 to 10. The lowest is the bottleneck.
4. Check which of the six parts is missing.
5. Fix one lever per round.
6. Draft the changed part.
7. Project the lift honestly. One change usually gives 10 to 40 percent. Two rounds on different levers can reach 2 to 3 times.

## Do not do

- Fake countdowns or "only 3 left" with no limit behind it.
- Guarantees that over-promise (refund plus a payout). Refund risk eats margin.
- Bonus inflation: a huge "value" on a small product with no comparable.
- Course-bro styling on a serious product.
- Discounting to win new buyers. Discount-askers leave sooner. Discount only to reward upgrades or a real seasonal window.

Banned words: game-changing, revolutionary, next-level, 10x, "secret", "limited time" with no time limit, "100% guaranteed" without conditions. Use numbers, named results, real timelines.
