# The Bench

Five seats give Chief one opinion each. No debate, no vote, no seat reads another. Siblings: boresight (SOP 1.11) reads Chief's order back; ivory tower (SOP 1.13) answers from literature. The Bench answers a decision question from five lenses.

## Steps

1. **Write the question** in one sentence. Name up to three files the seats may read. Do not rewrite it later.
2. **Launch all five in one message** (gear 2): `bench-operator`, `bench-storyteller`, `bench-money`, `bench-engineer`, `bench-skeptic`. Each brief holds the question and the file names, nothing else. Do not share any seat's answer with another.
3. **Wait** for all five.
4. **Lay them side by side** in this fixed table:

| Seat | Position | Top reason | Risk | Evidence | Confidence |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|

5. **One line of your own:** where the seats agree and where they split. Nothing more. Chief rules.

Nothing gates on the Bench. Seats are advisory and nobody grades anybody.
