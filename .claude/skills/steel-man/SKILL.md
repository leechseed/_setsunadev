# Steel-man

Standing rulings that apply:
- **Go recommended (10/12):** a plain yes takes every rec on the last offer; end offers with "say go".
- **Plain register (9/17):** short sentences, actor and verb, no jargon.

## Steps

1. **Name the target.** One sentence: the rec or decision X, and the best real alternative.
2. **Write the strongest honest case** for the other side, as an informed advocate would put it. No mockery, no weak version, no attack on motives.
3. **Keep it under 150 words, then stop.** Do not decide, do not rebuild the rec, do not give a verdict. Chief rules.

Skip it for settled facts and hard requirements; say so in one line instead of inventing a defense.
