### 3.4 O — Operations (How a Typical Week Actually Looks)

**Load Pattern:**

**Cardio / Strength-Stamina (3x/week)**

- 30-min easy jog/cycle (e.g., Tue/Thu/Fri post-gym).  
- Light bodyweight work in warm-ups: squats, planks.

**Machine / Anal / Throat (2–3x/week, 20–45 min)**

- Use **smaller toys** and **low speeds**.  
- Rough structure:

**Subspace Intro**

### 6.3 Phase 3 — Tactics

**Gym (5 days/week + 2 HIIT/pump days)**

**Flexibility (5x/week, 30 min)**

- Full splits work (front and side).

**Subspace Mastery**

## 7. Nutrition, Recovery & Tracking

### 7.1 Nutrition Phasing

- **Months 1–2 (Phase 1):**  
  - 200–400 kcal surplus.  
  - Protein: **1.8–2.2 g / lb** bodyweight.  

- **Months 3–4 (Phase 2):**  
  - High carbs peri-workout (pre/intra/post).  
  - Stay around maintenance or mild surplus if recovery is rough.

- **Months 5–6 (Phase 3):**  
  - 300–500 kcal deficit.  
  - Carb cycle: higher carbs on heavy/HIIT days.  

- **Always:**  
  - ~1 gallon water/day.  
  - Supplements: L-arginine, magnesium, omega-3s (if tolerated).

### 7.2 Recovery

- 5–10 min warm-up: light cardio + band activation.  
- Cool-down stretches post-gym and post-machine.  
- Pain rule:  
  - **DOMS:** okay, train around it.  
  - **Sharp pain / joint pain / weird nerve stuff:** back off 48h or more and, if persistent, see a doctor.

## 8. Subspace & Metrics Log

### 8.1 What to Track

- **Body:**
  - Weekly hips/ass/waist measurements.  
  - Monthly photos (front/side/back) in consistent lighting/clothes.

- **Sessions:**
  - Duration of machine/partner sessions.  
  - % of time you’d call “intense” vs warm-up/cool-down.

- **Headspace:**
  - Subspace depth 1–10 per session.  
  - Notes: what triggered highs? what caused overwhelm or panic?

### 8.2 Simple Log Format

- Date  
- Phase / Week #  
- Gym: exercises + loads (just key lifts)  
- Machine: duration, positions, toy size, intensity cycles  
- Subspace: rating /10 + 2–3 bullet notes  
- Any pain/flags
