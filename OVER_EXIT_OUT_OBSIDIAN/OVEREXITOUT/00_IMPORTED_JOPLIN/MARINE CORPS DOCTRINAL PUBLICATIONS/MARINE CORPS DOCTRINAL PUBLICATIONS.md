Marines operate in the spirit of, and are guided by, the philosophy of maneuver
warfare articulated in the eleven Marine Corps doctrinal publications

- MCDP 1 WARFIGHTING
- MCDP 1-1 STRATEGY
- MCDP 1-2 CAMPAIGNING
- MCDP 1-3 TACTICS
- MCDP 2 INTELLIGENCE
- MCDP 3 EXPEDITIONARY OPERATIONS
- MCDP 4 LOGISTICS
- MCDP 5 PLANNING
- MCDP 6 COMMAND AND CONTROL
- MCDP 7 LEARNING
- MCDP 8 INFORMATION

- 2026-09-03 — Repair: list extended from nine (through MCDP 6) to eleven (added MCDP 7 Learning, 2020, and MCDP 8 Information, 2022); two December-2025 summaries carried titles that do not exist in the series ("MCDP 4 — Security", "MCDP 8 — Logistics") and were parked as UNVERIFIED with provenance blocks, and "MCDP 4 — Logistics" and "MCDP 8 — Information" summaries were generated from the source texts (MCDP 4 Logistics 2023; MCDP 8 Information 2022).
