## 🤖 Claude  <sub>2026-05-17 23:43:32</sub>

No — deleting a chat on Instagram only removes it from your side. The other person still sees the full conversation.

What you're thinking of is "unsend," which is different. Unsending a *message* removes it for everyone. But deleting a whole *chat/conversation thread* just clears it from your inbox — the other person's copy is untouched.

So if you want something gone for both people, you have to unsend each message individually (tap and hold the message → Unsend). Note they may have already seen it, and unsending doesn't erase a screenshot.
