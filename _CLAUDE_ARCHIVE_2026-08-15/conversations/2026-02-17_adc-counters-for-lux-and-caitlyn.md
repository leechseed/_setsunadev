## 🤖 Claude  <sub>2026-02-17 10:05:34</sub>

For countering Lux + Caitlyn bot lane, you want ADCs that can either out-range/out-poke them, engage on them, or survive their combo and punish them. Here are your best options:

---

**Best Picks**

**Draven** is probably your strongest choice here. His lane dominance forces them to play scared, and if you can catch them with a stand aside + axe combo before they set up their traps and range advantage, you chunk them hard. Cait hates aggressive, bursty lanes.

**Jinx** might seem counterintuitive since she's also a scaling ADC, but her Fishbones range actually lets her poke Cait at near-equal range, and her chompers can completely neutralize Cait's net escapes. She wins the extended late-game fight too.

**Samira** is a strong pick if you have an engage support. She can dash through Lux's Q and E, and her W blocks Cait's Q. She turns their combo into a liability.

---

**Solid Secondary Options**

**Kog'Maw** outstretches Cait at full build and melts shields/tanks. Boring laning phase though — you'll need a protective support.

**Miss Fortune** with a burst/engage support (Nautilus, Leona) can all-in them before they set up. Her ult + Double Up can punish them hard under their own traps.

**Ezreal** can poke safely, dodge Lux Q/R with E, and doesn't get punished as hard by Cait's headshot passive since he stays mobile.

---

**The Support You Pair With Matters A Lot**

Lux+Cait absolutely destroys poke matchups, so avoid picking a poke support into them — they'll just out-range you. Go with either an **engage support** (Leona, Nautilus, Blitzcrank) to close the gap and punish, or a **shield/heal support** (Lulu, Karma) if you're playing a scaling ADC like Jinx or Kog'Maw who just needs to survive lane.

What rank/elo are you playing at? That might shift the recommendation a bit.

## 🤖 Claude  <sub>2026-02-17 10:14:16</sub>

Sivir is actually a **great** pick into Lux + Caitlyn, probably one of the better ones. Here's why:

**Why Sivir Works Well**

Her E (Spell Shield) is the big reason. It completely eats Lux's Q, E, and R, and Cait's Q. Every time you block one of those abilities you get mana back, which means they're essentially feeding your sustain every time they try to poke or engage on you. It completely neutralizes their combo if you time it well.

Her boomerang blade also gives her decent poke at range, so she's not totally helpless in the laning phase even though she'll still be slightly out-ranged by Cait.

**The Downsides**

Sivir does require good spell shield timing — if you're eating Lux Qs without blocking them you're going to have a rough time. It's a reactive ability so it rewards experience and game sense. Against a good Cait+Lux duo who knows to bait the shield out first, it gets harder.

She's also a teamfight/utility ADC rather than a lane bully, so you're not looking to destroy them in lane — you're looking to **survive and scale**, then dominate mid/late with her ult and team.

**Verdict**

Honestly Sivir might be the most **stress-free** counter to this lane because her E makes their whole gameplan frustrating for them. Pair her with an engage support like Leona or Nautilus and you have a solid setup.
