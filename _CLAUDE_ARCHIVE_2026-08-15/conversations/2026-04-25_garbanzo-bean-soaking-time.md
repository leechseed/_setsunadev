# Garbanzo bean soaking time

## 🤖 Claude  <sub>2026-04-25 16:27:19</sub>

8–12 hours, or overnight. Cover them with plenty of cold water (they'll roughly triple in size) and add a pinch of salt and baking soda if you want softer skins.

If you're short on time, do a quick soak: boil for 1 minute, cover, and let sit 1 hour.

## 🤖 Claude  <sub>2026-04-25 16:27:30</sub>

Yeah, past about 24 hours they start to ferment — you'll notice a sour smell, slimy water, or foam. At that point, toss them.

If you need to delay cooking, drain them after 12 hours and stick them in the fridge. They'll keep another day or two that way.
