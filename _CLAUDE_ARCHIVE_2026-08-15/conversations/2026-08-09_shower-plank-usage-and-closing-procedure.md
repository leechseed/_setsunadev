Flying J, got it. Here's the flow:

**The drink run:** Do it during the wait, not mid-shower. You join the shower queue via the Pilot app, a kiosk, or the fuel desk, then get a push notification (or overhead call) when your room is ready, along with a room number and door code. That waiting window is your shopping time. Once you're called, go claim the room — if you dawdle too long they can skip you.

**Stepping out once you're in:** The room is yours for the session — private, with toilet, sink, and towels provided. Your code usually re-opens the door during your session, but it's not guaranteed at every location, so the clean move is carry everything in with you. Worst case, the fuel desk can let you back into your assigned room. No hard timer on most rooms; ~30-45 minutes is the courtesy norm.

**Saying "I'm done":** Nothing. You just walk out. Leave the used towels in the room, door locks behind you, staff see it's vacated and reset it. No checkout, no telling anyone.

Yes — that's actually the standard playbook. Couples and co-drivers do exactly this: one person goes in first, then texts the other "ready," gets extra towels, and opens the door for them — security was never an issue. The guest can't let themselves in (the code is tied to you), so you're the door.

Two ways to play it:

- **Low-key:** you check in solo, guest hangs in the store/lounge, you text them the room number when you're in, open the door when they knock. Drivers who do this regularly say staff never say a word — at most the attendant asks if they need more towels.
- **Upfront:** just mention a travel partner at the desk. A Pilot employee confirmed you can share a shower with a friend — one at a time or together — and extra towels are free.

One caveat: some locations won't allow sharing, though most will — it's staff discretion. Keep it brief and quiet in the hallway (the walls are thin and there are cameras in the corridor, not the rooms) and nobody's going to bother you.
