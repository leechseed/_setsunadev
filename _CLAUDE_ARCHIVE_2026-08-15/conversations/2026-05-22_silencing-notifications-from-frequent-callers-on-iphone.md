 I'm settling on the silent ringtone approach as the clearest solution—it's straightforward to set up in the contact card and works without needing to toggle anything on or off, whereas a Focus mode would require manual activation each time.Two ways, easiest first:

**Silent ringtone (always on, per contact)**
Open the Contacts app, find them, tap Edit, then set both **Ringtone** and **Text Tone** to whatever silent option you have (you may need to download/import a silent ringtone since iOS doesn't include one by default — search "silent ringtone" in the App Store or grab a free .m4r file). Their calls and texts come through silently, no vibration. They have no idea.

**Custom Focus (toggle on/off)**
Settings → Focus → tap **+** → Custom. Under People, choose **Silence Notifications From** and add them. Then turn that Focus on from Control Center whenever you want them muted. Downside: you have to remember to enable it.

Quick mute for just their texts: open the Messages thread → tap their name at the top → toggle **Hide Alerts**.
