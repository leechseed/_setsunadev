Here's what actually returns real info without the runaround:

**Free and actually works:**
- **NumLookup** — best free option, no signup. Pulls carrier, line type, and often the owner's name straight from carrier records.
- **ZLOOKUP** — similar to NumLookup, decent for cell numbers including Google Voice/VoIP that other sites miss.
- **Spy Dialer** — uses voicemail recordings to confirm owner names. Genuinely free, no membership wall.
- **Truecaller** — huge crowdsourced database, great for spam ID. Best on mobile app.

**Paid but worth it for deeper info:**
- **BeenVerified** or **TruthFinder** — full background reports (addresses, relatives, social profiles). ~$20-30/mo, useful if you only need it for a month then cancel.

**For OSINT-style digging:** the free sites give you a name and carrier — from there, pivot to manual searching (Facebook search by phone, Google the number in quotes, check breach databases like HIBP or Dehashed). That combo usually gets further than any single lookup site.

Avoid Whitepages premium teasers and anything that asks for your email before showing results — those are mostly upsell traps.
