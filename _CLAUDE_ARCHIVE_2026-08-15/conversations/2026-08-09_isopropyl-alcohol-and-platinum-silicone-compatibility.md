# Isopropyl alcohol and platinum silicone compatibility

> **Conversation Overview**

The person asked a focused technical question about whether isopropyl alcohol damages platinum-cure silicone. Claude confirmed that IPA is safe for cured platinum silicone and is actually a standard cleaner for it, noting that prolonged soaking may cause temporary swelling but that the material fully recovers once the alcohol evaporates. Claude also clarified that IPA's reputation for damaging soft materials stems from its effect on TPE/TPR blends, not genuine platinum silicone.

## 🤖 Claude  <sub>2026-08-09 19:50:04</sub>

No — cured platinum-cure silicone is highly chemically resistant, and IPA is actually one of the standard cleaners for it. Wiping it down with 70–99% isopropyl is fine and won't degrade it.

Two caveats: prolonged soaking can cause temporary swelling and softening, but it fully recovers once the alcohol evaporates. And this is silicone-specific — IPA *does* break down TPE/TPR blends over time, which is where the "alcohol ruins soft materials" reputation comes from. If it's genuine platinum silicone, you're good.
