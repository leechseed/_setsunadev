**06 · THE NUMBERS — research base**

- CBEM (Judi's House): ~1 in 12–13 US kids bereaved of a parent/sibling by 18 — and CBEM exists because *no government agency counts bereaved children*. "Untracked" is near-literal.
- Hillis et al.: 200,000+ US kids lost a parent/caregiver to COVID. *Hidden Pain* report (COVID Collaborative) = the invisibility argument, in print.
- JAMA Psychiatry 2024: ~321,000 kids lost a parent to overdose, 2011–2021.
- Prolonged Grief Disorder entered the DSM in 2022; ~1 in 10 get stuck, worse for sudden/stigmatized deaths. Overdose grief has a literature name: **disenfranchised grief**.

**07 · TRAJECTORY — stunted is not destiny**

The resilience literature's core finding is almost embarrassingly cheap: most bereaved kids recover if **one stable adult** holds. Working interventions cost little — peer grief groups, camps, school programs, mentoring. The cruelty is distributional: fentanyl and COVID deaths clustered exactly where buffers are thinnest, so the unbuffered path dominates your sample. The bind: the most visible ladder up (OF) is the one you're shamed for climbing, by proxies of the net that never showed. And the sharpest mechanism — forward-imagination failure. The last known-good version of life sits *behind* them, on the other side of a death. Success has no lived referent. That's the RIP bio in one sentence.

**08 · HISTORICAL PARALLELS — the catch thesis**

Lost Generation (WWI + 1918 flu): art came up before the people did. Depression cohort (Elder's *Children of the Great Depression*): responsibility bred competence, but the real come-up was institutional — the GI Bill, the biggest catch-net ever built. Closest rhyme: the crack era — concentrated parental death/addiction/incarceration, grandmother-raised cohort, moral panic blaming the kids — and instead of a GI Bill it got mass incarceration. Its come-up was cultural: hip-hop. The grief of that exact cohort became the dominant art form on earth. **The wound doesn't determine the outcome. The catch does.** There is currently no GI Bill for fentanyl orphans — which is why it reads invisible.
