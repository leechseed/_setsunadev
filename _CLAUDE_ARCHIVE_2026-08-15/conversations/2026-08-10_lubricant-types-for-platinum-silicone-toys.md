## 🤖 Claude  <sub>2026-08-10 03:21:17</sub>

Water-based is the safe default — works with any silicone, no risk, just reapply as needed since it dries faster.

Silicone lube is the one people warn about, but that's mostly for cheap blended toys. True platinum-cured silicone is dense and non-porous enough that it usually holds up fine. If you want to run silicone lube (better for anal, lasts way longer), do a patch test first: drop of lube on the base of the toy, wait 15 minutes, wipe. If the surface stays smooth, you're good. If it gets tacky or gummy, that toy stays water-based only.

Hybrids (water-based with a little silicone mixed in) are a solid middle ground — longer-lasting than pure water-based, negligible risk to the toy.

Skip oil-based mainly because it's a pain to clean off silicone and wrecks condoms if those are in the picture.
