Common ingredients: L-citrulline (better than L-arginine, converts to it in the kidneys), beetroot extract, pine bark.

Libido/horniness is a separate system — hormones and neurotransmitters, mostly testosterone and dopamine. The supplements with actual evidence:

- **Tongkat Ali** — the most researched one. Raises free T by freeing it from SHBG, lowers cortisol, bumps libido. 200–400mg/day of a 200:1 extract is the standard clinical dose. Indonesian-sourced is usually the benchmark.
- **Fadogia Agrestis** — Huberman popularized this. Animal studies show T bumps; human data is thin. Usually paired with Tongkat. Most people cycle it (8 weeks on, 2–4 off).
- **Zinc + Boron** — actual hormonal cofactors. Zinc deficiency tanks T. Boron raises free T.
- **Ashwagandha (KSM-66)** — crushes cortisol, which indirectly helps T and drive. Also just makes you feel better.
- **Maca** — the one supplement with decent libido evidence that doesn't go through testosterone. Works on desire directly. Black maca is the libido-specific variant.
- **Horny Goat Weed (icariin)** — PDE5 inhibitor like a mild Viagra. More erection than desire.

For all-in-ones, the popular stacks right now are Momentous (Tongkat + separate Fadogia), Huge Supplements Enhance, Force Factor Test X180, and NatureBell's combo cap. If you want one bottle, look for Tongkat 400mg + Fadogia 600mg + zinc + boron + ashwagandha. That's the core stack.

Real talk though — if your libido feels low, sleep, body fat %, and stress hit harder than any supplement. And if something feels clinically off (persistent low drive, ED), a blood panel for total/free T, estradiol, and SHBG is worth way more than guessing with pills.
