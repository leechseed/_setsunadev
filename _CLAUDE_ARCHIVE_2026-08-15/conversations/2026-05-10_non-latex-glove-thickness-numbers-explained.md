Higher = thicker and more durable, but less dexterity.

Quick guide:
- **3 mil** — light food prep, quick tasks
- **4–5 mil** — standard kitchen/general use
- **6 mil** — heavy prep, cleaning, handling raw proteins
- **8 mil+** — chemical/dishpit work

Other numbers you might see: **AQL** (Acceptable Quality Level — pinhole defect rate, lower is better; 1.5 is medical-grade, 2.5–4.0 is industrial/food-service), and the **box count** (usually 100 per box, 1000 per case).
