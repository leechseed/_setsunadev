The user is asking about using Bar Keepers Friend on rusted quarter hotel pans—standard stainless steel kitchen equipment. This is a straightforward food service question that doesn't require research, just product knowledge about how this cleaner works on rust.

Bar Keepers Friend contains oxalic acid, which is really effective at removing rust from stainless steel, so yes, it'll work well for this job.Yeah, Bar Keepers Friend is actually one of the best things for that. The oxalic acid in it eats rust off stainless steel without scratching the way a heavy abrasive would.

Wet the pan, sprinkle the powder on, let it sit 30–60 seconds (don't let it dry out — make a paste if needed), then scrub with a non-scratch pad or the soft side of a sponge. Rinse thoroughly and dry immediately, since leftover BKF residue or water sitting on stainless can actually cause more pitting/rust spots over time.

If the rust is really set in, a second pass usually clears it. For deep pitting it'll clean the surface but the pits stay — that's just damage to the steel itself.
