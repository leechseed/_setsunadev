Classic CS2 gambling question. Let me give honest odds-based advice.Classic dilemma. Here's the honest math:

**Buying the knife directly**
- You get the knife. Guaranteed. Done.
- $450 spent, $450 of value in your inventory.

**Opening cases**
- Most cases cost ~$2.50 each + the case key (~$2.50) = ~$5 per open
- $450 = roughly **90 case opens**
- Knife drop rate is approximately **0.26%** per case
- 90 opens gives you about a **21% chance** of getting *any* knife
- That knife could be a $60 knife or a $2,000 knife — it's a lottery on top of a lottery
- Expected value on case opens is almost always **negative** (Valve and the market take their cut)

**The real math:**
On average you'd need to open ~385 cases to statistically expect one knife — that's **~$1,900** in expected spend for what you could buy outright for $450.

---

**Bottom line:** If you want *that specific knife*, buy it. Cases are entertainment spending, not investment spending. The dopamine hit of unboxing is the product — not the knife.

The only scenario where cases make sense is if you genuinely don't care what knife you get, you're okay walking away with nothing, and you're treating it as gambling money you've mentally already written off.

The user is asking about case strategy for CS2. Let me give practical advice.If you're committed to opening cases, **variety is better** — here's why:

**Variety of cases**
- Spreads your "bad luck" across multiple knife pools
- Different cases have different knife lineups, so you're not locked into one aesthetic
- Some cases have better knife-to-skin value ratios
- More interesting unboxing experience across 90 opens

**One case type**
- Only makes sense if you specifically want a knife *from that case's pool* (e.g., you only want a Butterfly or a Talon)
- Slightly easier to track/understand what you're working with

---

**Practical rec if you're going variety:**

Focus on cases where the knife pool is strong relative to case price. Right now the best bang-for-buck cases tend to be:

- **Fracture Case** — Butterfly knife pool, relatively cheap case
- **Clutch Case** — Shadow Daggers / Falchion but also has Huntsman/Butterfly
- **Horizon Case** — Talon, Navaja, Stiletto, Ursus knives
- **Prisma / Prisma 2** — Good knife pool, usually cheap cases

Avoid older expensive cases where the case itself costs $5–10+ before the key — that eats your budget fast.

---

**Honest reminder though:** 90 opens of mixed cases still gives you roughly a **1-in-5 shot** at any knife. Variety just makes the loss more fun if it doesn't hit.
