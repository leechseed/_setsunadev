---

**THE CHEEKY — Cucumber Sour Cream Salad**
*(Full hotel pan yield)*

---

**CUCUMBER PREP**

1. Fill a half hotel pan with cucumbers to the brim, stack 2–3 more on top to form a pyramid. Count the total.

2. Trim ½–1 inch off both ends of every cucumber. Be generous — there's a bitter part near the tip you want to clear.

3. Quarter each cucumber so pieces fit the food processor feed tube. Each full batch = 4 cucumbers / 16 pieces. Process all cucumbers in batches.

4. Dump shredded cucumber into a slotted half hotel pan sitting inside a solid half hotel pan. Press and squeeze out as much juice as possible. When the solid pan is about half full, empty it, return the slotted pan, and keep squeezing.

---

**SOUR CREAM BASE**

5. Flip each of the 4 sour cream buckets upside down over a full hotel pan. Stab through the bottom to break the vacuum, then peel off the film. Each bucket drops out in one piece. Repeat all 4.

---

**SEASONING & ASSEMBLY**

*Gather first: garlic powder, salt, pepper, red wine vinegar, normal olive oil, good olive oil, minced garlic.*

6. Drain half a container of minced garlic through a handheld strainer into a sixth pan. Let it drain fully.

7. Spread the squeezed cucumber evenly across the sour cream. Don't worry about clumps yet.

8. **Garlic powder** — large-hole side of shaker. Coat the entire surface in a thin generous layer.
   **Salt** — sprinkle in a zigzag pattern at a shallow angle. Solid amount, less than the garlic powder.
   **Pepper** — one line straight down the center, about the width of a ladle handle. Comes out fast, be careful.

9. **Red wine vinegar** — steady stream (not a full pour), sweep front to back for ~7 seconds. Roughly 1.5–2 cups. *(To be standardized.)*
   **Normal olive oil** — same sweeping motion, ~3 seconds.
   **Good olive oil** — same, ~3 seconds.
   **Minced garlic** — sprinkle evenly across the surface like you did the cucumber.

---

**MIXING & TASTING**

10. Mix with two ladles first to break clumps, then switch to one. Sweep all four corners — liquid pools there. Get the bottom of the pan too.

11. Taste using the bottom of the spoon laid flat on your tongue. You're looking for three layers:
    - BOTTOM: sour cream depth + oil. Rich, milky, hits the back of the tongue.
    - MIDDLE: cucumber body + subtle garlic.
    - TOP: vinegar brightness lifting everything.

12. Adjust if needed — flat = more salt, dull = more vinegar. Max 3 tasting rounds. Don't go beyond that.

---

**PORTIONING & STORAGE**

13. Ladle into sixth pans. Scrape all edges and corners clean.

14. Keep sour cream cold at all times. If stepping away, fridge it. Warm sour cream breaks and separates and won't reconstitute. Work relatively fast once cucumber is added.
