You're probably thinking of the **half-your-age-plus-seven** rule:

- **Minimum age you can date** = (your age ÷ 2) + 7
- **Maximum age** = (your age − 7) × 2

So at 30: floor of 22, ceiling of 46. At 40: 27 to 66.

One correction though — I don't think this is a South Park thing. It's an old folk rule that got popularized online by xkcd #314 "Dating Pools," which framed it as the "standard creepiness rule", and it also shows up in Parks and Recreation (season 2, "The Master Plan"). It may be getting attributed to South Park via memes, but I can't find an actual episode using it.

Worth noting it's a joke heuristic, not advice — it breaks down badly at the young end (it'd sanction a 22-year-old dating an 18-year-old) and gets absurdly permissive at the old end.
