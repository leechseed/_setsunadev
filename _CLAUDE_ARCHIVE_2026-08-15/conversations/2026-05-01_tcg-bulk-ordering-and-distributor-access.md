 I'm organizing my response around the distribution structure for trading card games—how products flow from publishers through authorized distributors to retailers—and then applying that framework to Riftbound specifically, noting which distributors Riot partners with for that product line.Yeah, so the TCG industry runs on a strict 3-tier system: **Publisher → Distributor → Retailer**. Stores almost never get product directly from the manufacturer.

**How it works for Riftbound specifically:**
Riot Games partnered with UVS Games (Universus) for global distribution. UVS sells to authorized hobby distributors — names like GTS Distribution, Southern Hobby Supply, ACD Distribution, Alliance, PHD — and those distributors are who your local game store actually orders booster boxes from. Stores submit pre-orders months ahead of release (often 4–6 months out), and allocation gets cut based on how big/established they are.

**The gatekeeping is real.** Money alone doesn't unlock it. To open an account with a distributor like GTS, you need:

- A registered business (LLC or sole prop)
- EIN / federal tax ID
- State sales tax / reseller certificate
- A real storefront — many distributors ask for photos of your signage, lease, etc. Online-only is harder.
- Sometimes a minimum opening order ($1K–$5K+) and net-30 credit checks
- For some games, tournament hosting requirements (WotC requires this for MTG; Pokémon rewards it with allocation)

The whole point of the requirements is to filter out flippers. Distributors get pressure from publishers to keep product out of scalper hands, so a guy with $50K in cash and no business gets denied — they want hobby shops actively growing the player base.

**Can you get in if you have the money? Yes, but you have to actually build the business.** The realistic path:

1. Form an LLC, get EIN, get FL sales tax permit (you're in [CITY] — easy through FL DOR)
2. Lease even a small commercial space — could be a shared retail spot or 200 sqft suite. This is the part most people skip and it kills the application.
3. Apply to 2–3 distributors (GTS, Southern Hobby, ACD are the big three for TCG)
4. For Riftbound specifically, also register as a store on the Riftbound Gaming Network for organized play access

**The shortcuts people actually use:**

- **Buying from distributors' clearance/secondary channels** — some stores resell allocation they couldn't move
- **Liquidation pallets** (B-Stock, Liquidation.com) — spotty for new TCG product
- **Buddy up with an existing store** — some shop owners will let you piggyback on their account for a cut, or co-invest in cases
- **Direct from Riot's preorder windows** — limited per Riot ID, but it's the only "consumer" path to sealed at MSRP for Riftbound

Wholesale margin on TCG sealed is thinner than people think — booster boxes typically wholesale at 65–75% of MSRP. That's why stores survive on singles, supplies, and events, not box flipping.
