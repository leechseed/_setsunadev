Without that license, selling prepared food anywhere in Florida is illegal, regardless of whether you're serving on public or private property. It runs about $347 for a full-year license or $178.50 for a half-year, paid to the DBPR district office. There's a plan review (you submit a floor-plan drawing of your cart) and then an in-person health inspection before they hand you the license.

Here's the part that changes your whole location question: in 2020, **HB 1193 preempted mobile-food-vendor regulation to the state.** A municipality, county, or other local governmental entity may not require a separate license, registration, or permit other than the state license, or charge any local vending fee. Translation: [CITY] and Leon County legally *cannot* make you buy a city hot-dog-cart permit or charge you a separate vending fee, and they can't ban mobile vendors from the city entirely.
