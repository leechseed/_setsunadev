"Sierra" splits a few ways:

**Etymology:** Spanish for "saw," extended to mean a jagged mountain range — that root drives most uses below.

**Geography:** Sierra Nevada (California/Spain), Sierra Madre (Mexico), Sierra Leone (the country), plus Sierra Counties in California and New Mexico.

**Given name:** Feminine first name, peaked in US popularity in the late '90s. Variants: Ciara, Cierra, Siera.

**NATO phonetic alphabet:** The letter S.

**Vehicles:** GMC Sierra (pickup), Ford Sierra ('80s–'90s European sedan).

**Tech:** macOS Sierra (10.12) and High Sierra (10.13); Sierra On-Line, the classic adventure game studio (King's Quest, Leisure Suit Larry); Sierra Wireless; Sierra Space.

**Brands/orgs:** Sierra Club (environmental), Sierra Nevada Brewing, Sierra Mist (the soda, rebranded as Starry), Sierra (TJX outdoor retailer), Sierra Bullets (ammunition).

**Nature:** A sierra is also a type of Spanish mackerel.
