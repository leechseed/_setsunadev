Yes, but with a couple catches:

Returns without a receipt are subject to store manager approval, and you'll get a Walgreens gift card instead of cash back — usually credited at the item's lowest recent sale price, not what you paid. You'll need a valid photo ID.

Two tips before you go: if you paid with a card or used your MyWalgreens account, the store can often look up your purchase and treat it like you have a receipt. And note some items can't be returned at all — gift cards, phone cards, sexual wellness items, and seasonal products among them.
