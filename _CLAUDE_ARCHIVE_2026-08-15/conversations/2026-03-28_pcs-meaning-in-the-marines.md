**PCS** stands for **Permanent Change of Station** — it's when a service member is officially ordered to relocate from one duty station to another.

In the Marines (and military broadly), a PCS move means you're not just temporarily deployed; your official assignment and home base are changing. It typically involves moving your household, updating your records, and reporting to a new command.

Common related terms: PCS orders, PCS move, OCONUS PCS (overseas move).
