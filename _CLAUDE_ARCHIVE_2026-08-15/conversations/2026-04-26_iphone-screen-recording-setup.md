The user wants to know how to screen record on an iPhone 17 Pro, which is straightforward since this is a built-in iOS feature that's worked the same way for years. I'll keep my answer brief and direct with simple steps since they're on mobile.Use the built-in screen recorder in Control Center:

1. **Add it to Control Center** (one-time setup): Settings → Control Center → tap the **+** next to *Screen Recording*.
2. **Open Control Center**: swipe down from the top-right corner.
3. **Tap the record button** (circle inside a circle). You get a 3-second countdown, then it records everything on screen.
4. **Stop**: tap the red time indicator in the top-left, then *Stop*. Or reopen Control Center and tap the record button again.

The video saves to Photos automatically.

Tip: long-press the record button before starting if you want to record your **mic audio** too (otherwise it only captures app sound).
