Their store-brand "Spring Valley" ashwagandha sometimes uses generic ashwagandha extract instead of KSM-66 or Sensoril. Check the label — if it just says "ashwagandha root powder" without naming the standardized extract, the potency is unpredictable. The KSM-66 version is the one to grab.

If you're already out and don't want to make another stop, Walgreens often runs "buy 1 get 1 50% off" deals on their own brand supplements — worth checking before you check out. But honestly, for $10 more on the bottle, just hit Walmart tomorrow or order from Amazon tonight and you'll save half.
