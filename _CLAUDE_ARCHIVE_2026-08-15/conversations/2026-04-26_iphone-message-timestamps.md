# iPhone message timestamps

> **Conversation Overview**

The person asked a brief technical question about how to view timestamps on text messages in the iPhone Messages app. Claude explained that timestamps appear automatically at intervals, and that to see the exact time for any individual message, the user can swipe left on a message to reveal timestamps sliding in from the right side of the screen, then release to hide them.

## 🤖 Claude  <sub>2026-04-26 18:28:08</sub>

On iPhone, timestamps in Messages show automatically but only at intervals. To see the exact time for any individual message, swipe left on the message with your finger — the timestamps will slide in from the right side of the screen. Release to hide them.
