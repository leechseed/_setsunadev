I'll keep my response concise and mobile-friendly.Yes — use a **photo widget**. Two options:

**Built-in Photos widget (easiest):**
1. Long-press an empty spot on the home screen until icons jiggle
2. Tap the **+** in the top-left
3. Search "Photos" and add the widget
4. Tap the widget while still in jiggle mode → **Edit Widget** → pick a specific album

The catch: it cycles through whatever's in the album. You can't lock it to one image.

**For a single, specific picture:**
Use a free app like **Widgetsmith** or **Photo Widget**. Install it, pick your photo inside the app, then add *that app's* widget to your home screen the same way as above. Tip: make a dedicated album in Photos first (e.g. "Home Screen") so it's easy to manage.
