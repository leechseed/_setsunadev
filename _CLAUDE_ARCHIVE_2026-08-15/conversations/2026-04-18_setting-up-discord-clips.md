- Clips has to be enabled *before* you start the stream you want to capture from — you can't retroactively flip it on mid-stream and grab a moment that already happened.
- There's also a per-stream Enable Clips toggle in the stream settings popup when you start screen sharing, if you'd rather not have it running constantly.
