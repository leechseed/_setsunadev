## 🤖 Claude  <sub>2026-08-10 13:46:59</sub>

You get it back — it's not a charge. Hotels put a pre-authorization hold on your card at check-in (room cost plus a deposit for incidentals) just to make sure the funds are there.

When you check out, they charge the actual final amount and release the hold. Heads up though: the hold doesn't vanish instantly — your bank can take 3–7 business days to free it up, longer if it's a debit card. It'll just drop off your pending transactions rather than show as a refund.

As long as you don't trash the room or raid a minibar, the only real charge is your room total.

## 🤖 Claude  <sub>2026-08-10 13:47:50</sub>

In your bank or card app:

- Open the app and go to your account activity/transactions
- Look for a **"Pending"** section at the top — holds sit there, separate from posted charges
- It'll show as Best Western (or sometimes the hotel's location name) with the hold amount

If your app doesn't split out pending, the hold might just be baked into your "available balance" being lower than your actual balance. That gap is the hold.

Once it releases after checkout, it just disappears from pending — no refund line will show up.

## 🤖 Claude  <sub>2026-08-10 13:50:10</sub>

Pretty much, yeah — with one caveat. That $243 hold is likely your room total *plus* an incidentals deposit (usually $50–100 extra). So the actual charge at checkout might be *less* than $243.

Right now nothing has actually left your account — the hold just reserves that money so you can't spend it. At checkout, the real charge posts (room + tax + anything you bought), and the $243 hold releases a few days later.

If you want to know exactly what your room will cost, check your booking confirmation or ask the front desk — they can tell you how much of that hold is deposit vs. room.

## 🤖 Claude  <sub>2026-08-10 13:50:54</sub>

Tell the front desk before you go — that's the key move. If you just walk out, they'll charge you for both nights no question.

What happens next depends on your rate:

- **Flexible/standard rate:** Most hotels will adjust to one night if you tell them you're checking out early, though some charge an early departure fee (often one night's rate anyway — ask)
- **Prepaid/non-refundable rate:** You're probably paying for both nights regardless, since that's the deal you booked

Best Western is franchised, so it's really up to that specific property. Go to the desk, say your plans changed, and ask if they can adjust the bill to one night. Being polite and asking directly works more often than you'd think — worst case they say no and you're out what you already committed to.
