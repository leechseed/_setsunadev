### How the 7-day Trade Protection actually breaks "flip ASAP"
Timeline if user buys today (May 22) on a third-party marketplace:
1. **Day 0 (May 22):** User pays $468.78, marketplace bot/seller sends the glove via Steam trade. Item arrives instantly but enters a **7-day Trade Protection window** with yellow shield icon. Cannot be re-listed, traded, or modified during this period.
2. **Day 0–7:** Item is "locked." User can equip it in-game but cannot sell it on any P2P marketplace because it cannot be traded out. (Some bot-based marketplaces will let you list, but the actual trade to the buyer can't execute until day 7+.)
3. **Day 7 (~May 29):** Protection expires. User can now list/sell. If listed at floor (~$560), it might sell same day; at $968+ gross, it will sit indefinitely.
4. **Day 7 + buyer find + 7-day escrow on seller side (~day 14–21):** Funds clear on the marketplace and can be withdrawn to bank.
5. **Day 14–21:** Bank transfer arrives (SEPA/Stripe typically 1–3 business days).

**Realistic best-case cash-in-bank: ~14 days. Realistic at $500-profit target price: never, at current market.**
