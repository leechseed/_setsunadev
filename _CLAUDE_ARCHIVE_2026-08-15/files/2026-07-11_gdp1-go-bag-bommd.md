Three structural notes. First, the spend is naturally back-loaded — day one costs about a third of the total, and nothing else is due until graduation criteria unlock it, so the program gates the wallet the same way it gates the reps. Second, the tripod, remote, and lighting overlap with the content operation's equipment list — one purchase serves both ledgers. Third, the girth ladder and destination tier are deliberately excluded from the build total: those are earned line items, and pre-buying them just puts a deadline on tissue that doesn't negotiate.
