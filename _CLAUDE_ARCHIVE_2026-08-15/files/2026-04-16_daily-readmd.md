## What's Actually Happening

She is not broken. She is *adapted.*
Every wall she has, she built for a reason.
Every test she runs, she ran it to survive.
She is not trying to hurt you.
She is trying to find out if you're real.

Your job today is simple:
**Be real. Be steady. Be boring. Be there.**

Not perfect. Not smooth. Not healed.
Just there. Same as yesterday. Same as tomorrow.

You are allowed to love someone.

The dream is real.

## When She Tests You Today

She might go cold.
She might disappear.
She might make you jealous.
She might say nothing when you expected something.
She might say too much when you expected nothing.

When that happens — breathe first.

Ask yourself:
*Is this about me, or is this her nervous system doing what it was built to do?*

Then respond from the calm version of you.
Not the wrecked version. Not the scared version.
The steady one.

**One line when things get hard:**
*"I'm not going anywhere. Take the time you need."*

Say it. Mean it. Then give her the space.
