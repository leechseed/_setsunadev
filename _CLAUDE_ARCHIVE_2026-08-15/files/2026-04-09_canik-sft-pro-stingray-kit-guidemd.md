## 2. How Many Mags to Have

The gun comes with 2 factory mags. That is not enough.

**Defensive Pistol Course minimum:** 3–4 mags. Most courses burn 500–1,000 rounds in a day and require you to reload constantly. Running with only 2 mags means you are reloading from the ground constantly instead of from pouches — which slows you and breaks training flow.

**USPSA/IDPA competition minimum:** 4–6 mags. Most USPSA stages require 2–4 reloads. IDPA is similar. You need loaded mags staged and ready to go.

**Recommendation:** Buy 4 additional factory Canik mags (bringing your total to 6).

| Item | Count | Unit Cost | Subtotal |
|------|-------|-----------|----------|
| Canik factory 18rd mag | 2 | ~$35 | $70 |
| Canik factory 20rd mag | 2 | ~$40 | $80 |
| **Mag Total** | **+4 mags / 6 total** | | **~$150** |

> Buy OEM Canik mags first. ETS and MecGar make reliable aftermarket alternatives if OEM is out of stock.

## 3. Optic

The SFT Pro Stingray comes optics-ready with RMRcc footprint and two adapter plates. Do not skip an optic — especially for competition. Red dots are now standard in USPSA Carry Optics division and IDPA CO division, both of which this gun qualifies for.

**Recommended:**

**Holosun 507C X2** — ~$250–$280
- 2 MOA dot + 32 MOA circle option
- Solar failsafe + battery on top (no slide removal to swap battery)
- RMRcc adapter plate is included or ~$20 extra
- Proven competition optic at this price point

**Budget option:** Holosun 507K X2 (~$220) — slightly smaller footprint, still solid.

**Step-up option:** Trijicon RMR Type 2 (~$450–$500) — if you want to spend up, this is the gold standard for durability.

> **Install note:** The SFT Pro takes an RMRcc footprint. Order the correct mounting plate for your optic if it doesn't come with one. Torque screws to spec and use blue Loctite. Check zero every 200–300 rounds initially.

## 9. Ammo

**For a defensive pistol course:** Plan for 500–1,000 rounds per day of instruction. Most two-day courses burn 1,000–1,500 total. Verify with the specific course before purchasing.

**Practice/training ammo:**

Use **steel case 9mm** (Blazer Brass, Winchester White Box, Federal Aluminum, or Tulammo) for range practice — ~$0.20–$0.27/round.

Buy **500–1,000 rounds minimum** before training.

**Recommended pre-training purchase:**

## 10. What to Read

### Books (Priority Order)

**1. "Practical Shooting: Beyond Fundamentals" — Brian Enos**
The single most important book for USPSA/IDPA shooters. Covers the mental game, awareness, sight pictures, and how to actually get fast without breaking down under pressure. Read this before your first competition. This is the community's bible.

**2. "Deadly Force: Understanding Your Right to Self Defense" — Massad Ayoob**
Essential for anyone taking a defensive pistol course. Covers the legal, moral, and practical framework for use of force. You will think very differently about every drill after reading this.

**3. "The Complete Book of the AR-15 / Handgun" — Patrick Sweeney (Handgun edition)**
Practical reference for understanding your pistol's mechanics, common malfunctions, and how to troubleshoot. Good for a new shooter to build mechanical literacy.

**4. "Combat Focus Shooting" — Rob Pincus**
The defensive pistol training philosophy. Pincus runs I.C.E. Training and his approach directly informs many modern defensive courses. Read before attending a course.

**5. "Thinking Fast and Slow" — Daniel Kahneman (bonus)**
Not a shooting book. But Enos references cognitive load and "flow state" heavily, and this book is the intellectual foundation for understanding how your brain processes under stress. Extremely valuable for understanding why training a certain way matters.

## 12. Pre-Training Checklist

Before you walk into your first defensive pistol course:

- [ ] Gun cleaned and lubed (field strip + bore snake + light oil on rails)
- [ ] Optic mounted, zeroed, and confirmed at 10–15 yards
- [ ] All 6 mags loaded and function-tested (run 50 rounds minimum before class)
- [ ] Holster adjusted to your draw height and retention preference
- [ ] Mag pouches positioned so you can reload without looking
- [ ] Electronic ear pro charged and working
- [ ] Eye pro packed
- [ ] 500+ rounds in the bag (plus whatever the course specifies)
- [ ] "Deadly Force" by Ayoob read (or at minimum the first 5 chapters)
- [ ] Know the four rules cold:
  1. Treat every gun as if it is loaded
  2. Never point it at anything you are not willing to destroy
  3. Keep your finger off the trigger until on target and ready to fire
  4. Know your target and what is beyond it
