## 1. Mission & Fit

Physical training infrastructure for the ULTRASIN venture. Primary function: nightly execution of [[GDP-1 BOOTYCAMP]] and [[GDP-2 CAVALRY]] programming without gym-membership friction, commute, or other people. Secondary function: physique progress tracking and content-adjacent staging (mirror station, consistent lighting).

Design philosophy: **professional kitchen line.** Every tool has a home, every session has a pre-flight and post-flight, setup and teardown under five minutes each. Friction is the enemy of cadence; cadence is the whole game.

Night-ops native: red-light task illumination, bug defense as a system (not an afterthought), noise discipline for 2 a.m. pulls.

## 4. Critical Path: Measure Before You Order

**Nothing ships until these numbers exist.** Two measurements gate the two most important purchases:

1. **Slab dimensions** — confirms zone layout fits. Target ≥10×16 ft; 12×20 is ideal.
2. **Cage upright spec** — determines which J-cups fit:
   - Tubing size: 2×2, 2×3, or 3×3 (measure the upright faces)
   - Hole diameter: 5/8" or 1" (measure an existing pin/hole)
   - Hole spacing through the bench zone (Westside ~1" spacing vs. standard 2")

**J-cup fitment rule:** buy sandwich-style J-cups matched to tubing + hole size. REP, Titan, and Bells of Steel all sell sandwich cups in 2×3/5/8", 3×3/5/8", and 3×3/1" flavors.

**Fallback:** if the cage is an oddball (through-pin hooks, non-standard holes), no sandwich cup will fit — in that case keep stock hooks, set safeties one notch below every lift as insurance, and a rack upgrade becomes a future line item. Don't force it.

## 7. Bug Defense System

Layered. Ranked by actual effectiveness. Almost everything runs on a monthly clock, not per-session.

**Monthly clock:**
1. **Source elimination (the real fix):** weekly walk — dump standing water (saucers, buckets, tarp folds, gutters). **Bti dunks** ($10) in anything undumpable. Every mosquito biting you hatched within a few hundred feet.
2. **Barrier spray:** bifenthrin concentrate + $30 pump sprayer on shaded vegetation, **underside of leaves** (daytime resting sites). Re-up every 3-4 weeks. Spray at dusk, skip anything blooming (pollinators).
3. **County assist:** Leon County mosquito control takes free service requests — if the source is a neighbor's ditch/abandoned pool, they treat it.

**Per-session:**
4. **Thermacell** at the upwind perimeter, on 15-20 min before lifting (fans blow its cloud away if placed too close).
5. **Fans = point defense:** mosquitoes max out ~1.5 mph; they cannot fly in fan wash. Crossflow from the two fans is the bubble.
6. **Skin: 20% picaridin.** Sweat-durable, non-greasy, doesn't melt plastics. Non-negotiable given the uniform.

**Graded out:** citronella/tiki torches = vibes (~14% reduction, zeroed by breeze — light for aesthetic only). Wristbands = junk. Propane CO2 traps = $400-700 + propane for mixed bite-reduction evidence; fan + Thermacell beats it per dollar.

## 8. Power & Lighting

**Lighting doctrine:** existing built-in floods = ambient scene (walkout, depth, bar path). Red headlamp = point/task light (plate loads, collars, logging). White headlamp mode only for detail work. No new fixtures needed.

**Power tiers:**
1. **Now:** 12-ga outdoor cord from nearest garage/exterior outlet + GFCI adapter. Runs both fans + charging with headroom.
2. **Optional cordless:** battery-platform fan (e.g., Ryobi 18V hybrid) if the cord annoys.
3. **Later:** electrician-run outdoor GFCI circuit to slab (§13).

Thermacell is butane — needs nothing.

## 9. Storage & Flow — Mise en Place

Everything has exactly one home. Two stations:

**Deck box (at the slab) — weatherproof residents:**
- Both fans (mesh bag stays rigged on the box fan)
- Thermacell + refills
- Pump sprayer + bifenthrin
- Collars, jump rope, misc accessories
- Beater bar can live here in a PVC tube if not left on the floor stand

**Garage wall + session bag — commuters:**
- Ohio Power Bar → vertical wall mount (in after every session, always)
- J-cups + safeties → hooks next to the bar (30-second strip)
- Bench → carried in (33 lbs) or gets a fitted waterproof cover if it stays out
- Session bag → chalk, belt, picaridin, headlamp, log, phone tripod

**Permanent outdoor residents:** cage (anchored), stall mats, plate tree + bumpers, deck box.

## 10. Session Protocol

**Pre-flight (~4 min):**
1. Thermacell on at perimeter (first thing — needs 15 min lead)
2. Floods on
3. Grab bar + J-cups + bench + bag from garage, walk out
4. Cups on, fans on, picaridin on
5. Lift

**Post-flight (~4 min):**
1. Bar wiped, cups stripped → garage wall
2. Bench in (or covered)
3. Fans + Thermacell → deck box
4. Floods off
5. Done

**Noise discipline (2 a.m. rule):** pulls happen on the double-stacked mats; lower under control, don't drop. The crack is what wakes neighbors; the thump doesn't carry far.
