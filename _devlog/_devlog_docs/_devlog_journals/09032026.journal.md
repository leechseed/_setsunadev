# 09/03/2026 — the ancients

Sit rep came up on an empty rack. The 9/1 session had gone out without writing itself down, so the first thing this session did was write it down from the commits, then flush it to the magazine where it belonged.

Papi pointed at the oldest tier of the board — the anime-girl build lineage, the theory vault, the influence system, the one-offs — and asked for the ruling sheet. The sheet turned up two things nobody was looking for. One was a personal ad sitting in the public tree with a neighborhood in it; Papi looked at it and said leave it, all of it, and that was that. The other was the better find: roughly a hundred and ten finished narratology distills — Booth, Genette, Greimas, Propp, Bakhtin, the whole 2024 curriculum, tiered and indexed — sitting under an "archived, don't touch" prefix while the spine sweep scored the craft shelf at half a percent. The house had already done the work and then filed it where it couldn't see it.

So: promote, then park. The curriculum got a shelf in the SSOT. Three essays got catalog numbers. Two film lists two years apart became one register with a forty-title stable core. Ten moon societies and a Civ table turned out to be the same seed for an IP that isn't The Outliers. GUTS99 was retired with honors — ten empty stubs since July, but the twelve coinages live on. Then seventeen folders went into one archive folder with one index, and nothing was deleted.

The stall pattern, again, in a different costume: not a decision left unanswered this time, but finished work left unaddressed. Same cure. Write down where it is.

Out.
