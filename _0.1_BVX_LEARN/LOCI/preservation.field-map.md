# FOOD PRESERVATION — THE FIELD MAP

**The whole field is six levers.** Every method takes one or more of these away from the microbes: **water** · **oxygen** · **warmth** · a **neutral pH** · **available sugar/salt balance** · **an empty niche** (fill it with a friendly microbe first). Smoke and nitrite add chemistry on top. Heat-and-seal kills and then locks the door. Once you can name the lever, every food in the world files itself.

## The tree

| Lever | Method | What it makes | The cuisines that live here |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Water out** | air-dry · sun-dry · dehydrate · freeze-dry | jerky, biltong, stockfish, dried fruit, dried mushrooms, papad, chuño (Andean freeze-dried potato, the oldest freeze-drying), pemmican | Nordic (stockfish), Southern African (biltong), Andean (chuño, charqui), Italian (porcini), Indian (papad), Arctic |
| **Salt** | dry-cure · brine · salt-pack | prosciutto, country ham, gravlax, salt cod, anchovies, olives, feta, umeboshi (salt + acid), lakerda, pastırma | Iberian (jamón, bacalhau), Italian (salumi), Nordic (gravlax), Turkish (pastırma, lakerda), Japanese (umeboshi), Greek (feta, olives) |
| **Sugar** | jam · candying · fruit paste | preserves, marmalade, membrillo, guava paste, candied peel, mostarda, Turkish fig and rose jams | Iberian (membrillo), Latin (guava paste), British (marmalade), Balkan/Turkish (slatko, reçel) |
| **Acid** | vinegar pickle · escabeche · chutney | cornichons, pickled onions, escabeche mussels, atchara, chutneys | French, Spanish (escabeche), Filipino (atchara), Indian (chutney), British |
| **Empty niche — lacto** | salt-brine fermentation | sauerkraut, kimchi, half-sours, turšija/torshi, curtido, makdous, pickled turnips, sour cabbage leaves for sarma | German, Korean, Jewish deli, Balkan/Persian (torshi), Salvadoran (curtido), Levantine |
| **Empty niche — alcohol/acetic** | yeast then acetobacter | kvass, boza, cider, wine, beer, mead, vinegar | Russian/Ukrainian (kvass), Balkan/Turkish (boza), everywhere (vinegar) |
| **Empty niche — mold/koji** | Aspergillus, Rhizopus, Bacillus | miso, soy sauce, tamari, sake, tempeh, natto, fermented tofu, doubanjiang, gochujang | Japanese, Chinese, Korean, Indonesian (tempeh) |
| **Empty niche — fish** | salt + autolysis | fish sauce, colatura, garum, jeotgal, bagoong, shrimp paste, surströmming, rakfisk, hákarl, narezushi | Vietnamese/Thai, Italian (colatura), Roman (garum), Korean, Filipino, Nordic, Japanese |
| **Empty niche — dairy** | culture | yogurt, kefir, labneh, kaymak, cheese (all of it), skyr, crème fraîche | Turkish/Balkan/Caucasus (yogurt, kaymak, ayran), Levant (labneh), Nordic (skyr), everywhere (cheese) |
| **Smoke** | cold smoke (< 30 °C) · hot smoke | lox, speck, smoked salmon, kippers, katsuobushi, smoked sausage, chipotle (smoked jalapeño), lapsang tea | Jewish deli, Tyrol, Scottish, Japanese (katsuobushi), Mexican (chipotle), Polish/German |
| **Oxygen out — fat** | confit · rillettes · potted · oil-pack · ghee | duck confit, rillettes, potted shrimp, tuna in oil, sott'olio, ghee, niter kibbeh, schmaltz | French, British, Italian, Ethiopian, Jewish |
| **Heat then seal** | water-bath canning (high-acid) · pressure canning (low-acid, ≥ 240 °F) · retort pouch · conserva/tinning | jams, tomatoes, pickles (jar); beans, fish, meat (pressure); sardines, mackerel, tuna, mussels, cockles, pâté, Spam, corned beef (tin); space food (pouch) | Portuguese/Spanish/Galician conservas, American canning culture, Breton (pâté Hénaff), NASA |
| **Cold** | root cellar · ice · freezing · frozen at sea | winter roots, frozen fish, ice cream, muktuk | Northern Europe, Inuit, industrial |
| **Modern** | high-pressure (HPP) · modified atmosphere · irradiation · vacuum · nitrite | cold-pressed juice, bagged salad, irradiated space meats, sous-vide, Prague powder cures | industry, NASA, the charcuterie counter |

## The cuisines that *think* in preservation

The ones where the pantry is the cuisine, not a backup to it: **Nordic** (salt, smoke, dry, ferment — the whole coast) · **Japanese** (tsukemono, umeboshi, miso, katsuobushi, narezushi: a preserved pantry with fresh food laid on top) · **Korean** (kimchi and jeotgal are the meal's spine) · **Chinese** (lap cheong, century egg, salted duck egg, preserved mustard greens, doubanjiang) · **Iberian** (conservas, bacalhau, jamón, chorizo, escabeche) · **Italian** (salumi, sott'olio, sott'aceto, bottarga, colatura) · **French** (charcuterie, confit, cornichons) · **Balkan / Turkish / Caucasus** (turšija, ajvar, kaymak, pastırma, sujuk, lakerda, boza — see `balkan-breakfast.locus.md`) · **Levant** (labneh, makdous, pickled turnips) · **Indian** (achar, papad) · **Andean and Mexican** (chuño, charqui, chipotle) · **Filipino** (bagoong, tuyo, longganisa, atchara) · **Ethiopian** (niter kibbeh, berbere) · **Jewish deli** (lox, pickles, pastrami, schmaltz) · **Appalachian / American South** (country ham, canning culture, pickles) · **Arctic** (igunaq, muktuk, hákarl) · **expedition and space** (pemmican, hardtack, thermostabilized pouches, freeze-dried).

## The syllabus — tier one, one per side (verification of editions = BOLO 40)

| Side | The one to start with | Why | Bench |
|---|---|---|---|
| **The philosophy of preservation** | Sue Shephard, *Pickled, Potted, and Canned: How the Art and Science of Food Preserving Changed the World* (Simon & Schuster, 2000) | the field as an idea and a history, method by method — the book that matches the ask | Kurlansky *Salt* (2002) and *Cod* (1997), pop-history, read as such |
| **The reference** | Alan Davidson, *The Oxford Companion to Food* (OUP, 1999; 3rd ed. Tom Jaine, 2014) | the "Oxford whatever of whatever" — one entry per food, per method, per cuisine | *The Cambridge World History of Food* (Kiple & Ornelas, 2000, 2 vols) for the academic layer |
| **The science 101** | Harold McGee, *On Food and Cooking* (Scribner, rev. 2004) | the levers above, explained | Jay, Loessner & Golden *Modern Food Microbiology* (7th ed., 2005) as the textbook |
| **The craft standard** | USDA *Complete Guide to Home Canning* (AIB 539, rev. 2015) via the National Center for Home Food Preservation — free | the controlling process text when making starts | UGA *So Easy to Preserve* (6th ed., 2014); Featherstone *A Complete Course in Canning* (14th ed., 2015) for industrial tinning |
| **Fermentation** | Sandor Katz, *The Art of Fermentation* (Chelsea Green, 2012) | the standard, method by method, every cuisine | Katz *Wild Fermentation* (rev. 2016) as the lighter 101; Redzepi & Zilber *The Noma Guide to Fermentation* (2018) for the modern kitchen |
| **A cuisine that thinks this way** | Nancy Singleton Hachisu, *Preserving the Japanese Way* (Andrews McMeel, 2015) | one whole pantry, as a book | Ruhlman & Polcyn *Charcuterie* (rev. 2013) — parked with the curing ruling |
| **Space** | Bourland & Vogt, *The Astronaut's Cookbook* (Springer, 2010) | NASA food systems by the people who ran them | NASA Space Food Systems Laboratory publications (thermostabilized, rehydratable, irradiated, natural-form) |
