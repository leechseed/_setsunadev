# THE BALKAN BREAKFAST — the proof locus

**The finding first.** The Balkan breakfast is a *preserved pantry served cold* with one hot bread on top. Nearly every item on the table is a preservation product: cultured dairy, brined cheese, dry-cured sausage, a roasted-pepper conserve, olives, pickles, jam, and a fermented drink. The hot element (burek, banitsa, proja, pogača) is the only thing made that morning. That is why it belongs to BOLO 39: it is the philosophy of preservation *as* a meal.

## The neighbors — the Overton window

- **East: Turkish kahvaltı** — the same table with more of it: white cheese, olives, sucuk, eggs (menemen), kaymak-and-honey, simit, tomatoes and cucumber, tea instead of coffee. The Balkan breakfast is kahvaltı with Slavic bread and pork where Islam thinned out.
- **South: Greek breakfast** — koulouri, tiropita, Greek yogurt with honey, olives, feta. Same substrate, more bakery.
- **Southeast: Levantine breakfast** — labneh, za'atar, olives, makdous, pickled turnips, ful. The preserved-pantry principle at its purest; no pork, more legumes.
- **North: Central European (Austro-Hungarian) breakfast** — bread, butter, cold cuts, soft cheese, jam, coffee. Where the Balkans stop, the pantry turns to charcuterie and butter.
- **By method:** kajmak ↔ clotted cream (Devon) ↔ mascarpone · ajvar ↔ Spanish pimientos, Georgian ajika · sujuk ↔ Spanish chorizo, Lebanese sujuk · turšija ↔ torshi (Iran), giardiniera (Italy), curtido · boza ↔ kvass (Russia), chicha (Andes).
