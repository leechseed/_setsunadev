# THE TASTING MAP

**Rule (Papi, 9/6):** the original, then close alternatives findable *anywhere* — supermarket, Amazon, or the nearest Asian, Latin, Mediterranean, or Eastern European grocer. Not tied to where he lives. Eat down the table; the field map tells you what lever you are tasting.

## Dry

| The original | Close alternatives, findable anywhere | Where |
|---|---|---|
| Norwegian stockfish (air-dried cod, no salt) | bacalhau / salt cod (salt + dry) · dried squid · dried shrimp | Latin or Portuguese grocer, Asian grocer, Amazon |
| Biltong (South Africa) | any beef jerky · Chinese pork jerky (bak kwa) | supermarket, Asian grocer |
| Chuño (Andean freeze-dried potato) | freeze-dried anything (backpacking meals) · dried porcini | Amazon, camping aisle |
