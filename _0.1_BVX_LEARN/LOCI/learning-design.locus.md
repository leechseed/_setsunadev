# Learning design: the charts Chief kept

**Where they came from.** Nine printed sheets Chief kept as visual models that inspired him, scanned 11/4 (Chief: "a bunch of visual models that I was inspired by"). Scan: `_PRIVATE/intake/other/2026-11-04_015_hand/read.pdf`, page order below. Filed here on Chief's "go", 11/4. Research, not canon.

| Pages | Model | What it says | Where it can work in the Command |
|---|---|---|---|
| 4, 6, 7, 8 | **Bloom's Revised Taxonomy** (Anderson & Krathwohl 2001) | Six steps of thinking, low to high: remember · understand · apply · analyze · evaluate · create. The sheets add verbs and question stems per step. Page 4 carries Chief's hand: "Lower Level Thinking / Higher Level Thinking" and a "Purpose" note. | Grades the teach-back loop: saying it back is *understand*; using it on a new case is *apply*. |
| 3 | **The Reading Rope** (Scarborough 2001) | Skilled reading = word recognition (sounds, decoding, sight words) braided with language comprehension (background, vocabulary, structure, reasoning, literacy). | The two strands map onto the plain register: the words land fast, then the meaning lands. |
| 2, 5 | **Fidelity checklists for reading comprehension** (Hennessy 2020) | What good reading teaching does: vocabulary · sentences · text structure · background knowledge · levels of understanding and inference. | A checklist shape for judging any teaching reply or explainer page. |
| 1 | **The Top 12 Most Useful & Universal Mental Models** (Michael Simmons) | A wheel of twelve model families, each with its sub-models. | Sits beside the EUROPATCH and charge-and-spend pattern cards. |
| 9 | **Making Thinking Visual with Graphic Organisers** (Murdoch & Wilson 2004) | Matches each thinking job to a diagram: recall (spider, cluster, concept map) · sequence (cycle, flow chart) · cause (futures wheel, fishbone) · analysis (Y chart, T chart, Venn) · decision (scales, comic strip). | Pick a page's diagram by the thinking the page asks for. |
