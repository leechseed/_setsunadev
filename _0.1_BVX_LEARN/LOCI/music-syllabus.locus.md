# The MUS syllabus — verified, corrected, and costed at zero

**The question.** What does a self-taught, time-poor reader actually read to hold both paradigms? The 9/6 shortlist was drafted from memory and never checked. This is the outside pass.

**Corrections to the draft.**

- **Laitz, *The Complete Musician*** — the draft named the 4th ed. (2015). Current is the **5th (2022), with Callahan**, OUP, 960 pp.
- **Strange, *Electronic Music*** — the draft's "1972/83" read as used-only. A **3rd-edition reissue is back in print**, funded by two Kickstarters.
- **Kostka & Payne, *Tonal Harmony*** — not wrong, just unversioned. Current is the **9th ed. (2024)**, Almén added.
- **Russ, Roads, Puckette, Open Music Theory** — all checked out exactly as drafted.
- **Missing:** Farnell, *Designing Sound* (MIT 2010, free borrow on Internet Archive) — the bridge between Puckette's signal maths and Strange's patch logic. And Hutchinson, *Music Theory for the 21st-Century Classroom* (free) as a gentler on-ramp than Open Music Theory.

**MUS.01 synthesis, in order.** Puckette, *Theory and Technique of Electronic Music* (free) — **6–8 weeks of evenings**, alongside a Pd install, which is where the hours actually go. Then Russ, browsable, dipped into per synthesis type. Strange only if the target is modular and analog rather than software. Roads is shelf reference, never read start to finish.

**MUS.02 theory, in order.** Open Music Theory (free) **or** Laitz — one, not both; they cover the same ground. Kostka & Payne only as a lateral alternative if the chosen text is not landing. Theory wants linear order, unlike synthesis's browsing.

**The zero-cost path, which is the one that matches the standing budget.** Puckette → Farnell (borrowed) for synthesis; Open Music Theory straight through for theory, with Hutchinson first only if the fundamentals are not solid. Two synthesis texts, one theory text, no purchases, no library holds.

**Honest on hours.** Six evenings a week are already committed to BOLO 24 through February 2027. The ordered path above assumes evening time that does not currently exist. It is queued, not scheduled.
