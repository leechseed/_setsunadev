# Weekly Maid Service — Cost Reference

Researched 2026-08-30. National numbers for a typical 3-bed / 2-bath, ~2,000 sq ft house.

## The numbers

| Cadence | Per visit | Per month |
|---|---|---|
| **Weekly** | $120–180 | ~$500–750 |
| **Bi-weekly** (sweet spot) | ~same per visit | ~$250–400 |
| One-time deep clean | $180–280 | — |

- Recurring service earns a **20–25% discount** vs one-time — the house never gets that dirty between visits.
- **First visit costs more**: most services require an initial deep clean at 1.5–2× the recurring rate.

## What moves the price

- **Metro premium:** SF/Boston/DC/Seattle run 25–45% above national; rural South/Midwest 10–20% below.
- **Size/mess:** small apartment $75–120/visit; bigger house, pets, kids climbs.
- **Independent vs franchise:** individual cleaner ~$25–50/hr, cheaper than Molly Maid / Merry Maids flat rates. Tradeoff: franchises are bonded/insured and cover no-shows.
