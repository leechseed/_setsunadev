# THE DISCORD INTRO REPLIES

Adult friend server. Four intro posts, four first-contact replies drafted. **None sent.**
Raw posts stay in the DMs; only my own drafts and the method are parked.

## The method (the reusable part)

The whole server runs on one filter: **did you actually read the post.** Every profile
says it in some form — a color test, "I drop dry chats," "don't message if you won't
stay." So the reply is built to prove reading, not to perform interest.

1. **Answer the gate first, literally.** If they set a test (favorite color), give the
   real answer in the first line, then be funny. Funny *instead of* answering fails.
2. **Pick the odd item out.** Every profile has one thing that doesn't match the rest.
   That's the hook. It's specific, it's unaskable by anyone who skimmed, and it's warm.
3. **One question, answerable in detail.** Not three. Not "tell me about yourself."
4. **Match length to their length.** A five-line profile gets a five-line reply.
   A wall gets a wall. Mismatched energy reads as trying too hard in either direction.
5. **Claim two overlaps, not all of them.** "I like everything on your list" = didn't read.
6. **Say nothing about what they fenced off.** The strongest proof of reading is the
   topic you *don't* raise.
7. **Follow their stated process rules** (request before friending, etc). Free credit.
8. **Close without asking for anything.** No "hope to hear from you."

**Standing hook across all four:** *I write fiction.* True, differentiating, invites a
follow-up, overshares nothing. Held back only where it doesn't fit.
