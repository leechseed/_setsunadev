01 // limb // the trunk

The Spine — the Comparative Tree

A story is a Grand Argument — proof, airtight from every angle, that one approach to a problem succeeds or fails, and was worth it or not. Dramatica is the ruled spine because it alone is a tree; every rival is a subset, a template on one branch, or a lens on the audience.

L0The Root — what a story isthe Grand Argument

One complete argument made through a single Story Mind. Rival root claims: Aristotle (mimesis of complete action) · Egri (proof of a premise — the direct ancestor) · McKee (character revealed by choice under pressure) · the neuro school (a simulation the brain runs — models the audience).

L1Four Throughlinesone problem, four seats

OS (they — the problem among everyone) · MC (I — inside one skin) · IC (you — the pressure to change) · RS (we — the argument between them). No rival model has all four. The hero's journey is the MC line in mythic dress; the "B-story" is the RS unnamed.

L2The Structural NestingDomain → Concern → Issue → Problem

Each throughline descends four quad levels: 4 Domains (Situation · Activity · Fixed Attitude · Manipulation) → 16 Concerns → 64 Issues (each with a Counterpoint) → the atomic quad: Problem / Solution / Symptom / Response. Each throughline claims one Domain exclusively — the OXO §9 rotation is this exclusivity in action. The chart itself is held: BVX.0091.

L3The Eight Dynamicshow it moves and ends

Resolve · Growth · Approach · Style · Driver · Limit · Outcome · Judgment. Outcome × Judgment is the four-ending matrix; OXO's Failure/Good is the Personal Triumph cell. Weiland's positive/flat/negative arcs are this matrix renamed.

OXO instance: Change · Stop · Do-er · Linear · Action · Optionlock · Failure/Good.
L4Plotthe argument in time

8 static appreciations (Goal, Consequence, Cost, Dividend…) + 4 Signposts and 3 Journeys per throughline, acts turned by Drivers. Below this floor the spine stops — scenes and beats belong to plot_systems. The rivals live down there: three-act = the OS skeleton · Save the Cat = a timed template · Story Grid = the fractal scene grammar · McKee = the beat engine.

L5Characterfunctions before persons

64 elements distributed so the Story Mind is complete; the 8 archetypes are pre-bundled sets. Protagonist ≠ MC — function and perspective are independent axes. House binding: L12 FUNCTION / mc_problem_element. Persons — psychology, body, sexuality — are L1–L11, downstream, never in the spine.

L6Themestructural, not a message

Issue vs Counterpoint argued through the throughline; Problem vs Solution at the atom; Outcome + Judgment pass the verdict. McKee's controlling idea = the same, compressed to a sentence.

L7Genre & Audiencethe outermost ring

Dramatica's weakest ring; the library carries the strength: Coyne's genre conventions (the reader-contract) and Truby's Anatomy of Genres (moral-argument traditions). The four production stages: storyforming → storyencoding → storyweaving → reception.

02 // limb // the containers

The Scale Ladder

Narrative comes in eight container sizes. Containers hold; threads change. A character arc, a setting arc, a rivalry — those are threads running through containers at any span. "Arc" was never between sequence and story; it runs across all of them.

R1Beatone action, one reaction

The smallest dramatic exchange — one micro-shift in a value or intention.

Whole when: something tried, something answered, something changed.
R2Scenethe smallest container that turns

A unit of continuous dramatic attention completing a miniature arc. Pacing formally lives here — see the Texture Layer's duration machinery. Its notation is the Scene Card (Setting section).

Whole when: a value flips polarity (McKee) and the five commandments are present (Coyne). No turn, no scene.
R3Sequenceone dramatic question

A chain of scenes under one question, raised at the head, answered at the tail. Coyne's fractal rule is the load-bearing beam: the five commandments recur at every rung.

Whole when: the question is answered — usually replaced by a worse one.
R4Act / Movementone span of the argument

Each throughline consumes one Signpost; a Driver turns the story. House note: OXO's six Movements are presentation containers; the four signposts are structural order. The mapping table is flagged plot_systems work.

Whole when: all four throughlines advance one signpost; a driver has turned the story.
R5Storythe storyform

One complete Grand Argument — the spine's whole tree, satisfied. Instance: oxo_primary_v1.

Whole when: all four throughlines close; Outcome and Judgment are delivered.
R6Nested Storyformstory within story

A complete argument inside a parent — already canon (integration protocol Pattern 1): own storyform_id; characters may switch roles across forms; the parent wins conflicts.

Whole when: whole as a story and consistent with the parent.
R7IP / Seriesmany stories, one container

Four canonical patterns for stories inside one series: anthology (independent forms, shared world) · serialized (one form stretched — OXO's shape) · episodic-with-mythology (nested episode-arguments under a parent) · braided (parallel forms sharing one OS).

Whole when: every member form closed; the shared canon uncontradicted.
R8Universe / Canonmultiple IPs, shared substrate

The LEECHSEED level — the canon-node and SSOT machinery are this rung's implementation.

Whole when: never — a universe stays consistent, not finished.
Standing addendum
Every completion test carries one final clause: …and no in-scope invariant is breached. A scene that turns beautifully while violating a standing invariant is not a whole scene — it is a breach. See The Law.

04 // limb // the discourse side

The Texture Layer

Every telling is a configuration of ten answers. The one law worth tattooing: who speaks ≠ who sees.

T1Who speaks

Narrator in/outside the story; frame levels (nest like R6); reliability; when the telling happens (after, during, before, interleaved).

T2Who sees

Focalization: zero (unrestricted) · internal (through a character) · external (surfaces only).

T3In what order

Story-time vs telling-time. Anachronies, formalized.

T4At what speed

Pacing, formalized — five speeds: scene (1:1) · summary · stretch · pause · ellipsis. Pacing is the pattern of speeds.

T5How often

Once-for-once · n-times-for-once (trauma's signature) · once-for-n-times (habit's — how S8 renders).

T6In what words

Register, syntax rhythm, FID; the distance dial — "show don't tell" is a dial, not a commandment.

T7With what feeling

Tone (author attitude) vs mood (reader atmosphere). Hogan: emotion as structure, not decoration.

T8Through what thresholds

Paratext — a design surface for an IP builder: episode titles, movement names, in-world documents.

T9Breaking which frames

Metalepsis; the unnatural catalogued, not forbidden.

T10Aimed at whom

Narratee and implied reader. In games, the player-narratee fusion.

The Law — Narrative Invariants

Tropes describe; invariants forbid. The constraint layer sits over the whole lattice, and enforcement rides every completion test: …and no in-scope invariant is breached.

Doctrine 0 — the seven meta-rules

IEarned, not declared — two concrete uses before promotion.
IIExists only in writing — held in memory, it's a mood.
IIINames what it forbids — falsifiable in-scene, or it's decoration.
IVHolds at every scale — coherence is the invariant surviving zoom. This is the ladder's dimension.
VNames its derivation — no lineage, no audit, no trust.
VIFew — more than seven in a layer is a preference list.
VIIDies by countermand, never by neglect — a quiet violation is a breach.
