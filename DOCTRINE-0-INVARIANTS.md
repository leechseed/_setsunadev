# DOCTRINE 0 — INVARIANTS ON INVARIANTS

This is the doctrine that governs the doctrine. Before the MCDP-shaped stack acquires its own capstone, the system requires rules for what qualifies as a rule. These are the invariants about invariants. Step one of the doctrine project.

Written to the Marine Corps Doctrine Standard: declarative, no hedging. States states.

---

## 1 · The seven

**I. An invariant is earned, not declared.**
Two concrete uses before promotion — the Quartermaster rule, already canon. A rule proposed at the moment of its first use is a hypothesis. The system promotes; the author does not anoint.

**II. An invariant exists only in writing.**
Inherited from the registry rule: a decision that is not written down did not happen. An invariant held in memory is a mood the author is currently in.

**III. An invariant names what it forbids.**
Every invariant is falsifiable in-scene: it points at a concrete thing that can no longer occur. An invariant that forbids nothing constrains nothing, and a constraint that constrains nothing is decoration.

**IV. An invariant holds at every scale.**
Line, scene, movement, series — sentence, document, system. This is the validation method already proven against *Arcane* and *Westworld*: coherence is the invariant surviving zoom. A rule true at only one altitude is a local convention, and is filed as one.

**V. An invariant names its derivation.**
Every invariant carries a Derived From — the source, ruling, or observed pattern it was promoted out of. The VICTORIA_MIDNIGHT ingest already enforces this at §9. An invariant with no lineage cannot be audited, and what cannot be audited cannot be trusted at scale.

**VI. Invariants are few.**
A layer holding more than seven is holding preferences. Scarcity is the enforcement mechanism: each promotion past the seventh demands a demotion or a merger. The count is the quality gate.

**VII. An invariant dies by countermand, never by neglect.**
Demotion is explicit, dated, and written — the standing-order logic. A quietly violated invariant is not a revision; it is a breach, and it invalidates every downstream document that trusted it.

## 3 · Open ruling — the name

"Invariant" is doing the work but the word is disliked. Candidates, in recommended order:

| Candidate | Register | Case |
|---|---|---|
| **Standing Order** ⭐ | Marine | An order that remains in effect until countermanded. Invariant VII falls out of the name for free. Fits BVMC frame and The Administration's voice. |
| **Law** | Ecclesial | Precedent exists (Ecclesial Laws). Short. Risks colliding with legal-layer language in the twin-track ruling. |
| **Non-Negotiable** | House | Already in use for the media extractions. Reads as adjective, awkward as a noun at scale. |
| **Keel** | Nautical/metaphor | The thing that keeps the ship upright and is invisible above the waterline. Evocative, zero precedent in-repo. |

**Constraint on the ruling:** machine fields (`narrative_invariant`, one-sheet `Invariants:`) keep the old term until schema-propagation decision #3 executes — renaming prose and schema in separate moments forks the SSOT. One ruling, one propagation pass.
