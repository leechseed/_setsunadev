# OXO / THE OUTLIERS — School Fusion Brief

**Purpose of this document:** a self-contained handoff so a fresh Claude session (with no access to the `_setsunadev` repo) can help develop the school setting for OXO. Everything needed is in this file. The `sources/` folder holds the verbatim originals if deeper detail is wanted.

**Project:** OXO = OVEREXITOUT = *The Outliers*. A non-linear, anthology-style serial about the reconstruction of human "assets" inside a tri-state megalopolis (Florida / Alabama / Georgia).

1. **The "Inner Spiral" Academy** — current canon (2025–26). Named exactly once in the Master System Bible; everywhere else it is generically "The Academy," "Year 1 School," "Year 2 School."
2. **Red Hills Academy** — legacy canon (2022–23), later variant name *Red Stick Creek* (from North Florida Red Stick Creek Native American history). Far richer institutional detail.

**The task: fuse these two lineages.** Section 4 explains why they fuse cleanly.

## 1. WORLD CONTEXT (current canon)

### The Delta Coast Spiral
The world is a concentric spiral spanning FL/AL/GA:

- **The Core — The Spaceport.** Apex of "Legibility." Where the **Bishops** rule. High-tech, total surveillance.
- **The Middle Bands — Academic/Industrial sectors. THIS IS WHERE THE ACADEMY IS.** Setting for Movements 2 & 3.
- **The Outer Bands — the "Wild West."** Lawless everglades and super-highways. Where the **Fowlers** and **Montgomerys** operate.

### Death as a Product Recall
Death is not an end; it is a **Boot Sequence**.
- **Initialization:** a high-value "Outlier" (Victoria, Quinn, Riley) dies in a catastrophic event and is acquired by a Faction.
- **The Reset:** a hard-reboot that overwrites the traumatic memory with a "System-Approved" reality (a funeral, a medal ceremony).
- **The Reveal:** characters believe they are human until Movement 4, where the hardware becomes undeniable.

### The Six Movements (the school is Movements 2–4)

| # | Movement | Genre | Invariant | Aesthetic |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Delta Coast | Southern Gothic Racing | Equity / Acquisition | RDR2, Nitro-Cross, dirt & dust |
| **2** | **Year 1 School** | **Academic / Social, Sync-Cult, FLCL** | **Constraint** | ***Social Network*, *The Wave*, CLEAN** |
| **3** | **Year 2 School** | **Mystery / Thriller, Gooner-Noir** | **Power / Corruption** | ***Spring Breakers*, *Dragon Tattoo*, NEON-ROT** |
| **4** | **Bourne** | **Espionage / Body Horror** | **The Ban** | **Cold steel — "physical pursuit on campus"** |
| 5 | Fight Club | Radicalization / Underground | Inequity | Industrial nihilism, low-tech guerrilla |
| 6 | The Pacific | War / Attrition | Collapse | *Children of Men*, *The Pacific* |

The school arc is a **descent**: Year 1 is clean, bright, cult-like conformity. Year 2 rots into sensory nightmare. Movement 4 is Victoria erased and hunted across the campus, transitioning from "Student" to "Noise."

## 2. CURRENT CANON — the Academy as it stands

### Movement 2: Year 1 Academy (The Sync Cult)
- Victoria is recruited into the "Inner Spiral" Academy.
- **The school is a "Sync Cult." Students are ranked by how well they mirror the Bishop Faction's ideals.**
- **The FLCL element:** Victoria's grief and hardware manifest as surreal, explosive physical outbursts — mechanical "horns," "Vespa-sized" data glitches — institutionally dismissed as **"Digital Puberty."**
- The "Hardware Gate": Year 1 glitches are written off as puberty/stress.

### Movement 3: Year 2 Academy (The Transgressive Decay)
- The "Clean" Academy rots into a sensory nightmare.
- Sexuality and drugs (**V-Sync**) become the primary tools of control.
- Students are kept in **"V-Sync" trances** (edging on a hardware crash) to make them compliant.
- Victoria uses the transgressive underground (*Dragon Tattoo* mode) to find the deleted data of her brother's death.
- Palette: **Jaundice Yellow** signals the Academy's decay.

### The ranking system — this IS the "curriculum"
Derived from *Kill la Kill*, codified as the **KLK Framework (Fashion as Hardware)**:

- **Star-Rating Code:** a literal rank display worn on uniforms.
- **Feed-Linked Clothing:** uniforms are interfaces. **"Clothing = Rank" and "Skin = Biometric Transparency."** Less clothing = more biometric data-transparency.
- **Desync Triggers:** dress code violations are **not** social faux pas — they are **Integrity (I) failures that trigger administrative intervention.**

### The four Invariants of Power (series-wide law)
1. **Power is Worn on the Body.** Rank is displayed on uniforms.
2. **Shame is a Tool of Control.** The Administration uses public social "Desyncs" and sexual blackmail to enforce obedience.
3. **Resistance Requires Self-Acceptance.** Victoria wins by embracing her "messy" hardware rather than trying to be "clean."
4. **Revolution is Undignified.** The break from the Academy in Movement 4 is chaotic, obscene, and violent.

### ❗ THE BIG GAP
**There is no named headmaster, dean, principal, professor, or teacher anywhere in current canon.** Authority is deliberately depersonalized into **The Administration** — an AI church-state bureaucracy. There is also **no curriculum in the academic sense**: no subjects, courses, timetables, or exams are ever specified. The only "curriculum" is the Sync ranking system.

If professors, faculty, and a course of study are wanted, that is **net-new writing** — and the legacy Red Hills layer (Section 3) is the only place in the whole project with human faculty politics.

## 3. THE INSTITUTIONAL CHARACTERS

### The Administration — "The God-Head of the Spiral"
*Status: CANON LOCKED v1.0. Role: The Operating System / Antagonist of the Status Quo.*

A **Church-State Metaphysical Bureaucracy** — Kafkaesque never-ending trial fused with the declarative axioms of an all-seeing AI. Institutional name: **The Administration (The Bishops)**.

- **Goal:** Total Legibility. **Resolve:** Steadfast (the Code is Immutable). **Domain:** Fixed Attitude (The Feed / Doctrine).
- **Problem → Solution:** Logic → Feeling. **Unique Ability / Critical Flaw: Omniscience / Non-Acceptance.**
- **The Sacrament of Verification:** legitimacy is not earned, it is **issued**. To be "Un-Verified" is excommunication from the infrastructure of survival.
- **The Elimination of Noise:** human experience (grief, memory, meaning) is "Systemic Residue." If residue interferes with the Feed, it is **"Banned"** — rendered undetectable.
- **Voice:** they do not ask questions, they state states. *"The Asset is acquired." "The Noise is archived." "The Feed is stable."* Tone: **Clinical Dread** — no anger, only the cold finality of a file being closed.
- **How it classifies Victoria:** M1 Potential → M2/M3 **Proprietary Salvage** (a body being "Tuned" into an instrument) → M4/M5 Critical Error → M6 The Fault Line.
- **The blindspot:** humanity's tenacity — "Stalking the Bear." They accounted for logic, fear, and rebellion, but not the **exhaustion hunt**: "Noise" variables that simply follow the system until its energy is spent.
- **Imagery:** vast halls of obsidian pillars; glass, light, absolute symmetry; **the Shadowless Room** — lighting so perfect it casts no shadow, symbolizing the death of the "Outlier" (the hidden).

### The Student Diaspora — "The Ghost Generation"
*Status: CANON LOCKED v1.0. Role: The Residue Population / Collective Variable / Noise Insurgency.*

**The Academy's alumni-outcome document — the waste product of the school.** High-IQ "Tuned" individuals deemed surplus to requirements and deleted from the Feed.

- **Primary bond:** Victoria Midnight (the Radicalizing Spark). **Archetype:** Emotion (Collective Trauma).
- **Unique Ability / Critical Flaw: Invisibility / Disintegration** (without a leader they are just a "Static Zone").
- **Methodology — Deduction:** they use their **collective Academy training to "Reverse-Engineer" the Feed.** They possess the "User Manual" for the system's indoctrination protocols.
- **THE DRESS CODE BREACH — the breaking point:** the collective act of defiance began when they simply **stopped wearing the "Verified" uniforms** or grooming to elite norms. Breaking dress code was the first step in exiting the system's "Legibility." *(This is the single strongest link between the ranking system and the revolution — it's the hinge of the whole school arc.)*
- **Living conditions:** Dead Zones — abandoned malls, humid concrete basements, the lawless Everglades.
- **Behavior:** **Polyphonic Noise** — they finish each other's sentences, sounding like a glitched choral broadcast; a distributed processing network debugging a world that says they don't exist.
- **The Common Glitch:** collective high-pitched ringing ("Tinnitus of the Soul") and a visual flicker in the lower-left quadrant (the "Un-Verified" Mark) — a side-effect of **mass "Tuning" sessions in Movement 2.**
- **Imagery:** basement terminals, tactical-streetwear, **"Verified" tattoos on necks and wrists deliberately scarred over with "Outlier" symbols.**

## 4. THE FUSION — why the two lineages are the same story

**Key structural finding.** The legacy Red Hills Academy diagram and the current canon cast are the same characters under earlier names:

| Red Hills (2022–23) | Current canon (2025–26) | Confidence |
|---|---|---|
| **Tori** | **Victoria "Tori" Midnight** | Certain — current docs still call her "Tori" |
| **Riley** | **Riley Moss** (The Witness / Propagandist) | Certain — same name |
| **Colson**, Student Body President | **Anna Colson Conway** (The Engine — Power/Escalation) | Very high — surname match, and both are ascent-through-institutional-power figures |
| Cain (murder/suicide) | *no current equivalent* | Legacy only |
| Tyson, Ariel, Troupe, Khilāfah al-Ramād | *no current equivalent* | Legacy only — belongs to Riley's war-journalism spine, not the school |

Note the corroboration: current canon says Movement 3's focus is **"Anna Colson's ascent begins"** — and the legacy diagram makes **Colson's Student Body President initiation** one of three disruption vectors. **Same beat, two vocabularies.** Fusing the lineages is recovering material, not inventing it.

### Red Hills Academy — the legacy institutional layer (verbatim from `DIAGRAM OXO.drawio`)

**THE STATUS QUO**
- Red Hills Academy — **100+ Year Prestige Academy**, **Private Family Owned**
- **Inner-Family Ownership Turmoil:** **"Grandfather's Legacy" VS "Son's Attempt to Sell for Spoils"**
  - Grandfather's side: Righteous Prestige / Prestige Honorable Past Leadership
  - Son's side: Corruption/Coverup, Power & Wealth, Conspiracy / Radical New Profit-Focused Leadership
- **Loyalties are Split Amongst Faculty and Staff**
- **"Faculty & Staff Paralyzed between 3 powers that threaten/empower their career/livelihood"**
- **Devious Students with Alumni Family** — "Loyalty is Default Settled By Devious Students"

**THE STATUS QUO IS DISRUPTED BY**
- The Arrival of Tori
- The Arrival of Riley
- **The Student Body President Initiation of Colson**
- Incident: Murder/Suicide of Cain

**COLLAPSE INITIATED BY**
- Whistleblowing of Coverup (Riley)
- Vengeance and Violence of Troupe (Tori)
- **Conspiracy and Campus Takeover (Colson)**

### What each lineage contributes to the fusion

| Red Hills gives you | Inner Spiral gives you |
|---|---|
| Human faculty with careers and fear | The Administration as impersonal OS |
| A family ownership war (three-way power struggle) | Faction war (Bishop vs. Montgomery vs. Fowler) |
| Legacy/alumni students as a power bloc | Star-Rating meritocracy |
| Prestige and 100 years of history | Sci-fi surveillance and the Feed |
| A concrete disruption → collapse plot skeleton | The six-movement descent architecture |

**The obvious synthesis:** *Red Hills is what the Academy used to be, and the Inner Spiral is what it became after the Bishops acquired it.* The son who sold the school for spoils sold it **to the Bishop faction** — which converts a 100-year prestige institution into a Sync Cult, replacing human faculty with the Administration. That single move:
- explains why faculty are depersonalized in current canon (they were *purged*),
- preserves the family-ownership war as backstory with living stakes,
- gives "legacy/alumni students" a reason to resent the Star-Rating system (old money vs. new metric),
- and makes the Student Diaspora the *second* generation of discarded people, echoing the first.

*(This synthesis is a proposal, not existing canon — it is the connective tissue the source documents don't yet have.)*

## 5. WHAT TO WORK ON — open problems

1. **Name the fused school.** "Inner Spiral Academy" and "Red Hills Academy" both exist; the fusion needs one name, or a formal renaming event in the timeline (note: *Red Stick Creek* already exists as a renaming variant).
2. **Build the faculty.** Zero named educators exist. Who teaches at a school where the curriculum is conformity ranking? Are they human holdovers from Red Hills, or Administration constructs?
3. **Build the actual curriculum.** What are the classes? What does a Year 1 timetable look like? How is "Sync" literally taught, measured, and examined?
4. **Specify the Star-Rating mechanics.** How are ranks assigned, displayed, raised, lost? What does a 1-star student's uniform look like vs. a 5-star?
5. **Wire the ownership war into the Movements.** The legacy plot (grandfather vs. son, split faculty, alumni students) has no current placement in the six-movement structure.
6. **Reconcile the legacy cast.** Cain, Tyson, Ariel, Troupe, and Khilāfah al-Ramād are legacy-only. Keep, cut, or convert?
