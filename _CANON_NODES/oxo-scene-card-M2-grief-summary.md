**What this card is.** The first plot unit in the lattice whose told-order record is filled as **summary** (told time compresses happened time) and **iterative** (told once, happened many times) — the two modes ssot_04_plot_system.md's THE TOLD ORDER section named but had no worked instance for (OPEN call 13). Row 9 already points at this same fabula event for its P4 TURN and P7 REVEAL, but reads it at `duration: scene`, singulative, "one licensed stretch at the sync… though the fabula event it points at is itself iterative ⧗" (ssot_04_plot_system.md THE INSTANCE, P11 row). That line is this card's mandate: something upstream has to carry the iterative class itself, at compressed duration, or row 9's zoom-in has nothing to zoom into. This card is that something.

**It is not a discrete dramatized scene the way row 9 is.** The fabula marks `m2_grief_outbursts` `kernel_satellite: satellite` — "the workings-out of the choices made at the kernels," not itself a choice point (Bal 2017: 156-159; Chatman 1980: 53-55, both quoted in ssot_04_fabula.md's EVENT RECORD section). A montage/summary unit built on a satellite record is the correct shape for that kind of event: it does not need — and per the fabula's own kernel/satellite test, should not force — a single Gap-driven turn the way a kernel event does.
