# Population Signatures

Module 2 of L2b MORPHOLOGY. Module 1 ([L2b-morphology-gluteal](L2b-morphology-gluteal.md)) built the substrate: SKELETON (27 cells, `I·F·T`) → MUSCLE (PROJECTION · SHELF) → FAT (level × distribution) → CONDITION (skin · tone · line · carriage), RULED canon 2026-09-09. This module answers the question that node left open: what a sport, a craft, or a market does to that substrate over years, and what that lets you assume before you look closely.

> **Governing claim.** A population signature is two things multiplied: a **selection filter** (who gets into the population, which is to say which skeletal cells the sport or craft admits and keeps) and a **loading fingerprint** (what the training does to MUSCLE, FAT and CONDITION over years). **Selection sets the cell. Loading sets the tissue.** The literature is unusually clean on this split. Malina and colleagues (2013, growth and maturation, *Sports Medicine*) reviewed the gymnast case and concluded that the smallness of artistic gymnasts is a product of selection and self-selection, not of training; Norton and Olds (2001, kinanthropometry, *Sports Medicine*) showed athlete morphology in every measured sport diverging from the population over the twentieth century as selection intensified; Lee and Piazza (2009, biomechanics, *Journal of Experimental Biology*) showed sprinters carrying skeletal proportions no training produces. On the tissue side, muscle grows where it is loaded and not elsewhere (Wakahara and colleagues, 2012, exercise physiology; Handsfield and colleagues, 2017, on sprinters), fat level moves with energy balance while fat pattern is substantially heritable (Shungin and colleagues, 2015, genetics, *Nature*), and the gluteal node's own View A already lists which layer each lever reaches. So a population tells you two things at once, and they must be read separately: a prior on the bone, and a prior on what has been done to it.

---

## 1 · How the two halves combine

- **Selection is a filter on the 27 cells, not a point.** No sport admits one cell. Each admits a range and rejects the rest, and the strength of the filter varies: gymnastics filters hard on frame size, figure competition barely filters the pelvis at all. Confidence is carried per row for that reason.
- **Loading is a vector on the three trainable layers.** It is written here in the substrate's own terms: PROJECTION upper and lower max · SHELF (med/min) · FAT level and pattern · CONDITION (tone, line, carriage). Where a population also trains carriage (lordosis, pelvic tilt as a skill), that is the CONDITION entry, and per Contreras it changes apparent projection without changing tissue.
- **Two populations can share a cell and read differently** because their loading vectors diverge. That is the gluteal node's volleyball example generalized, and it is the point of the module: the cell is the skeleton's fact, the signature is the life's fact.
- **A market is not a population.** Sports and crafts select on function and load on function. A market (the adult performer case, §4) selects on the camera read and admits surgical modification, which the substrate has to model as an override that ignores loading. It gets its own section.

## 3 · Per-population sections ⧗

### Sprinter

**Mechanism.** Maximal-velocity running is hip extension at high force and high frequency, and the muscle that best separates elite from sub-elite sprinters is the gluteus maximus (Miller and colleagues, 2021, exercise physiology, *Medicine & Science in Sports & Exercise*); the hypertrophy is non-uniform, concentrated in the hip extensors and flexors rather than spread over the leg (Handsfield and colleagues, 2017, biomechanics, *Scandinavian Journal of Medicine & Science in Sports*). Skeletal selection is real but measured elsewhere: sprinters carry longer forefoot bones and shorter Achilles moment arms than non-sprinters (Lee and Piazza, 2009), and no pelvic breadth data were consulted, so the `I` prior is low confidence.

**Versus the nearest neighbor (powerlifter).** Both load hip extension hard. The sprinter's fat is low and the hamstring is the partner, so the glute-ham junction is a hard line and the glute:thigh ratio is high; the powerlifter's thigh matches the glute and the junction is buried.

**For character generation.** Roll from a narrow-to-medium `I` prior, allocate mass to both poles of the max with the hamstring as co-recipient, set fat low, and let CONDITION carry the read: this is the Tessera or the Dome built, not born.

### Gymnast

**Mechanism.** Artistic gymnastics filters on frame size harder than any other population here, and the filter is selection, not training: Malina and colleagues (2013) found that short stature and late maturation in gymnasts are explained by who enters and who stays, with no evidence that training attenuates growth. Loading is tumbling and landing: explosive hip extension plus heavy abductor demand in landing control, at a very low fat level held for years.

**Versus the nearest neighbor (sprinter).** The gymnast's quad is as loaded as the glute, so the read is one compact block rather than a glute sitting on a hamstring. Same low fat, opposite neighbor.

**For character generation.** Small frame first, dense tissue second; most gymnasts land mid-cell and unnamed, which the gluteal node says is the correct outcome for most people. Off the mat and a few years out, the same skeleton at rest fat reads Cupola.

### Ballet

**Mechanism.** Classical ballet selects for long limbs, low mass, and hip external-rotation range, and then loads at high repetition and low force. The turnout literature is the selection-versus-training case in miniature: dancers' hip external rotation does not account for the turnout the technique demands, and the remainder is made up below the hip (Hamilton and colleagues, 1992, sports medicine, *American Journal of Sports Medicine*; Gilbert, Gross and Klug, 1998, physical therapy, *JOSPT*). Koutedakis and Jamurtas (2004, exercise physiology, *Sports Medicine*) profile the dancer as an athlete with low body fat and endurance-type conditioning rather than mass.

**Versus the nearest neighbor (gymnast).** Same narrow frame, opposite loading: the gymnast's glute is built, the dancer's is drawn. The dancer's SHELF can exceed the PROJECTION because turnout works med/min and the deep rotators continuously while the max is never loaded heavy.

**For character generation.** Line over mass. PROJECTION low, SHELF moderate, fold minimal, carriage vertical. The female taxonomy has no name for this read; the male Column is the nearest, which is an open item (§7).

### Heels / twerk (dance floor + performance)

**Mechanism.** Two loads, one carriage. Heels shift the line of gravity and alter pelvic and lumbar posture; the postural literature disagrees on direction and size, but agrees the gait and posture change (Cronin, 2014, kinesiology, *Journal of Electromyography and Kinesiology*). Twerk is rhythmic anterior-posterior pelvic tilt at high repetition, which the vault's own 2026-07-11 note already identified as glute isolation from the lumbar spine. Neither builds much tissue; both train carriage, and Contreras's rule that lordosis produces apparent projection at rest independent of mass is the entire mechanism of the signature.

**Versus the nearest neighbor (bikini).** The bikini competitor builds the shelf and then poses it; the dance-floor performer poses first and builds little. Same lordotic read, different tissue underneath, and at rest without the carriage the twerk signature drops a type.

**For character generation.** This is the module's proof that CONDITION is a layer with force: hold cell and tissue fixed, add trained lordosis and pelvic control, and the read moves from Amphora to Cantilever. Selection here is by phenotype (people who already read projected self-select onto the floor), which is why the prior is on `F3 · T3` plus gynoid fat and not on any measured bone.

### Powerlifter

**Mechanism.** Squat, bench and deadlift select on levers and load hip extension at maximal force through a deep range. The anthropometry is measured: powerlifters carry greater bone breadths, larger girths and higher mesomorphy than the general population, scaling with weight class (Keogh, Hume, Pearson and Mellow, 2007, sports science, *Journal of Sports Sciences*; Keogh and colleagues, 2009; Lovera and Keogh, 2015, *Journal of Sports Medicine and Physical Fitness*). Sumo and wide-stance lifters add abductor load, which is a SHELF entry; conventional lifters do not.

**Versus the nearest neighbor (wellness).** Both are lower-body mass. Wellness preserves gynoid fat and builds the glute ahead of the thigh under judged criteria; the powerlifter builds whatever the total requires, and fat follows the weight class rather than a look.

**For character generation.** The gluteal node's Buttress is defined as "the default of heavy lower-body labor", and this is that default with a barbell. Mass everywhere below the waist, low glute:thigh, dense CONDITION, anterior tilt as habit rather than skill.

### Bikini

**Mechanism.** The division's published criteria reward shape, balance, complexion and presentation and score down muscularity, hardness and striation (NPC / IFBB Pro League rules; public, not peer-reviewed). The response is isolation hypertrophy of the upper max and med/min, the exact movements Contreras's EMG work ranks highest for the upper glute, with fat brought low but held over the lower pole so the read stays round. Carriage is trained as a stage skill: the back pose is a cocked hip with the lordosis engaged.

**Versus the nearest neighbor (figure).** One rung of muscle and a few points of fat. Figure wants separation between the poles of the max; bikini penalizes it. The same competitor moving from bikini to figure crosses from Cupola to Tessera without the skeleton moving.

**For character generation.** The built-round type. High upper PROJECTION, high SHELF, fat low but present, and a posed CONDITION that reads a type up when the pose is on. Carroll's *Building the Bikini Body 2.0* (BVX.0306) is in the vault unread and would sharpen the loading column.

### Figure

**Mechanism.** Figure selects on the upper body: shoulder-to-waist ratio, the V and X frame, lat width. The pelvis is nearly unselected, which means the cell prior for this population is close to flat and the module says so rather than inventing one. Loading is compound plus isolation to a lower fat level than bikini, at which the glute-ham tie-in and the upper/lower separation of the max surface.

**Versus the nearest neighbor (bikini).** Separation. Figure is the Tessera by the division's own criteria: "muscle separation, no striation" is the gluteal node's definition of that type read back.

**For character generation.** The one population where the population tells you almost nothing about the bone and almost everything about the tissue. Roll the skeleton from the general distribution; write the loading vector as given.

### Wellness

**Mechanism.** The IFBB Pro League stood the division up in 2019 around a physique the Brazilian scene had already produced: lower-body dominant, glutes, hips and thighs carrying the mass, upper body deliberately smaller. Selection follows the criteria: wide `I`, gynoid fat that the stage preserves rather than strips, and a lower-body hypertrophic response strong enough to build both poles of the max and the shelf together.

**Versus the nearest neighbor (the surgical override).** This is the honest line. Wellness produces the same silhouette the BBL market sells, and the vault's physique watchlist already carries the division with its own label (Division-context) beside Documented, Speculated and Natural-claimed precisely because the read alone cannot separate the two. The module inherits that discipline: a wellness read is a prior on selection and loading, never an inference about surgery.

**For character generation.** The Amphora at maximum, the Cantilever in the pose. For a generated character the override question does not arise; for a real one it is a label, not a read.

## 4 · Adult performer, treated honestly ⧗

**Not a population.** Sports select on function; the adult market selects on what the camera reads and what the audience buys, and it admits surgical modification as a normal input. It therefore contains several signatures at once, and the substrate has to add one thing the gluteal node did not need: an **override**, a change to FAT or PROJECTION that ignores loading and leaves the skeleton where it was.

**The three sub-signatures.**

| Sub-signature | What it is | How it reads against the 13 | The tell |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Natural-athletic** | Crossover from the bikini, fitness-influencer and dance-floor populations; the loading fingerprint is inherited from whichever one | Cupola and Amphora on the female side; Dome and Vault on the male side (the register's lane P now carries men by Chief's ruling of 9/9) | Tissue markers of loading are present: a shelf that came from med/min, a glute-ham line, dips where the frame has `T2–3` at that fat level |
| **Surgically-augmented** | **Fat graft (BBL):** autologous fat moved to the buttock and, commonly, into the trochanteric depression; subcutaneous placement only, per the ASERF task force after the mortality series (Mofid and colleagues, 2017, aesthetic surgery, *Aesthetic Surgery Journal*; the vault's parked reference records the mortality shift from about 1:3,000 to about 1:15,000 as technique changed and Florida's 2023 ultrasound-guidance mandate, both flagged there as leads to verify). **Implants:** a fixed silicone volume placed over or within the max, intramuscular in the Gonzalez method (Gonzalez, 2004, plastic surgery, *Aesthetic Plastic Surgery*) | A fat graft is a **FAT-layer override**: distribution drawn by the surgeon, and it fills dips, so a Corbel skeleton reads Cupola without the bone moving. An implant is a **PROJECTION-layer override**: high-set, hard-edged upper-pole volume with the lower pole and the fold unchanged, so it reads Dome or Cantilever above an unmodified under-butt | Inconsistency across layers. Projection without loading markers (no shelf from med/min, no glute-ham line); a hip:waist ratio beyond what the ilium supports, which the gluteal node already calls frame-limited; dips filled on a frame that shows `T3` on the lateral thigh above the graft; on implants, a superior edge or a pole mismatch |
| **Slim-thick** | Narrow ribcage and upper body, very small waist, large glute and thigh volume. Two routes: the natural one is `I3 · F3` gynoid at moderate fat, the Amphora turned up; the surgical one is waist liposuction plus fat graft in the same procedure, the removed fat supplying the added volume | Amphora and Cantilever, exaggerated | The waist-frame consistency rule separates the routes; and the natural route carries thigh mass with the glute while the surgical one frequently does not |

**The substrate rule.** Write the override as a flag on the cell call, never as a change to the cell: `override: none · fat-graft · implant · unknown`. Loading still applies underneath it (grafted fat gains and loses with weight like any fat; muscle under an implant still trains), which is why the tell is always a cross-layer inconsistency and never a single feature.

**The male lane.** The register's P lane now includes men (the Cream Factory and BBXL seeds), and the market selects the Dome and Vault reads for them; augmentation exists in the male market at lower volume and the same override rule applies. No separate sub-signature is proposed until the register has enough male rows to need one (§7).

**Labeling.** A cell call on a real performer carries the physique watchlist's four labels (Documented · Speculated · Natural-claimed · Division-context). An override is written as `fat-graft` or `implant` only when Documented; otherwise `unknown`. The repo is public, the watchlist ruled this non-negotiable, and this module does not loosen it.

**A cross-check worth pulling.** Mendieta's gluteal evaluation system for surgeons (Mendieta, 2006, plastic surgery, *Clinics in Plastic Surgery*; 2007, *Aesthetic Surgery Journal*) classifies frames as A, V, round and square from the bony pelvis before it considers fat and muscle. It runs on the same skeletal-first principle as Contreras from the other side of the table, it is not in the vault, and it belongs in the parked glute-enhancement reference (`oscar-mike/glute-reference.md`) as much as here.

## 5 · How a cell call works with this module

Population first, because it is cheap and visible. The module supplies the prior range. Chief narrows to one cell from the tells. Three lines, on a hypothetical:

```
1  POPULATION   wellness                         → prior I3 · F2–3 · T2–3 · PROJ u↑↑ l↑↑ · SHELF ↑↑ · FAT ↔ gynoid
2  TELLS        no dips at stage fat → T2  ·  widest point below the crest → F3  ·  upper body a size smaller → confirms
3  CALL         I3·F3·T2 · WELLNESS · nearest: Amphora · override: unknown (Division-context)
```

- **Line 1** is the only thing the caller has to know before looking: which population. The rest of the line is copied from §2.
- **Line 2** is the narrowing. Each tell moves one axis or confirms the prior. Dips at a known fat level are the `T` tell; the widest point relative to the crest is the `F` tell; the top line and the waist are the `I` tell.
- **Line 3** is what gets written into the register's staged morphology-cell column and the BOLO 23 anchors: cell · population · nearest type · override flag with its label. The performer register (`_CANON_NODES/performer-register.md`) and the physique set were both waiting on Chief's calls; this is the format that makes each one a cheap ask.

Two habits. A performer who fits no population gets `POPULATION: none` and the general prior (all 27 cells), which is honest and still useful. A performer who fits two (a bikini competitor who dances in heels) gets both, and the loading vectors are added, not averaged.
