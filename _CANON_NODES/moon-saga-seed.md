# MOON/SAGA — THE SOCIETIES SEED

**What this is.** Two loose fragments that belong together and belonged to nothing on the board: a list of the surviving societies of the moon, and a Civilization V leader table meant to be mapped onto them. Both point at the MOON/SAGA fabula in the 2024 `_g-on_dir`, a separate IP from The Outliers. Nothing here is ruled. This node exists so the seed has one address instead of three.

**The design intent, verbatim from the 2024 devlog:** *"Align each of the societies with civilizations from CIV VI, a hero, etc."* and *"each society should have a general philosophy of cognition that serves as a framework of the development and establishment of each surviving society on the moon, e.g. embodied cognition, cartesian dualism."*
