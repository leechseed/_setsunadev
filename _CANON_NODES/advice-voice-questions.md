## What the data says

- Creators ask how to work less without earning less. Burnout, daily posting, and lowball chats came up again and again.
- Boundaries are the biggest emotional topic. Women want to keep a regular, but not be on call.
- Privacy fear runs high: faces, leaks, bans, and future jobs. Women say "faceless" and "flagged" a lot.
- Kink questions are practical and hopeful. They ask for steps, safe ways to ask, and how to feel in charge.
- Situationship posts ran hottest in volume, and they use the phrases "gf treatment" and "did I mess up".
- Signs are almost absent. Only a handful of posts name any, so sign tags are rare.
- Hottest subs for volume were FemaleDatingStrategy, AskWomenOver30, CreatorsAdvice, and TwoXSex. FemaleDatingStrategy was mostly podcast promos.
- Missing from the pilots: creator burnout, bans and leaks, body and pain questions, kink club nerves, and poly etiquette.
