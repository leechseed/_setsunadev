**The one mechanic.** Own the category noun, then sell the two audiences from two shelves. **Transferable tips.**
- Register the category noun as the handle on every platform on day one, before the persona exists; a niche noun is the thing people type, and it outlasts any name change.
- Keep the free-tube bio to one sentence and one paid link — this channel's has been exactly that for three years while subscribers rose 2.5×; the Linktree lives on X, not on the tube.
- Build the price ladder as three points from one catalog — a $5 previews tier, a $9.99 full tier with small PPV inside it, a $50 tier that only removes the PPV — and run promos as dated weekend windows rather than permanent discounts.
- Cut every shoot into SKUs before it ships: standard, extended, director's cut, bundle at ~2× — the clip store is 303 listings from visibly fewer shoots, and the bundle is the highest ticket on the shelf.
- When a second line arrives that a different buyer wants, give it its own store name and cross-link ("Other Stores"), and give it its own wall behind the paywall; do not mix it into the main feed.
