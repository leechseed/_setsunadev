# Gluteal Morphology

One causal substrate. Two named taxonomies (male, female). Three views onto the same model.

> **The governing claim, from Contreras:** shape is **skeletal first**. Muscle and fat modulate a frame they cannot redraw. Any taxonomy that starts from the surface read is describing an output and calling it a cause.

---

## 1 · The substrate — four layers

```
SKELETON    ilium width × femoral neck length × trochanter prominence
   ↓        27 cells. Immovable. Sets the ceiling and the silhouette.
MUSCLE      PROJECTION (glute max: upper + lower) · SHELF (med / min)
   ↓        ← load history. The only layer training moves.
FAT         level × distribution pattern
   ↓        modulates every read; gates hip-dip visibility
CONDITION   skin · tone · line · carriage
            where believability and eroticism both live
```

Layers 1 and 3 are Contreras. Layer 2 is your Glute Doctrine. Layer 4 is the condition doctrine.

---

## 2 · The coordinate system

Three skeletal axes, three positions each. **27 cells.** Structurally identical to Royden's Body Matrix, arrived at independently.

| Axis | Narrow / Short / Small | Medium | Wide / Long / Pronounced |
|---|---|---|---|
| **I — Ilium width** | narrow blades, close-set | medium | wide, flared |
| **F — Femoral neck length** | short — femur sits close | medium | long — femur set out wide |
| **T — Greater trochanter** | small, low relief | medium | pronounced, palpable |

Notation: **`I2·F3·T3`** = medium ilium, long neck, pronounced trochanter.

### Contreras's two anchor points, verbatim

- `I1–2 · F3 · T3` → **heart- or pear-shaped**
- `I3 · F1 · T1` → **V-shaped**

Everything else in this document extends his mechanism to the remaining cells. **Those two cells are sourced; the rest are derived.**

### Secondary skeletal modifiers

- **Ilium-to-socket vertical distance** — governs how tall the glute reads
- **Sacral angle / lumbar lordosis** — governs apparent projection *at rest*, independent of muscle mass. A high-lordosis frame reads projected with less tissue.

## 3 · Derived features

Not shapes. Consequences.

| Feature | Derivation |
|---|---|
| **Hip dips** (trochanteric depression) | `T` prominence × `I` width × (inverse) body fat. Lean + wide + pronounced = pronounced dips. **Skeletal. Training cannot remove them.** |
| **Under-butt fold** | lower-max mass × fat over the lower pole × femoral attachment. Your P4 "carve the fold" target. |
| **The shelf** | med/min mass × `I` width. Upper lateral fullness. |
| **Hip:waist ratio** | `I` width × waist circumference. Frame-limited. |
| **Glute:thigh ratio** | max mass ÷ quad/ham mass. The most trainable ratio on the board. |

## 4 · FEMALE taxonomy

Named at the recognizable clusters, not all 27 cells. Register is architectural — these are load-bearing forms, and the vocabulary carries the mock-scholarly voice without going crass.

| Type | Cell | Read |
|---|---|---|
| **The Keystone** | `I3·F1·T1` | Contreras's V. Mass high and central, tapering down. Wide blades, femur tucked close, no trochanteric flare. Broad at the top, narrow at the fold. |
| **The Amphora** | `I2·F3·T3` | Contreras's heart/pear. Widest *below* the iliac crest, drawing in to the waist. The vessel silhouette. |
| **The Cupola** | `I1–2·F2·T1` + high fat over the glute | The bubble. Outward curve from every angle, domed, no flat planes. Small trochanter means no dips to interrupt the curve. |
| **The Lintel** | `I3·F2·T1` + low projection | Square. A horizontal top line and a flat posterior plane. Wide, structural, undomed. |
| **The Cantilever** | `I2·F3·T3` + high upper-max + lordosis | Maximum rearward projection with a hard upper shelf. The frame *and* the loading *and* the spinal angle all pushing the same direction. Rare — needs all three. |
| **The Corbel** | any `T3` at low body fat | Stepped profile. Hip dips read as a designed feature — an intentional notch between crest and trochanter — rather than an absence. |
| **The Tessera** | low fat + high max development | Visible separation between upper and lower max. Segmented rather than continuous. Almost always built, not born. |

## 5 · MALE taxonomy

Separate system, same axes. The skeleton is universal; the modifiers are not.

**What differs structurally:** narrower typical ilium · android fat distribution (less subcutaneous over the glute, so muscle reads more directly) · typically lower lordosis · at low body fat, striation and separation surface far earlier than on a gynoid distribution.

Consequence: **male glute shape is more muscle-legible and less fat-mediated.** The skeleton shows through sooner, and training moves the read further.

| Type | Cell | Read |
|---|---|---|
| **The Buttress** | `I3·F2·T2` + dense max | Broad, load-bearing, functional. Reads as capacity rather than ornament. The default of heavy lower-body labor. |
| **The Dome** | `I1–2·F2·T1` + high max | The bubble. Narrow frame concentrates all mass into projection with no lateral spill. The most trainable male type — narrow ilium is an *advantage* here. |
| **The Plinth** | `I3·F1·T1` + low projection | Wide base, flat plane, minimal rearward depth. Structural and undramatic. |
| **The Escarpment** | `I2–3·F2·T2` + high med/min | Pronounced upper shelf with a sharp drop to the fold. Built by lateral and abduction work — your `SHELF` axis, expressed. |
| **The Column** | `I1·F3·T2` | Narrow blades, femur set out, minimal flare. Vertical read; the glute continues the line of the leg rather than interrupting it. |
| **The Vault** | high lordosis + high lower-max | Projection driven by spinal angle as much as tissue. Curved under-surface, deep fold. Reads projected even at moderate mass. |

## 6 · View A — training targets *(ULTRASIN)*

The honest column. What moves, what doesn't.

| Element | Trainable? |
|---|---|
| Ilium width, femoral neck, trochanter | ❌ **Never.** Skeletal. |
| Hip dips | ❌ Skeletal. Fat level changes visibility only. |
| Hip:waist ratio | ⚠️ Waist only. The hip half is bone. |
| Glute max mass — projection | ✅ **The main lever.** Your `PROJECTION` axis. |
| Upper vs lower max balance | ✅ Contreras's own subdivision — trainable separately. |
| Med/min — the shelf | ✅ Your `SHELF` axis. Most-neglected, highest marginal return. |
| Glute:thigh ratio | ✅ Most trainable ratio available. |
| Fat level and distribution | ✅ Level yes; pattern is largely genetic. |
| Condition — skin, tone | ✅ Fully. And per doctrine, this is where the erotic read lives. |

**Your own frame determines which type you are building toward.** GDP-1's four phases move the muscle layer only. Naming your cell honestly at the start prevents training toward a silhouette your skeleton cannot produce — which is the most common failure in glute programming and the reason most people quit.

## 7 · View B — character generation *(L2b MORPHOLOGY)*

Generation order, causal not categorical:

1. **Roll the skeleton** — `I·F·T` from a population-appropriate distribution
2. **Apply load history** — sport, labor, training age → muscle allocation across PROJECTION / SHELF
3. **Apply fat** — level and pattern, sexed
4. **Apply lordosis** — posture and carriage
5. **Read the type** — the named type is an *output*; if it lands mid-cell it simply has no name, which is correct and true of most people
6. **Apply CONDITION** — the erotic and believability layer

A volleyball middle blocker and a libero can share a cell and read completely differently, because steps 2 and 4 diverge. That's the model working.

## 8 · View C — aesthetic taxonomy *(ORANGE)*

The naming layer is the IP. Three claims this system makes that nothing else in the space does:

1. **Shape is skeletal.** Most popular content sells trainability of things that are bone. This taxonomy is falsifiable and honest, which is the differentiator.
2. **Hip dips are a feature with a coordinate**, not a defect. `The Corbel` exists as a named positive type.
3. **Two sexed taxonomies from one substrate** — nobody has done the male side systematically at all.

## 10 · Open

- Named types are unvalidated against any corpus. Decision was **axes first, code later** — so these are hypotheses about where the clusters sit, not observations that they do.
- Population signatures (sprinter, gymnast, ballet, heels/twerk, powerlifter, bikini vs figure vs wellness, adult performer) are unmapped. That's the next module.
- Ilium-to-socket vertical distance is in Contreras but the passage was truncated in extraction — re-read that section before locking the secondary modifiers.
- No aging model. Fat redistribution and tissue quality shift the read substantially over decades and none of that is captured.
