## The pieces (as heard)

1. **The belt is hot.** Radiation in grades; the people born there carry exceptional resistance.
2. **The hot-zone divers.** A few Outliers can enter the hottest zones and bring out rare material, Moon stuff, which Earth's economy now runs on. Victoria (Tori) is one.
3. **The rig.** An umbilical along the spine (the exposed back, her strength), run out to an autonomous crawler up to a quarter mile away; the line carries the goop.
4. **The goop.** Kombucha-like: a microbial film on top, a stem-cell broth beneath. Left unused long enough, something fetal grows. A big radiation dose makes it feed the diver's repair.
5. **The Severance.** About 100 years back: nukes in space cut Earth from the Moon. The Moon did it on purpose, by Kessler cascade, because the Moon is hell and they could not leave any other way.
6. **The Outliers' blood.** Descendants of Moon people who came back to Earth and survived in the trash pit. Their DNA carries a mission they don't know they carry: take Earth so the Moon can come home. Petty at the root.

7. **The calf ports.** The rig also seats in both calves, the body's "second heart." An in-world erotic genre grows up around them.
8. **Genome-locked.** Each diver's broth is grown from her own DNA and works only on her. The fetal thing is, in a way, her child; every dive into the heat spends it. She sacrifices her kid each time she walks in.

## Science hooks (CDR, offered, not ruled)

- **The second heart.** The calf muscle pump is real: the soleus and gastrocnemius squeeze the deep veins with every step and push blood back up to the heart, which is why physiology calls the calf the second heart. A rig that ports there could pump the broth up through the diver's own venous return; she has to keep moving for it to flow.

- **The Mother.** A kombucha culture's floating mat is called the *mother* (a SCOBY pellicle, a symbiotic culture of bacteria and yeast). Name the goop's top film after it.
- **Radiotrophic film.** Melanized fungi found growing on the walls of Chernobyl's reactor appear to grow faster under gamma radiation (*Cryptococcus neoformans*, *Cladosporium sphaerospermum*). A film that eats radiation fits the dive: the hotter the zone, the more the Mother feeds.
- **Radiation-proof partners.** *Deinococcus radiodurans* rebuilds its own shattered DNA; the tardigrade's Dsup protein shields DNA. Either can be the belt-born resistance in the blood.
- **The broth.** Pluripotent stem cells under the film: the repair stock pushed down the line into a diver with acute radiation syndrome (the gut and the marrow fail first; a marrow transplant is the real-world fix).
- **The fetal thing.** A teratoma is a real tumor of disorganized tissue (hair, teeth, sometimes eye) grown from pluripotent cells. Left unspent, the broth organizes. Name the moment: the broth *quickens*.
- **Her own cells.** Genome-locked reads as *autologous*: grown from the patient's own cells, so the body never rejects it (induced pluripotent stem cells are made by rewinding a person's adult cells to the embryo-like state). A fetus from one parent's cells alone is a *parthenote*, the product of parthenogenesis, a virgin birth. Every spent broth is a parthenote she never carried.

## The heat — CDR draft model (10/1, offered, not ruled)

Chief 10/1: "let's figure out how the heat works … a scientific way this whole system can work … future tech … the canonical year is like 2199 or 2299. 2099 seems way too soon."

- **Where the heat comes from.** The Severance (nukes detonated in orbit, the Kessler cascade) leaves a shell of wreck around Earth that has been falling for a century: the Moon's orbital reactors, plutonium power cells (RTGs), warhead cores, Moon-built hulls. High-altitude bursts also pump artificial radiation belts (real: Starfish Prime, 1962).
- **Why it lands on the belt.** The Coast steers reentry onto the Power Line: the belt is the impact range. That is why it is built of space junk, why it is hot, and why the hot spots are craters.
- **What they dive for.** Moon material that only arrives by falling: helium-3-rich regolith ballast (fusion fuel), vacuum-forged alloys, and Moon-made parts Earth can no longer build. Earth's grid runs on it; the Severance cut the supply line, so the hot craters are the mine.
- **The grades (real units, sieverts per hour).** G0 background · G1 the belt's streets (millisieverts per hour; deadly over years to outsiders, nothing to the belt-born) · G2 work zones (~0.1 Sv/h; shifts measured in hours) · G3 crater rims (~1 Sv/h; an unshielded outsider is sick within the hour) · G4 **the heat**, crater cores (10+ Sv/h; minutes to a lethal dose; divers only). Real anchors: about 4–5 Sv at once kills half of those exposed; Chernobyl's Elephant's Foot read about 100 Sv/h in 1986.
- **Why the belt-born resist.** Inherited from the Moon: the first colonists were engineered for lunar surface radiation (no atmosphere, no magnetic field), with DNA-shielding and DNA-repair genes (real: the tardigrade's Dsup; *Deinococcus*). The Outliers carry it without knowing why. The same blood holds the sleeper mission.
- **What the dose does.** Radiation kills dividing cells first: marrow, then the gut lining (acute radiation syndrome). A diver isn't shielded; she is **resupplied**.
- **The rig.** The crawler parks at the G2/G3 line a quarter mile back: distance is shielding (inverse square law), and the Mother would die in the heat. The line runs in to her. The **spine ports** seat in the vertebrae, which hold much of the adult marrow, and stream broth straight into it: a bone-marrow transplant running live. The **calf ports** are the pump: the calf muscles squeeze the broth up the veins, so she has to keep moving or the flow stalls.
- **The Mother and the broth.** The film on top is radiotrophic (melanin, the Chernobyl fungi): it feeds on the dose that rides back down the line in her blood, and powers the broth. Beneath, her own pluripotent cells. Unspent, they organize and **quicken** into a parthenote. A hard dose breaks the quickened tissue down for parts: the kid is spent.
- **The year (rec).** OXO's present **2199**; the Severance **2099**, the turn of the century, one hundred years back. The Moon colony goes up in the 2050s–70s and has turned hell by the 2090s.
