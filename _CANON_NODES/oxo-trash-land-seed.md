## ⧗ The one-line read

**Trash Land is the rim of the Spiral.** The world is concentric — Core (Spaceport) → Middle Bands (Academy, industry) → Outer Bands (everglades, super-highways). A spiral needs an edge, and canon never named one. The trash range is the edge: a man-made Appalachia that turns the tri-state megalopolis into a closed system. **Legibility needs a sealed vessel; the range is the seal.**

## ⧗ Placement on the map

- **Where:** the northern rim of the Outer Bands, along the **Fall Line** — the real geological seam where the Coastal Plain ends and the Piedmont begins (Columbus → Macon → Augusta in Georgia, across central Alabama). The Spiral is coastal plain; the range is piled where the real mountains start. *A fake mountain range built at the foot of the real ones.*
- **Scale:** Appalachian proportions — a hundred-plus miles deep, the length of two states. Address form: `TRASHLAND.<cut>.<face>.<pit>`.
- **Parent / children:** parent = world (the Spiral); children = the **cuts** (the passes through it, the only routes north), the **faces** (the slopes worked by pickers), the **pits** (the interior, where the old is deepest).

## ⧗ Canon clicks — why this seed snaps in

- **The Spiral gets its rim.** Concentric geography with no edge was a gap; the range closes it and explains why the tri-state world is a world.
- **Deletion needs a landfill.** The series' core mechanic is people-as-assets, recall, deletion. Trash Land is deletion made physical. It gives the Boot Sequence's lie a place where the truth is buried by the ton.
- **The Diaspora rhyme.** The Student Diaspora is *the waste product of the school*; the first population under the range is the waste product of the Spiral. The handoff already proposed the Diaspora as *the second generation of discarded people, echoing the first*. The range is where the first generation went. That proposal now has a place to stand.
- **M1A salvage economy gets a source.** Victoria's opening world runs on salvage; canon never said salvage from where.
- **The countersign lands.** *"The Asset is acquired. The Noise is archived. The Feed is stable."* Trash Land is the archive of Noise.
- **Movements 5 and 6 get terrain.** Fight Club's low-tech guerrilla underground and The Pacific's war of attrition both want a lawless, unmapped, resource-rich no-man's-land. The range is that by definition.

## OPEN — all unruled

1. **The name.** Placeholder stands; bench above.
2. **Who built it, and why** (S7). Three candidates: **(a) the North dumped south** and the pile became the wall the South then claimed as its border · **(b) the Bishops built it** to seal the Spiral — legibility requires a closed system, and a wall of the Spiral's own waste is the cheapest wall there is · **(c) treaty landfill** — both sides dumped toward each other by agreement and the DMZ was the result. Recommend (b) *as the official story* with (a) *as the truth under it* — a founding lie, like every other founding in this world.
3. **The North.** The DMZ implies two polities. Is the North a separate nation, a seceded remainder, or the same country with a hard internal border? Canon has never named anything north of the Outer Bands.
4. **What is in the pits** (S10): recalled hardware — yes/no. If yes, this is the physical evidence engine for Movement 4's reveal and The Drop's *"he might be alive"* thread.
5. **Which Movement it enters.** Horizon in M1 (seen, not entered) · ground for M5 / M6 · or a Tori excursion in M3's forensic mode.
6. **The invariant** (S12): candidate — *"Nothing that enters the range comes back as what it was."* Rhymes with the Boot Sequence; check it against The Drop's "recall ≠ deletion" before ruling.
7. **The Fall Line placement** — confirm the real-geography anchor or move it.
