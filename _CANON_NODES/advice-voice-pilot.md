An adult woman creator writes in with her sign and the other person's sign, and THE VOICE answers from the heart, shameless and kind. ## Pilot 01

**Hook:** She wants it. He wants more.

**The question:** "I'm a Leo cam girl and I hooked up with a Scorpio guy from my chat. The sex was unreal. Now he texts me at 3 a.m. like he owns me. I like it, kind of? I don't want to catch feelings. How do I keep this hot without it getting heavy?"

**The read**
- Leo is Fire: a Fire Moon attaches through admiration and freedom, so she wants to be adored, not owned. (Map §L8 IMPRINT)
- Scorpio is Water: Water attaches with high intensity, enmeshed and all-in. (Map §L8 IMPRINT)
- Fire and Water make steam or smoke. Both are Fixed signs, so neither backs down first. (Astro, modalities; common lore)

**Answer skeleton**
- Name it: this is a heat she picked, not a trap.
- Tell her Scorpio hears "casual" as "secret." He needs a clear rule, not a hint.
- She is Leo. She needs to be wanted out loud. Ask for that.
- Set the 3 a.m. rule: late texts are for fun, not for check-ins.
- If she stays curious, she can keep it hot. If she goes numb, she is done.
- Close on: wanting someone is not owing them.

**Subtitle lines**
- Leo wants to be adored.
- Scorpio wants to be all in.
- That's fire meeting deep water.
- Both of you are stubborn.
- Say the rule out loud.
- Late texts can be play.
- Hot does not mean heavy.
- You picked this heat.
- Keep it fun or walk.

**Sign-off:** "Stay shameless, babe. The stars already saw it."
