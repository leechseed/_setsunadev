# OXO TELLING PROFILE · series instance (all answers ⧗)

**What this is.** The discourse twin of the storyform, instanced for the ruled medium. The medium law (Module 4): a storyform survives adaptation, a Telling Profile does not. So every question below is asked as *what the choice means on screen*, not on the page. **Canon inputs:** Failure/Good · Tori = Change/Stop/Do-er MC (Activity/Understanding) · Anna = IC, "a demonstration, not an argument" · OS = Situation/The Past, Consequence = Memories · tone RULED 007/Kojima blockbuster sincerity (the Drop, 8/26) · the six movements and their palette · P1/P2 Modern Poetics. Every ⧗ line is a Claude draft for Papi to strike or keep.

**The one-line profile ⧗:** *No human narrator. The Feed speaks; Tori sees. The past is earned, never given. Cut like a racing film, glow like a scoreboard, mean every word.*

## T1 · WHO SPEAKS · voice / narration
- **The question:** who tells, from where, how reliably, in what tense.
- **On screen:** VO or none · a diegetic system voice · "previously on" as series voice · title-card voice.
- **⧗ Recommended:** **no human VO, ever.** The only spoken narration is **the Feed / the Administration's declarative voice** (canon: "they do not ask questions, they state states") · intradiegetic, simultaneous (present tense), **unreliable by doctrine** (the Reset is the first lie). *Reason:* Tori's Saturn-in-Gemini "speech is a cage" makes a Tori VO a chart violation; the medium says interiority is performed, never stated; and a narrator who forecasts while the picture shows her intervening **is** Interdiction vs. Prediction as a discourse device.
- **Menu:** Tori VO, subsequent (memoir register; risks Failure reading as elegy) · Diaspora chorus VO from M5 on (the "distributed processing network," polyphonic) · the Feed VO retired at M4 when she is erased (the narrator loses her).

## T2 · WHO SEES · focalization
- **The question:** through whose eyes the camera knows what it knows.
- **On screen:** shot selection · one focal character per episode or variable · surveillance-eye inserts · Feed graphics as a second lens.
- **⧗ Recommended:** **Tori-fixed internal as the series default**: she is in every scene, the camera never knows more than she does. **One licensed second lens:** the Feed's surveillance eye (external, surfaces only, overlaid with rank/Verified graphics), used as an instrument in cold opens and Administration beats, never as a character POV. **Anna gets no POV.** *Reason:* Growth = Stop means the audience must live inside the meritocratic belief to feel it collapse; and an IC with interiority becomes a second MC, which the ruling forbids ("a demonstration, not an argument").
- **Menu:** variable internal (Tori / Anna alternating episodes; the braided pattern) · zero focalization via the Feed's omniscience (the surveillance state as narrator-eye; strong for M4, corrosive before it) · Riley-focalized episodes (the Witness as camera).

## T3 · IN WHAT ORDER · order across episodes
- **The question:** story time vs broadcast time; which anachronies are licensed.
- **On screen:** linear vs the legacy "Scramble" (Lane 1 Truth / Lane 2 Puzzle) · flashback as footage · trans-movement cold opens (legacy tag `#TransMovementColdOpen`) · information gates (the cyborg reveal must not leak before M4's episode).
- **⧗ Recommended:** **linear across episodes, with exactly one licensed class of analepsis: recovered data.** A flashback is only ever an in-world playback (the Sync-dive, the deleted crash footage, the Feed's edited funeral). The past arrives as evidence, never as free exposition. Prolepsis only as paratext (a cold open from a later movement, if ruled under T8). *Reason:* Goal = The Past, Consequence = Memories: if the telling hands the audience the past for free, the story's Goal has no value. Linear PSS Tori should live in a linear telling; the puzzle belongs to the world, not the cut.
- **Menu:** the full legacy Scramble (nonlinear broadcast order, Final Draft Lane 2) · linear with one framing prolepsis per movement (M6 cold open in the pilot: the Failure shown, the Good withheld) · strictly linear, no anachrony at all.

## T4 · AT WHAT SPEED · pacing per act
- **The question:** the pattern of scene / summary / stretch / pause / ellipsis; the edit is the instrument.
- **On screen:** cut rate per movement · montage policy · act-out setpieces (P5: at act breaks, never crowded).
- **⧗ Recommended:** **scene-speed default, each movement owning one signature move keyed to its genre:** M1 racing tempo installs the house baseline (pace-note cadence as cut rhythm) · **stretch is reserved for Sync** (dilation = trance; V-Sync in M3 stretches until it hurts) · **ellipsis is the Administration's weapon: the Ban is a hard cut**, a person removed mid-scene and never returned to · M5 runs iterative montage · M6 attrition stretch. *Reason:* Driver = Action, Do-er MC: the cut carries the story's motor; giving the antagonist the edit's ellipsis puts "the Elimination of Noise" into the grammar itself.
- **Menu:** uniform prestige-drama gait (scene-speed throughout, no signature moves) · montage-forward (Social Network register for M2, sustained) · the Drop's comic stretch (the scream) as a recurring duration gag series-wide.

## T5 · HOW OFTEN · frequency as motif
- **The question:** singulative, repetitive, iterative; the serial apparatus of recap.
- **On screen:** "previously on" · the crash retold · ritual montage · repeated shots as motif.
- **⧗ Recommended:** **the crash is the series' repetitive motif**: told n times, each time a different version (the Reset's funeral, the Feed's cut, the recovered footage, the Sync-dive), converging on the truth in M3. **The "previously on" is in-world: the Feed's classification recap** (Potential → Proprietary Salvage → Critical Error → Fault Line), so the recap voice is the antagonist filing her. **Campus ritual renders iteratively** (rating updates, Sync sessions) as S8 HABIT. *Reason:* Consequence = Memories; repetition-with-variation is the argument about who owns memory, staged as apparatus.
- **Menu:** conventional recap (network voice) · no recap (streaming-native, cold open only) · the crash shown once, in full, in the pilot (forfeits M3's forensic engine).

## T6 · IN WHAT WORDS · register
- **The question:** diction, dialogue design, distance (show vs tell).
- **On screen:** dialogue register per faction · action-line register in the screenplay · how much is said vs shown.
- **⧗ Recommended:** **three dialogue registers, canon-keyed:** **Axiom** (the Administration: declarative, no questions, "the Asset is acquired") · **Noise** (the Diaspora: a single brain debugging, overlapping, unfinished) · **Truth** (Tori: Expressive Range 2, few words, all of them true, said when they should not be). Southern Gothic diction for the Delta Coast and the legacy bloc. **Distance: maximal mimesis; nobody explains the world; the Feed's overlays are the only exposition surface.** Action lines in blockbuster-sincerity register: present tense, kinetic, no winking in the lines themselves. *Reason:* Tori's Critical Flaw is Truth and her Truth Exposure Index is 18; dialogue design must make speech cost her. The Kojima move is earnest in the frame, never ironic in the mouth.
- **Menu:** quip register (Marvel-proper; retired by the tone ruling) · naturalist overlapping dialogue throughout · heightened theatrical (KLK declamation) for the Administration only.

## T7 · WITH WHAT FEELING · tone and mood curve
- **The question:** the implied author's attitude; the audience-side weather over the season.
- **On screen:** register, score, palette, how much fun is permitted inside the fall.
- **⧗ Recommended:** **adopt the Drop's ruled register series-wide:** 007/Kojima blockbuster sincerity, "a movie you rewatch over and over," deadly earnest and self-aware in the same frame. **Mood curve = the movement color chart:** Burnt Orange → Cyan/White → Deep Purple → Electric Blue → Olive Green → Blood Red; palette descent clean → NEON-ROT → hunt. **The ending's feeling is Personal Triumph:** meaning survives defeat, so M6 must be sincere or it reads as misery. *Reason:* the Drop seed left the register's scope open; P1 Entertainment-First and the seed's own note (the tone ruling restates P1) argue it is the house register, not a side-story quirk. Failure/Good needs fun as a load-bearing value to land as Good.
- **Menu:** prestige-grim (the register the storyform would drift to unattended) · the Drop's register scoped to side stories only · tonal shift by movement (sincere M1–M2, ironic M3, grim M4–M6).

## T8 · THROUGH WHAT THRESHOLDS · paratext
- **The question:** titles, cards, cold opens, codex, on-screen text.
- **On screen:** episode titles · movement cards · cold-open policy · in-world documents · chyrons and overlays.
- **⧗ Recommended:** **episode titles in Axiom grammar** (three to four declarative words in the Administration's voice: "The Asset Is Acquired") · **a movement card at each movement boundary, in its chart color** · **on-screen text belongs to the Feed only** (rank, Verified, classification labels); no expository chyrons outside the Feed's UI · **cold opens are the Feed's eye** (T2 license) · codex = the in-world documents (the Ecclesial Laws, the Star-Rating rule sheet) surfaced as props, never as end cards. *Reason:* "Power is worn on the body" and legibility is the system's weapon; making every threshold the Feed's makes the paratext itself the antagonist's, and the movement cards carry the six-movement invariant set as a visible rhythm.
- **Menu:** Tori-voiced titles (her Truth register; conflicts with T1) · numbered episodes only, no titles · movement names as season subtitles instead of cards · trans-movement cold opens (legacy PSTO logic; pairs with the T3 prolepsis option).

## T9 · BREAKING WHICH FRAMES · metalepsis policy
- **The question:** may the telling acknowledge it is a telling.
- **On screen:** fourth-wall address · tonal frame-breaks (the show reminding you it is a show) · UI addressing the viewer.
- **⧗ Recommended:** **tonal frame-breaks permitted, diegetic metalepsis forbidden.** The 007 wink lives in staging, score, spectacle and paratext (the Drop's stretched-comic scream is the model), never in a character looking at the lens. *Reason:* Failure/Good is cheapened the moment the fall is acknowledged as a performance from inside the diegesis; the Kojima move keeps the frame earnest.
- **Menu:** one licensed exception: the Feed's overlay addresses the viewer as a Verified user (second-person UI; fuses with T10, implicates the audience in the legibility regime) · full metalepsis in M5 (the Diaspora's propaganda breaks the frame) · never, including tonal.

## T10 · TO WHOM · narratee and implied viewer
- **The question:** who the telling addresses inside the text; what viewer it assumes.
- **On screen:** direct address (none / some) · the audience contract · who the recap is for.
- **⧗ Recommended:** **implied viewer = the American Center (P2, ruled doctrine):** rooted, localized, specific; the Delta Coast is the spine, not the backdrop. **Narratee = none explicit**; the Feed's recaps are addressed to the Administration, overheard by the viewer. *Reason:* P2 is canon; overhearing the system file its own asset is the viewer's position for the whole series.
- **Menu:** the viewer as Feed user (rides the T9 exception) · the Diaspora's future listener (M6's bridge to the Future War / Ghost Mission; the record as narratee) · the narratee as the dead brother (Tori's Truth lines addressed to Jebb; conflicts with T1's no-VO).
