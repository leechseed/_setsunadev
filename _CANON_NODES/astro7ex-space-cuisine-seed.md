# THE SPACE CUISINE SEED

**The idea as spoken.** The people up there — in space and on the moon — perhaps eat only preserved foods, and only the ones they preserved themselves. Cuisine goes under setting (ruled 9/6); this seed is the row set, waiting for a place to hang on.

## The three rows

| Layer | The seed |
|---|---|
| **S6 ECONOMY** (who feeds it) | A closed loop. Nothing fresh arrives; everything eaten was put up by someone on station, from what the station grows or receives in bulk. Preservation is the food economy, not a hedge against it. The real-world floor: NASA's shelf-life problem (18 months for station food, five years wanted for Mars), submarine patrol stores, Antarctic winter-over pantries, sailing-ship provisioning (salt, hardtack, scurvy). |
| **S8 HABIT** (the patterned life) | Putting-up as ritual: the harvest-and-preserve cycle is the calendar. Who preserves for whom is the belonging gradient — you eat what your people jarred. Monotony is the enemy the culture organizes against (the documented problem of expedition food); the fermented and the smoked are the flavor of home because they are the only flavor there is. |
| **S3 SENSORIUM** (how it meets the senses) | The smell of brine, smoke, and koji in recycled air; the sound of seals; food that is dense, salty, sour, umami-heavy, nothing crisp. Sensorium built from the field map's levers: salt, acid, ferment, smoke, oil-pack, retort. |

## Open

1. **Which place.** The moon societies (`moon-saga-seed`) are the obvious host if MOON/SAGA is ASTRO7EX's moon. Unruled.
2. **What they cannot make.** No cold-smoke without smoke handling; no open ferment without containment. The constraints are the cuisine's character — rule them when the station exists.
3. **The Earth mirror.** Trash Land's people (S6: the Spiral feeds it) eat what? The same seed inverted — ground truth for the Outliers, if wanted.
