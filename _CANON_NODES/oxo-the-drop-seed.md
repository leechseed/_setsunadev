# THE DROP — side-story seed

> *Fall below the line and the school throws you down the hole. Everyone hears the scream. Nobody talks about the scream.*

## The mechanic

- The Ultra School's **ranking list** has a floor. Fall below a certain rank and you are **killed — because every student carries top-secret information**. Attrition as classification policy.
- The method is **ritualistic**: thrown down an enormous hole — thousands of feet — the Moon-Door image (Game of Thrones). Kicked off. The fall is long enough to hear.
- **The scream is the signature**: stretched, discordant, doppler-warped — *almost comedic*. "Oh my god, what the fuuuuck—" receding. Horror delivered in a register that's nearly funny. (Tone doctrine below makes this deliberate, not accidental.)

## The story beats (as spoken)

1. **It happens to someone they like** — someone they couldn't help. Establishes the mechanic with a casualty that costs.
2. **The etiquette layer**: campus social-media culture holds it *disrespectful to acknowledge what you heard*. The scream is public; mentioning it is a violation. (Politeness as complicity.)
3. **The trope inversion — boy-who-cried-wolf shape**: Tori: "didn't you hear that sound?" Others: "…I heard it too." But: "I need proof before we tell everyone he might be alive."
4. **The expert**: they take it to a student who is the school's savant at the **Sync-dive** — the in-world analog of Cyberpunk 2077's braindance (memory/sensory playback editing). Staged like an oracle's table, palm-reader dressing.
5. **The subversion beat**: before any evidence — the girl simply says, ***"I believe you."*** The oracle framing set up for skepticism pays off in immediate faith. Funny, warm, disarming.

## Tone — RULED 2026-08-26 (the one ruling in this seed)

## Canon clicks (why this seed snaps in clean)

- **The ranking floor = S4 LAW weaponized** — Star-Rating with a kill threshold; the Administration's punitive-removal doctrine (what killed Jebb) made *ritual and public*.
- **"He might be alive" is in-world plausible** — death is a **Boot Sequence** in this universe (death-as-product-recall, Master Bible). The trope-story's hope isn't naive: recall ≠ deletion. The kids are accidentally doing forensics on the world's core mechanic.
- **The Sync-dive savant = V-Sync tech given a face** — M3's V-Sync trances already exist; a student who *edits* the stream is a new character seed with an existing tech substrate.
- **The scream etiquette = S8 HABIT × legibility** — a ritual of not-hearing, the campus version of the Diaspora's scarred-over tattoos.
- **Tori initiating the "did you hear it" chain** fits her M3 forensic mode (digging for deleted data) and her Truth flaw — she says the unsayable, again.
