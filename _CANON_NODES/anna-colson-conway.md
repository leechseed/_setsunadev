# Anna Colson Conway

> **She is not an argument. She is a demonstration.** — [[oxo-storyform]] §4

## REORIENT — where the feeling is

Cyan-white, then Verified blue — but the blue sits on her like a uniform she was issued for good behavior, not a color she fought for. No grease, no crash, no salvage economy. She is legible to the system because she was never anything else. Where Tori's file starts with an anomaly the Feed had to go looking for, Anna's file reads clean from page one: nothing to acquire, because she offered it.

She does not narrate. [[oxo-telling-profile]] T2 gives her no POV — the camera is Tori-fixed, with one licensed second lens, the Feed's surveillance eye. So Anna exists on screen exactly the way the Administration's file on her exists: from outside, in overlays and outcomes, never in confession. What she says, when she says anything, lands in the Feed's own register — declarative, no hedging, no interrogative. **⧗ Proposed: she has adopted Axiom as her own idiolect, not been assigned it.** *Reason: Fixed Attitude is an unshakeable operating premise, not an office — the difference between her and the Administration's actual voice is that she means every word of it personally, where the Feed means none of them.*

The thesis she carries is not hers to argue. It is hers to be true in front of Tori, scene after scene, without raising her voice:

> **Power ascends. Fairness was never the operating system.** — [[oxo-storyform]] §4

Tori has to learn this. Anna has already stopped needing to learn anything.
