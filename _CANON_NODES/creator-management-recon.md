# Creator management recon — the licensed-agent era vs. the OFM lane

Business and legal side only. Every figure is tagged **HIGH / MEDIUM / LOW**; figures from agency or vendor blogs are tagged **(agency-sourced)**. Pronouns are they/them unless a source shows otherwise.

## 0 · Bottom line

- **Two different businesses share the word "agency."** The studio-era agency is a **licensed booking agent**: it procures paid shoots from third-party producers and takes **10–15%** ([THR, Nov 2012](https://www.hollywoodreporter.com/news/general-news/clients-are-porn-stars-388523/), HIGH). The fansite-era "OFM" agency is an **operations vendor** running DMs, traffic and posting on the creator's *own* account for **20–50%+** ([Desirely 2026](https://www.desirely.co/en/blog/onlyfans-agency-commission-rates), MEDIUM, agency-sourced). Nobody licenses the second one; the platforms recognize it only as "you remain legally responsible."
- **The commission gap is the whole story.** The 2022 "standard" for a legit account manager was **5–15%** per consultant Amberly Rothfield ([Rolling Stone, 18 Jan 2022](https://www.rollingstone.com/culture/culture-features/onlyfans-model-management-industry-exploitation-1073615/), MEDIUM); the same piece documents a manager taking **66%**. Riley Reid launched her own agency in 2021 because friends were paying "up to 30%" ([AVN via TopCreator, Apr 2021](https://topcreator.net/riley-reid-launches-a-s-h-agency/), MEDIUM).
- **Florida licensing:** ch. 468 Part VII licenses anyone who "for compensation, engages in the occupation or business of procuring, or attempting to procure, engagements for an artist," and "artist" includes "a model" ([§468.401](https://www.flsenate.gov/Laws/Statutes/2025/468.401), HIGH). Running a creator's own subscription account is not obviously "procuring an engagement"; booking that creator into a collab, studio shoot or brand deal plainly is. There is a **one-artist exemption** ([§468.403(1)](https://www.flsenate.gov/Laws/Statutes/2025/468.403), HIGH). **Attorney question, not an answer.**
- **Platform posture:** OnlyFans' Terms (last updated Aug 2024) say assistance "does not affect your legal responsibility. Our relationship is with you, and not with any third-party" ([onlyfans.com/terms](https://onlyfans.com/terms), HIGH). No official OnlyFans agency registry was found. **Fanvue** is the only one of the three with a published Agency–Talent Agreement, native manager accounts and on-platform payment splits ([legal.fanvue.com](https://legal.fanvue.com/agency-talent-agreement), HIGH). **Fansly** has native per-permission grants (documented by third parties); its official agency policy page was not retrievable this pass.
- **Reputation, not capital, is the entry barrier.** The cold-outreach OFM lane has been mapped to manosphere YouTube/Discord courses ([Prism, 8 Jan 2024](https://prismreports.org/2024/01/08/onlyfans-management-schemes-youtube-manosphere/)), a Reuters chatter investigation ([30 Jul 2024](https://www.reuters.com/investigates/special-report/onlyfans-sex-chatters/)), two subscriber class actions (2024–25) and a Filipino-labor exposé ([Rappler, 5 Feb 2026](https://www.rappler.com/newsbreak/in-depth/shady-onlyfans-agencies-put-filipino-workers-risk/)). Creators now vet on password policy, content ownership and exit terms.
- **Creator-first is a documented path.** Jameson (ClubJenna, 2000), Angel (BurningAngel, 2002), Reid (A.S.H., 2021), Nelson (Autumn's Angels, ~2021), Bop House (2024), and self-described Teasy / Louna's / Wonderland. The origin of the whole OFM lane, per Rothfield, was literally network-shaped: "your boyfriend is running your account, would he like to run mine, I'll give him a percentage" ([RS 2022](https://www.rollingstone.com/culture/culture-features/onlyfans-model-management-industry-exploitation-1073615/)). Common precondition before anyone signed: **visible own results plus referral trust.**
- **For the reader's $0 setup:** a disclosed 3D-rendered persona is the operator's *own* account — no agency relationship exists yet, so no licensing trigger and no chatter-impersonation exposure (nobody is being told a human is typing). The moment a second *human* creator is booked into anything with a third-party payer, the ch. 468 question goes live. The 18 U.S.C. §2257 definitions turn on depictions of "an actual human being" ([28 CFR 75.1](https://www.law.cornell.edu/cfr/text/28/75.1)) — another attorney question for a rendered persona.

## 1 · The traditional path (studio era, roughly pre-2016)

**What it was.** A licensed talent agency booked performers into shoots for third-party producers, negotiated a flat per-scene rate, and took a commission. The San Fernando Valley industry was "led by a cadre of talent agencies that cut deals and jockey to represent the stars of tomorrow" ([THR, Nov 2012](https://www.hollywoodreporter.com/news/general-news/clients-are-porn-stars-388523/)).

**The licensing requirement.** California's Talent Agencies Act, Labor Code §1700.4, defines a talent agency as "a person or corporation who engages in the occupation of procuring, offering, promising, or attempting to procure employment or engagements for an artist" ([leginfo](https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/codes_displaySection.xhtml?lawCode=LAB&sectionNum=1700.4), HIGH); §1700.5 makes doing so unlicensed unlawful. Licensees submit fingerprints and references, keep an inspectable office, post a surety bond, and file their fee schedule with the Labor Commissioner ([CA DLSE](https://www.dir.ca.gov/dlse/talent_agency_license.html), HIGH). By 2012 "14 agencies are licensed and bonded" versus "two or three reputable adult agencies 10 years ago" ([THR 2012](https://www.hollywoodreporter.com/news/general-news/clients-are-porn-stars-388523/), HIGH). The licensed shops formed LATATA (Licensed Adult Talent Agency Trade Association) in 2009, "led by L.A. Direct," seven members, to "ferret out rogue agents" ([THR 2012](https://www.hollywoodreporter.com/news/general-news/clients-are-porn-stars-388523/), HIGH).

**Commission — verified.**
- "Top agents can pull down $250,000 a year from their **10 to 15 percent** takes" — THR, Nov 2012, HIGH. Spiegler "takes 10 to 15 percent, receiving the larger percentage if he handles transportation for the client." Unlicensed "suitcase pimps" "take commissions of more than 20 percent, which is the maximum allowed by California's Labor Commissioner" (THR; the 20% cap is a reporter's statement, MEDIUM — the DLSE page describes fee schedules submitted for approval rather than a fixed statutory cap).
- Current guide sites still put agent commission at "typically 10–15%" ([Before Your Scene, reviewed 26 Apr 2026](https://www.beforeyourscene.com/learn/how-much-pay), LOW — guide site, not trade press).
- The reader's prior belief (10–15%) is **confirmed** for the licensed studio-era agent.

**Manager vs. agent.** APAG: "Only an agent may book work for you, arrange casting calls, or negotiate deals. Managers are not allowed to do so" ([APAG](https://apagunion.com/performer-concerns/licensed-adult-talent-agents/), HIGH). In law, a personal manager advises and develops a career but may not procure work; *Marathon Entertainment v. Blasi* (Cal. 2008) held the Act applies to managers who procure, while allowing the Labor Commissioner to sever rather than void the whole contract for incidental procurement ([FindLaw](https://caselaw.findlaw.com/court/ca-supreme-court/1190441.html), HIGH).

**The day-rate economy.** Performers "charge set rates, so the conversation often centers on the availability of an actor" — a flat per-scene fee agreed before the shoot, tiered by scene category, roughly $800–$1,200+ per scene for an in-demand performer in 2012 ([THR 2012](https://www.hollywoodreporter.com/news/general-news/clients-are-porn-stars-388523/), HIGH for 2012). Current guide figures: new female performers $800–$1,500 per scene, established $1,500–$3,000+, male performers starting $300–$500; "you get paid the day of the shoot" ([Before Your Scene 2026](https://www.beforeyourscene.com/learn/how-much-pay), LOW). Spiegler said average female annual income had fallen from ~$100K to "as little as $50,000" by 2012; XBIZ's Dan Miller put 70–80% of a performer's revenue in movies, the rest in dancing, toys and "personal website subscriptions" (THR 2012, MEDIUM) — the seed of the next era. Agents also handled websites, transport and testing logistics (THR 2012).

## 2 · The new path (fansite era, 2016→)

**Where it came from.** "Before the pandemic it was just major stars and their boyfriends who ran their accounts… Then a girl would be like 'Hey, your boyfriend is running your account, would he like to run mine, I'll give him a percentage.' More and more people started getting into it" — Amberly Rothfield (xie/xir), creator-business consultant ([RS, 18 Jan 2022](https://www.rollingstone.com/culture/culture-features/onlyfans-model-management-industry-exploitation-1073615/), HIGH as a quote). OnlyFans reported a 75% jump in sign-ups in April 2020 alone (same piece).

**The service bundle** (consistent across press and agency sites): DM/PPV sales ("chatting"), traffic (social funnels, paid ads, shout-outs), content planning/scheduling, account operations, sometimes production. VICE's 2023 reporting: chatters work 4–12 hour shifts managing "three or four creators at a time," sourced from Pakistan, India and the Philippines ([VICE, 24 Oct 2023](https://www.vice.com/en/article/onlyfans-management-agency-chatters/), HIGH). Rest of World (2025): agencies in the US and Europe employ contract chatters in the Philippines and Kenya; "dozens of new job ads… every day on Reddit and Facebook"; sales quotas up 25% in six months ([Rest of World 2025](https://restofworld.org/2025/onlyfans-ai-dm-bots/), HIGH).

- **Net vs. gross matters more than the headline.** Most agencies quote against net-of-platform (after OnlyFans' 20%); some quote against gross ([Aruna](https://arunatalent.com/blog/onlyfans-agency-commission-rates/), agency-sourced). A "50%" full-service deal leaves the creator ~40% of gross.
- **Flag:** every 2026 "norm" above comes from agencies or agency-directory blogs whose interest is to normalize 30–50%. The only independent anchors are the 2022–2023 press figures (5–25%).

**How people enter.**
- **Courses / Discords / YouTube.** Prism traced creators' inbox spam to Spanish-language YouTuber Sergi Berenguer (Discord "Pimp School," Telegram "OF Models for Sale") and to Kyle Plummer's "How To Start An OnlyFans Agency In 2022 (Step By Step)": form a company, build a website, hire cold-messengers on Upwork, send "100 cold-messages a day," and "take over the creators' bank accounts for better control of their income" ([Prism, 8 Jan 2024](https://prismreports.org/2024/01/08/onlyfans-management-schemes-youtube-manosphere/), HIGH). Courses sell on Gumroad, Udemy and Whop from ~$20 to ~$1,000 ([Udemy listing](https://www.udemy.com/course/onlyfans-management-agency-course-from-beginner-to-pro/); [Whop review](https://scribehow.com/page/Is_6_Figure_OFM_on_Whop_a_Scam_2026_Verdict__tFnjUav8R0a4mlc4AKO0Ow), LOW).
- **Tooling vendors** (Infloww, OnlyMonster, SuperCreator) publish "how to start an agency" guides and CRM/multi-login products ([SuperCreator](https://www.supercreator.app/guides/how-to-start-an-onlyfans-agency), vendor).
- **Creators recruiting friends** — see §3.

**Scale of the lane.** OnlyFans paid creators **$6.3B** in FY2025 (to 30 Nov 2025) on $7.2B gross; 5,076 creators have earned >$1M lifetime; >$30B paid out since 2016 ([Variety, 2026](https://variety.com/2026/digital/news/onlyfans-millionaires-creators-earned-more-than-1-million-1236842444/), HIGH). One agency-directory estimate puts OFM agency revenue at **$380M–$1.25B/yr** assuming 30–40% of significant earners are managed ([Desirely](https://www.desirely.co/en/blog/onlyfans-statistics), **LOW, agency-sourced**). No independent count of agencies exists; Reuters found NEO Agency alone managing ~70 creators and "roughly 20 other agencies" using AI chat tools ([Reuters via Yahoo, 30 Jul 2024](https://finance.yahoo.com/news/ai-bots-talk-dirty-onlyfans-110852316.html), HIGH).

**The reputational problem.**
- **Exploitation reporting:** RS 2022 (contracts with sunset clauses and non-competes, managers continuing to post as the creator after termination, lock-outs, a Florida-addressed "agency" that was one Facebook page). Unruly Agency, founded by influencer Tara "Elektra" Niknejad (she) and Nicky Gathrite (he), was sued in 2021–22 by creators alleging content posted without consent and exit threats; a separate ex-employee suit alleged managers were told to pose as models and were misclassified as contractors ([RS, 15 Mar 2022](https://www.rollingstone.com/culture/culture-news/onlyfans-creators-unruly-agency-nudity-lawsuit-1320185/), HIGH). Unruly's own May 2026 press release says the claims were dismissed in its favor ([NatLawReview press release](https://natlawreview.com/press-releases/unruly-agency-lawsuit-dismissed-baseless-emerges-stronger-poised-growth-new), LOW — self-issued).
- **Reuters, 30 Jul 2024:** two special reports — one on human "chatters" impersonating creators ([reuters.com](https://www.reuters.com/investigates/special-report/onlyfans-sex-chatters/)), one on AI bots doing it; OnlyFans' terms say "You cannot use an AI chatbot to write chats or direct messages," and OnlyFans told Reuters creators "can choose a third party to run their accounts but remain 'legally responsible'" ([Reuters on X, 30 Jul 2024](https://x.com/Reuters/status/1818381075745325243); [Yahoo mirror](https://finance.yahoo.com/news/ai-bots-talk-dirty-onlyfans-110852316.html), HIGH).
- **Subscriber class actions:** *N.Z. v. Fenix International* (C.D. Cal. 8:24-cv-01655, filed July 2024, Hagens Berman) named agency defendants; on 12 Dec 2025 the court dismissed fraud claims against OnlyFans because "Fenix Defendants made explicit disclosures regarding the use of third parties," dismissed wiretap claims, applied §230 to "facilitating communication," and granted leave to amend ([Eric Goldman, 21 Dec 2025](https://blog.ericgoldman.org/archives/2025/12/onlyfans-defeats-chatter-scam-claim-n-z-v-fenix.htm); [Hagens Berman](https://www.hbsslaw.com/cases/onlyfans-chatters), HIGH). A second suit, *Brunner & Fry v. Fenix* (N.D. Ill., 2025), pleads fraud, contract and unjust enrichment ([Court Watch, 2 Apr 2025](https://www.courtwatch.news/p/onlyfans-sued-after-two-guys-realized-they-might-not-actually-be-talking-to-models), HIGH).
- **Labor exposés:** Filipino chatters at ~$2/hour with productivity deductions, classified as "freelancers" outside the Labor Code ([Rappler, 5 Feb 2026](https://www.rappler.com/newsbreak/in-depth/shady-onlyfans-agencies-put-filipino-workers-risk/), HIGH); Fortune on AI replacing chat workers ([Fortune, 5 Apr 2024](https://fortune.com/2024/04/05/ai-is-coming-for-the-onlyfans-chat-industry)).

## 4 · The legal shape

**(a) Management company vs. licensed talent agency.**
- **California:** §1700.4 "procure… employment or engagements for an artist"; *Marathon v. Blasi* — managers who procure fall under the Act; incidental procurement may be severed rather than voiding the whole contract ([FindLaw](https://caselaw.findlaw.com/court/ca-supreme-court/1190441.html), HIGH). Contracts for unlicensed procurement can be voided by the Labor Commissioner with disgorgement — the classic manager risk.
- **Florida, ch. 468 Part VII — what the statute says (HIGH, verbatim):**
  - "'Talent agency' means any person who, for compensation, engages in the occupation or business of procuring, or attempting to procure, engagements for an artist." "'Artist' means a person performing on the professional stage or in the production of television, radio, or motion pictures; a musician or group of musicians; or a model." "'Engagement' means any employment or placement of an artist, where the artist performs in his or her artistic capacity" ([§468.401](https://www.flsenate.gov/Laws/Statutes/2025/468.401)).
  - "A person may not own, operate, solicit business, or otherwise engage in or carry on the occupation of a talent agency in this state unless the person first procures a license… A license is not required for a person who acts as an agent for herself or himself, a family member, or exclusively for one artist" — but such a person may not advertise as a "talent agency" or "talent agent" ([§468.403](https://www.flsenate.gov/Laws/Statutes/2025/468.403)).
  - Mechanics: application fee ≤ $300 plus fingerprints; one year of relevant experience; five character affidavits ([§468.403](https://www.flsenate.gov/Laws/Statutes/2025/468.403)); $5,000 surety bond; licensure $400, renewal $405; "normally processed within two weeks" ([DBPR FAQ](https://www2.myfloridalicense.com/talent-agencies/faqs/), HIGH).
  - "A talent agency may not charge a registration fee"; must give the artist a contract copy within 24 hours "which lists the services to be provided and the fees to be charged"; **no statutory commission cap** ([§468.410](https://www.flsenate.gov/Laws/Statutes/2025/468.410)). No false advertising; no fee-splitting "without the written consent of the artist"; no sending anyone to a place "resorted to for the purposes of prostitution" ([§468.412](https://www.flsenate.gov/Laws/Statutes/2025/468.412)).
  - **Does managing a creator's own subscription account fall under it?** The statute keys on *procuring engagements* — "employment or placement" with a *buyer/employer*. Chatting, posting and running ads on the creator's own account involves no third-party employer, so on the text it does not look like procurement. But (i) a subscription creator is plausibly a "model"; (ii) booking collabs, studio shoots, cam-site deals or brand deals *is* placement; (iii) Florida's DBPR has not been found to have ruled on fansite management either way. **Attorney question.** The one-artist exemption is directly relevant to a first signing.
- **Practical takeaway:** most OFM agencies operate as unlicensed *management/services* companies and stay out of booking; a roster that includes shoot-booking needs the license conversation in whichever state the agency and the creator sit.

**(b) 18 U.S.C. §2257 / 28 CFR 75.** A "secondary producer" is "any person who produces, assembles, manufactures, publishes, duplicates, reproduces, or reissues" matter containing a covered depiction; "primary producer" is who "actually films, videotapes, photographs, or creates a digitally- or computer-manipulated image… of a visual depiction of an actual human being"; mere distribution, hosting and "dissemination of a depiction without having created it or altered its content" are excluded ([28 CFR 75.1](https://www.law.cornell.edu/cfr/text/28/75.1), HIGH). Contracting a custodian "does not relieve the producer of his liability"; records kept five years after dissolution ([28 CFR 75](https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-28/chapter-I/part-75), HIGH). The Third Circuit held the requirements unconstitutional only *as applied* to plaintiffs aged 30+ and vacated a nationwide injunction, so the statute stands generally ([FSC v. AG, 3d Cir. 1 Sep 2020](https://law.justia.com/cases/federal/appellate-courts/ca3/18-3188/18-3188-2020-09-01.html), HIGH). An agency that re-posts, edits or re-issues a creator's content is exposed to secondary-producer status; a persona that depicts no "actual human being" sits outside the §2257 definition on its face and inside §2257A's simulated-conduct regime only if a human is depicted — **attorney question** for a rendered persona.

**(c) Chatters.**
- **Classification (US):** the DOL's 2024 "totality of the circumstances" rule (eff. 11 Mar 2024) is no longer enforced by DOL since FAB 2025-1 (May 2025) and a 26 Feb 2026 NPRM proposes rescinding it, but it "remains in place for private litigants" until rescinded ([DOL](https://www.dol.gov/agencies/whd/flsa/misclassification/2026rulemaking); [Foley](https://www.foley.com/insights/publications/2025/05/dol-alters-enforcement-position-independent-contractors/), HIGH). Unruly's ex-employees sued alleging misclassification as contractors ([RS 2022](https://www.rollingstone.com/culture/culture-news/onlyfans-creators-unruly-agency-nudity-lawsuit-1320185/)). Scheduled shifts, quotas and scripts are control factors.
- **Overseas norms:** contractor status paid via Wise/Payoneer; $1–5/hour, 12–14-hour shifts ([Street Grace interview](https://www.streetgrace.org/research/victoria-sinis), LOW); ~$2/hour with productivity deductions ([Rappler 2026](https://www.rappler.com/newsbreak/in-depth/shady-onlyfans-agencies-put-filipino-workers-risk/), HIGH); "roughly double the wages of a call center agent" ([Rest of World 2025](https://restofworld.org/2025/onlyfans-ai-dm-bots/), HIGH).
- **Impersonation / deception exposure:** FTC Act §5 bars "unfair or deceptive acts or practices" ([15 U.S.C. §45](https://www.law.cornell.edu/uscode/text/15/45)); the FTC Impersonation Rule (16 CFR 461, eff. 1 Apr 2024) covers government and business impersonation, and an amendment to add *individuals* was proposed with a hearing on 17 Jan 2025 but not found finalized ([FTC](https://www.ftc.gov/legal-library/browse/rules/impersonation-government-businesses-rule); [FTC hearing notice](https://www.ftc.gov/news-events/news/press-releases/2024/12/ftc-hold-informal-hearing-proposed-rule-amendment-banning-impersonation-individuals), HIGH). Florida's UDAP is FDUTPA, [§501.204](https://www.flsenate.gov/Laws/Statutes/2025/501.204). OnlyFans' terms make the creator "legally responsible" and ban AI chatbots in DMs (§2). *N.Z. v. Fenix* shows OnlyFans can shelter behind its disclosures; **agencies and creators cannot** — the claims against agency defendants were dismissed for vagueness with leave to amend, not on the merits ([Yahoo/Court summary, Dec 2025](https://www.yahoo.com/news/articles/court-throws-explosive-onlyfans-lawsuit-181531018.html), MEDIUM).

**(d) Contracts — what creators are warned about.** From press and creator-side lawyers (agency blogs listed for completeness, marked):
- **Content ownership grabs** — agency claims ownership/co-ownership of content made during the term; fair form is a limited license that ends with the contract ([Aruna](https://arunatalent.com/blog/onlyfans-agency-red-flags/), agency-sourced; [Venustas Law](https://venustaslaw.com/blogs/blog/onlyfans-creators-beware-five-contract-traps-that-could-ruin-your-career), attorney).
- **Exclusivity** across platforms (Fansly, Fanvue, Instagram) — "platform concentration is the single biggest risk to creator income" ([Honeytrap](https://thehoneytrap.co/blog/15-red-flags-onlyfans-agency-contract), agency-sourced).
- **Termination**: >12-month lock-ins, sunset clauses, non-competes, 30-day income forfeiture, agencies continuing to post after exit ([RS 2022](https://www.rollingstone.com/culture/culture-features/onlyfans-model-management-industry-exploitation-1073615/), HIGH).
- **Revenue mechanics**: commission >50% is a red flag; agency holding earnings or the creator's bank access is the coercive-control pattern ([Prism 2024](https://prismreports.org/2024/01/08/onlyfans-management-schemes-youtube-manosphere/); [Honeytrap](https://thehoneytrap.co/blog/15-red-flags-onlyfans-agency-contract)). Passwords: never — use native permissions ([SirenCY](https://www.sirency.com/blog/onlyfans-agency-scam-red-flags), agency-sourced).
- **Upfront "setup fees"** ($500–$5K) and earnings guarantees ([SirenCY](https://www.sirency.com/blog/onlyfans-agency-scam-red-flags), agency-sourced) — note Florida §468.410 bars registration fees for *licensed* agencies.
- **Creator-advocacy sources:** APAG's licensed-agent list and "managers may not book" rule ([APAG](https://apagunion.com/performer-concerns/licensed-adult-talent-agents/)); SexWork CEO (MelRose Michaels) teaches creators to run their own operation rather than outsource ([sexworkceo.com](https://www.sexworkceo.com/post/how-to-run-your-onlyfans-like-a-ceo)); Free Speech Coalition is the trade body litigating age-verification ([FSC v. Paxton](https://www.supremecourt.gov/opinions/24pdf/23-1122_3e04.pdf)). No FSC- or SWCEO-authored agency-contract checklist was located (§6).

**(e) Payment rails.**
- **Mastercard AN 5196**, effective 15 Oct 2021: acquirers must certify adult merchants verify age and identity of everyone depicted, hold written consent, review content before publication, and run complaint/takedown processes ([Mastercard newsroom, 2021](https://www.mastercard.com/news/perspectives/2021/protecting-our-network-protecting-you-preventing-illegal-adult-content-on-our-network); [Austreme summary](https://www.austreme.com/en/mastercard-new-rules-adult-content/), HIGH). OnlyFans announced then reversed its explicit-content ban in Aug 2021 citing "banking partners and payout providers" ([CNN, 20 Aug 2021](https://edition.cnn.com/2021/08/20/tech/onlyfans-explicit-content-ban-payment/index.html); [Fortune, 25 Aug 2021](https://fortune.com/2021/08/25/onlyfans-reverses-porn-ban-sex-workers-sexually-explicit-content), HIGH).
- **Visa Integrity Risk Program**, 1 May 2023: adult MCCs must register (reported $500–$950 annual fee), tiered risk, age verification and content moderation, $25K per-merchant non-compliance assessment ([Segpay](https://segpay.com/blog/visa-integrity-risk-program-high-risk-merchants/); [Austreme](https://www.austreme.com/en/visa-is-introducing-the-visa-integrity-risk-program-virp-on-1-may-2023/), MEDIUM).

Aylo/Pornhub blocked Florida ([Fox 35](https://www.fox35orlando.com/news/pornhub-threatens-pull-out-florida-over-hb-3-law-online-age-verification-privacy-fight)).

## 5 · Creator-first vs. operator-first: what the record says

| Dimension | Creator-first (studio/persona first, roster from network) | Operator-first (course → cold outreach) |
|---|---|---|
| **Credibility** | Built in: founders cite their own numbers (Adams: $2M before 5K followers; Nelson: 1.2M followers; Reid: AVN Performer of the Year). Louna: trust came from "full transparency, hands-on involvement, and real results" | Structurally weak: creators receive "three or four" identical pitches a day and treat them as "dodgy" and "scammy" ([Prism 2024](https://prismreports.org/2024/01/08/onlyfans-management-schemes-youtube-manosphere/)); the lane's own blogs now lead with "how to tell we're not a scam" |
| **Recruitment** | Referral: Rothfield's "would he like to run mine" origin; Reid's friends; Nelson's referral link; Bop House drew 12,000 applicants for one slot after it was famous | Cold DMs at 100/day via Upwork contractors, dating apps, IG ([Prism 2024](https://prismreports.org/2024/01/08/onlyfans-management-schemes-youtube-manosphere/)); RS cases were all cold-approached |
| **Capital** | Near zero to start; the cost is *time* to produce results on your own account first. Adams reinvested ad spend at a claimed 8:1 | Near zero to start; cash goes to paid traffic and chatter payroll; courses $20–$1,000 |
| **Time to first client** | Slow: months to years of own results before a peer asks | Fast: a signing within weeks is the sales pitch — but churn is the documented pattern (RS: models leaving within months) |
| **Failure modes** | Governance and drift (Bop House: "controlling"); founder becomes the bottleneck (Angel's call-time problem); opacity about whether the agency still exists (A.S.H.) | Lawsuits (Unruly, NJAC), lock-outs, impersonation liability, labor exposure, payment-rail freezes, platform action |

**Direct answer.** Yes — "become a creator/studio first, then grow a roster from the network" is the *documented* path, and it is the path the lane itself grew out of. What the people who did it said was required before anyone signed:
- **Proof on your own account** — every verified founder had a visible result first (Adams, Nelson, Reid, Angel).
- **A trusted-person relationship, not a pitch** — Nelson: "Find an individual you see as trustworthy"; Rothfield describes peers asking, not agencies asking.
- **Transparency on money** — Ari's break with NJAC came when the manager "refused to show her any invoices or documentation"; Louna's stated trust mechanism is transparency ([RS 2022](https://www.rollingstone.com/culture/culture-features/onlyfans-model-management-industry-exploitation-1073615/); [SuperCreator](https://www.supercreator.app/guides/inside-lounas-models)).
- **A reason to exist beyond commission** — Reid's stated reason was a *lower* cut and a "safe place"; Bop House's was cross-promotion; Angel's was a genre nobody else served.
- **For the reader specifically:** the disclosed 3D persona is the "proof on your own account" step; its economics are the pitch deck. Nobody in the record signed with an empty roster and a course certificate.
