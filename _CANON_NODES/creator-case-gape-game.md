## 10 · TTP

**The one mechanic — the keyword-named second channel.** The thing worth studying is that Gape Game is not a brand; it is a *search term with a verified badge on it*. The operator already had a persona channel (KOZZYPRODUCTION, 3.5K subscribers by Oct 2021) and, instead of pushing more uploads into it, opened a second verified page whose name is the category buyers type, filled it with ~30 clips in three months, and pointed it at the same OnlyFans, X, and PayPal. Four years later the keyword page has 27.4K subscribers and 6.25M views, the persona page 39K and 14.2M, and the two together hold a rank position in the gay segment that neither would hold alone — the keyword page catches category search, the persona page catches the name, and the paid destination is single. Everything else in the record is the absence of everything else: no press, no socials worth counting, no storefront, a 16-word bio unchanged for thirty months. The mechanic is *duplicate the free surface, not the paid one.*

**Transferable tips.**
- Name your second channel for the search term buyers use, not for yourself; keep the persona channel separate; point both at one paid link. Pornhub, XVideos, and their peers each allow multiple verified pages per operator — use them.
- Launch a channel with a burst, not a trickle: ~30 uploads in the first three months is what the launch-period capture shows, and it is the only stretch that matches the 2026 view-accrual rate.
- Write the bio once as an ad — physical line, one-line offer, one call to subscribe — and leave it alone; put the commercial menu (customs, live chat, donations) in the platform's own link hub, which costs nothing and redirects with platform trust.
- Adopt every monetization surface the tube ships (Modelhub tip → FanHub) the month it ships; each one is a free link row on the page that is already ranking.
- Expect the keyword channel's subscriber curve to flatten around year three; plan the persona channel — or the next keyword channel — before it does, because that is where this operator's 2025–26 growth actually happened.

**Does not transfer.** The whole funnel rests on a verified-model badge on a tube that ranks by orientation segment and sorts by category — verification requires a real person's ID and face on camera, and the category is a body-specific act. A disclosed rendered persona cannot hold that badge, cannot upload to those category pages under the model program, and has no equivalent keyword surface with 6M free views a year attached.
