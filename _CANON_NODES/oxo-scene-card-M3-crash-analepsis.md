Inside Tori's Movement 3 search of the transgressive underground for the deleted record of Jebb's death (fabula `m3_underground_search`), the scene tells fabula event `m1b_crash_jebb_death` — the crash, M1B — out of order. Canon licenses exactly this move by name: the telling profile's T3 ruling permits "the deleted crash footage" and "the Sync-dive" as two of its examples of the one licensed analepsis class, "recovered data" ([[oxo-telling-profile]] T3), and T5 rules the crash itself the series' "repetitive motif... converging on the truth in M3" ([[oxo-telling-profile]] T5). ## THE ANACHRONY TYPE — checked against THE TOLD ORDER, not copied

The plot doc's OPEN call 14 candidate already named the type **internal analepsis, homodiegetic** (same line, Tori in both M1 and M3). Checked here against [📐 ssot_04_plot_system.md](../ShroomsQ/_CANON/_SSOT/04_PLOT_SYSTEMS/📐%20ssot_04_plot_system.md)'s own THE TOLD ORDER definitions, measured against the first narrative's own time span, as ordered:

> "external (entirely before the first narrative's own start, no interference with what is already told), internal (falls inside the first narrative's own span, risks redundancy or collision with it), mixed (straddles the start)."

- **Not external.** External requires the anachrony to fall *entirely before* the first narrative's own start. OXO's first narrative begins at Movement 1 — M1 is one of the six ruled movements the story itself narrates, not backstory before it (victoria-midnight.md, "Where she appears": "M1 | Delta Coast — Southern Gothic Racing. M1A salvage economy → M1B racing. Jebb dies."), and Act 1 is ruled M1+M2 (ssot_04_plot_system.md THE INSTANCE, P6 row-9 note). The crash sits inside M1B, inside Act 1 — inside the first narrative's own span, not before it. The fabula record's own `time.reach: "pre-story"` label ([📐 ssot_04_fabula.md](../ShroomsQ/_CANON/_SSOT/04_PLOT_SYSTEMS/📐%20ssot_04_fabula.md), `m1b_crash_jebb_death`) measures distance from an unspecified story-present — a different question from Genette's test against the first narrative's own start — and does not override the span check.
- **Not mixed.** Mixed requires the anachrony's own extent to straddle the narrative's starting point — part before, part after. The crash's own extent is `"point (a single crash)"` (ssot_04_fabula.md, same record) — a zero-span event cannot straddle anything.
- **Internal, confirmed.** What remains is internal: the crash falls inside the first narrative's own span (Act 1, M1B) and this M3 telling risks exactly the "redundancy or collision" the definition names — T5 rules the crash retold multiple times, "each time a different version... converging on the truth in M3" ([[oxo-telling-profile]] T5), i.e., this telling collides with and supersedes earlier ones by design.
- **Sub-type:** homodiegetic, and further **completing** rather than paralipsis — it fills an ellipsis left by the earlier, partial or falsified tellings (the Reset's funeral, the Feed's cut, per T5), not a lateral omission of a separate strand.

**Verdict: internal analepsis stands as carded by call 14. Not corrected** — the candidate line's own reasoning ("falls inside the story's own span, Act 1 = M1+M2") is the same test THE TOLD ORDER names, applied correctly.
