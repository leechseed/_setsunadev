# CREATOR CASE — BILBUANA

**Bottom line.** A Finland-born trans amateur creator (self-set profile: Trans Woman, Birth Place Finland, joined Pornhub ~Oct 2019, age 20 at the first snapshot) who ran **one free-tube channel for about sixteen months** — a launch batch of ~16 items in October 2019, ~11 more through February 2021, nothing since — with OnlyFans (and briefly FanCentro and Modelhub paid clips) as the paid layer, named in a one-sentence bio. No X, no Fansly, no press, no interview, no earnings, and no live presence beyond the Pornhub page and a bio link to an OnlyFans that Wayback never captured. **Thin record — Kirenes precedent: this file is short and says so.**

## 10 · TTP

**The one mechanic.** A front-loaded free-tube catalog run as an unattended asset: ~16 items in the launch fortnight, ~11 more over the next sixteen months, then nothing — and the channel still nearly doubled its subscribers (5.5K → 10.4K) and tripled its views (452K → 1.35M) in the years after the last upload, with a one-sentence bio pointing the whole audience at a paid site. The same record shows the asset's shape: view share followed length (three clips ≥ 9 min ≈ 63% of views; five clips < 1 min ≈ 1%), the two paid items were the two longest things she made, and one ten-minute clip carried ~49% of everything — so when it was removed in 2024–25 the channel lost 46% of its lifetime view count in a single event, with no upload to replace it. Against Papi's note: the record can show "quality → value" only as *length and a single hit*; it says nothing about angle or setup, because she never wrote a word about how anything was made.

**Transferable tips.**
- Launch with a batch, not a trickle: sixteen items on day one bought 500 subscribers in ten weeks on a channel with no social account behind it.
- Make the long pieces and skip the sub-minute ones: in this catalog the five shortest clips earned about 1% of views combined and the three longest about 63%. Plan a few 8–12-minute pieces plus one free trailer per paid item.
- Put the paid-site URL in the bio *text*, not only in the platform's link field — the field vanished with a template change in 2022 and the text is still there in 2026.
- Prune and retitle within the first six months: cut the weak launch items, write titles keyword-first in the third person, and then leave them alone.
- Never let one file hold half the asset. Mirror the hit on a second surface the day it pulls ahead — this channel had no second surface and lost 46% of its views to one removal.

**Does not transfer.** The niche and the surface are both specific. Pornhub's verified-model program with its paid-clip, tip, fan-club and custom buttons and its ad share was the whole monetization stack here, and the platform dismantled that stack itself between 2022 and 2023 — a zero-budget operator building today cannot copy a 2019 Modelhub funnel. Tube verification is a real-person, ID-and-face process; a disclosed rendered persona sits in a different compliance lane and cannot use "Verified Model" as the trust signal this channel leaned on. The only paid layer (OnlyFans) is invisible in the record, so the funnel's conversion is unknowable, and the dormant-compounding effect depended on a tube's recommendation engine feeding a niche search term — a curated or masked lane has no equivalent free discovery surface. Nothing here on production transfers because nothing here on production exists.

**Feeds.** **The studio launch (BOLO 24)** — primary: the batch-launch and length-over-count findings are the two cheapest sourced rules in the register so far, and the bio-text-over-link-field rule costs nothing. **The curation funnel (BOLO 27 / ULTRA DARK)** — a benchmark for what a 19-clip niche catalog does unattended on a free tube (521 → 10.4K subs; 1.35M peak views) and how concentrated the views are (one clip ≈ half). **The masked-performer Lab venture** — weak: the record has no statement on face or anonymity; only the single-point-of-failure lesson carries. **The manager/agency path** — nothing; a solo channel with no roster and no terms.
