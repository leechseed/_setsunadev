# TRASH LAND — the standards body

**The one-line read (⧗):** Trash Land's standard is the border itself, and it costs every person it excludes the one thing a border promises to whoever is inside it — proof of belonging.

## Canon — what is already spoken

- A **biome / area of the map** of the ASTRO7EX world.
- Called **"Trash Land"** — Papi's word, **placeholder only**.
- **A giant border, a DMZ, between the southern part of the US and the northern part.**
- **The DMZ is a massive border of trash.**
- **Man-made.** A built border between the South and the North.
- **The size and width of a mountain range.**
- From the BOLO 30 row: **"astrosex" = ASTRO7EX** (dictation artifact) and **"DLC" = DMZ** (dictation artifact) — logged as transcription noise, not naming.

Everything below this line is ⧗ development against those lines plus the seed's S1–S12 slice.

## ⧗ The body: who set the standard

A DMZ needs an author even when nobody wants to claim it. Two readings fit what is spoken.

**Reading A — a treaty body.** North and South each sent a delegation once, long enough to draw checkpoints at the cuts and agree on nothing else. Two liaison offices still nominally exist, one per side, meeting only at the toe of the slope. Gives S4 LAW an enforcement mechanism and Movement 5/6 something to overthrow.

**Reading B — nobody, and the trash is the standard.** No body ever convened. The pile is the law: it got tall enough that crossing became physically impossible except at the cuts, and both governments ratified the fact after it was already true. Enforcement is not an office, it is the terrain plus whichever syndicate controls a cut that season.

**Recommend Reading B.** The seed's S7 ruling already leans this way — "economics before it was a border, dumping first, wall second… nobody built it on purpose, everybody built it" — and the recommended founding story (b, the Bishops sealed the Spiral, with (a) the truth under it) reads cleaner if the "body" is a fiction the Administration lets stand. A body with no meetings is a sharper DMZ than one with meetings nobody attends.

## ⧗ The dynamic: individual versus society

| Role | The standard they meet | Freedom it takes | How they bend or break it | What the break costs |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **A crosser** | The Cut Protocol | the freedom to move north on their own timetable, for their own reason | buys, forges, or steals a logged crossing slot; goes off-cut at night | caught off-cut, they are treated as unlogged cargo, not a person — the DMZ's founding fiction (nobody's jurisdiction) becomes literal for them |
| **A resident of the range (a picker)** | The Face Rights | the freedom to dig anywhere the good strata is, not just the claim they hold | poaches an unclaimed or contested face, especially after a slide | loses the claim they did hold, or answers to whichever syndicate runs that face — enforcement with no court behind it |
| **Someone from the school side (a Spiral resident, DCUS-adjacent)** | The Look-Away | the freedom to know what happened to someone the Feed deleted | looks north anyway, or goes looking for a specific buried thing (a name, a person, a recall) | social cost first (the campus scream-etiquette penalty at world scale), then literal cost if it leads them to a cut |

## ⧗ The S12 invariant

The seed's candidate is *"Nothing that enters the range comes back as what it was."* It survives, but as a **place invariant about matter and status**, not yet a standards-body invariant about the border itself — the seed's line describes what the range does to things, the body needs a line about what the border does to people trying to cross it. Recommend a sibling, scoped to the standards body specifically:

**Candidate, place-scoped, unruled:** *"No one crosses a cut without being logged; whoever goes off-cut stops being a person the Seal recognizes."*

Falsifiable in-scene per DOCTRINE 0 III: a scene where an off-cut crosser is treated as cargo, denied any appeal, or simply not counted by either side's records, confirms it; a scene where an off-cut crosser is still tracked, named, or granted standing by either polity breaks it. Both the seed's invariant and this one can stand together: the seed governs the *matter* that enters the range, this one governs the *people* the body governs.

## Canon clicks

- **The body without a body is the sharpest DMZ.** A standards organization that never convened, whose law is a pile that got tall enough, matches the seed's own founding logic (economics first, wall second) better than inventing a treaty desk canon never mentioned.
- **The Cut Protocol gives the Administration's forbiddance a physical floor.** The seed already calls the range "the Feed's physical forbiddance, the one border a deleted person cannot log through" (S4) — the standards ladder just names that mechanism instead of leaving it a metaphor.
- **The syndicates already had jurisdiction; this just tells you what they're enforcing.** S6's haul economy (Fowlers, Montgomerys) becomes legible as the body's actual enforcement layer once Face Rights and the Haul Manifest exist as named standards.
